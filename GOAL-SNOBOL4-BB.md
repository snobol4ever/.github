## ⛔⭐⭐ THE MODEL — canonical laws (Whack Contract s23k · The Unwind 08-03b · Design of Record s21x-c · Statement-Port Laws 08-06c)

**THERE IS NO GRAPH FRAME.** Every BB allocates at α (own K), reads/writes only its own cell, releases at ω, jumps. The whole-graph carve is a corpse; readers still addressing into a carve are the reds.

**WHACK CONTRACT (Lon s23k):** (1) Whack-free has exactly TWO mechanisms — DETERMINABLE: one `add rsp,K_total` at final success; INDETERMINABLE: empty RBP frame at the indeterminacy boundary, whack = `mov rsp,rbp; pop rbp`. (2) Whack sites are exactly: end of STATEMENT · end of MATCH · FENCE commit points. (3) THE GLUE DOES NOT WHACK. (4) STATEMENT_END is the sole statement whack authority (`op_zgpop`'s home) — whacks fused into last-operator BBs are the over-free disease. (5) OPERATOR RULE: an operator frees ONLY its own result, ONLY at ω — never operands. (6) A BB's own α claim is ~16–64B; a statement head carrying the whole claim is UCLAIM debt.

**THE UNWIND (Lon 08-03b, four clauses):** 1. ROLL UP forward — α carves own K; spine accumulates non-popping. 2. ROLL DOWN backward — FAILURE NEVER WHACKS: box N's ω frees OWN K (restoring own cursor/CAS state first) and jumps to pred's β; a one-shot box's β IS its ω-continuation (`add rsp,K; jmp pred_β`); RSP at β == RSP at γ by induction; the depth-knowledge problem is DELETED, not solved. 3. WHACK at a fenced sync point, FORWARD only (FENCE commit; MATCH_END is SN4's language fence — a succeeded match is never re-entered). 4. WHACK at final success — STATEMENT_END γ, the sole statement release; STATEMENT_BEGIN.β the sole fail terminus (arrives at entry depth BY CONSTRUCTION, jumps to :F or next statement). SURVIVES: the ΣK ZPOP fold (legal fusion iff every folded β is whitelisted-trivial); law-4 RBP frames. **STATEMENT-PORT LAWS (Lon 08-06c):** statement FAILURE lands at STATEMENT_BEGIN.β (release may whack-free BY ADD — unwind + abandon-path re-base guarantee rsp==claim base on arrival); SUCCESS lands at STATEMENT_END.α; BEGIN frames ONLY unknown-extent statements ("DO NOT set up a frame when you do not need it") — known extent ⇒ `add rsp,ΣK`, unknown ⇒ END pops (`op_stmt_dyn`/dyn_whack).
