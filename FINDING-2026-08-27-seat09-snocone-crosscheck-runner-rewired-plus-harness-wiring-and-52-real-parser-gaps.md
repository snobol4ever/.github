Rewired to `gcc -c` (assemble) + `gcc ... -lscrip_rt -Wl,-rpath` (link against `out/libscrip_rt.so`), mirroring `test_corpus_snobol4.sh`'s `compile_mode4()`. Deleted the now-pointless runtime-archive-build cache (nothing to build anymore — `libscrip_rt.so` is prebuilt); kept the per-test binary cache, rekeyed off `scrip`+`libscrip_rt.so` mtimes instead of the old archive-source stamp.
