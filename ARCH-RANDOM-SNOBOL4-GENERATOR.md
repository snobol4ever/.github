## 3. THE RULINGS

**R1 THE SCOPE LADDER.** The generator takes one word, scope=N (0 <= N <= inf_scope_max()), and emits the construct families of steps 1..N and no later one. scope=0 is today's text, so every batch hq_dotnet has run under seed0 20261022 keeps its lines; a batch is identified by (scope, seed0, N) for ever. The generator's default is 0 and the driver's --scope default is 0 (it passes no word at 0, so it runs on a generator from before step 1); the campaigns name --scope explicitly, at the highest landed level, which --list prints as the ceiling (a grep of inf_scope_max, never a run). A generator that refuses scope=N ("bad option") is the MEASURED state of a step that has not landed: the harness reads it RED, never REFUSED. The SCRIPtix demo's own run names scope=<ceiling> in infinite_snobol4.cmdline, bumped at each landing, because the demo's job is to find differences and its ideal ref (no DIFF line) does not depend on the scope.

**R2 ASSIGNMENT ONLY INTO A CAPITAL.** Lon's 10-03 rule, *"do not allow assignment, =, operator in expressions since they would modify subsequent tests"*, is kept by its purpose: a test may assign ONLY to a capital A..Z, which both sides null before every test, never to a..z or to a keyword, so no test changes a later one. That admits the replacement statement (W = subject) W ? pattern = replacement, the multiple assignment W = V = subject and SPITBOL's embedded match-and-replace as a value (step 5), and the result line already prints every capital.

**R3 THE EVALUATOR IS ONE PROGRAM IN TWO SPELLINGS.** inf_eval.sno (the road sbl, snobol4dotnet and snobol4jvm run) and the Snocone section's inf_render/inf_judge/check (the SCRIPtix road) are the same evaluator; a step that changes rendering (9) or the road (11) changes both in one landing and re-cuts inf_eval.ref and infinite_snobol4.ref from sbl -bf in that landing. Where Snocone cannot spell what the road needs (a direct goto for the CODE road), the SCRIPtix side keeps its EVAL road and refuses the kinds it cannot take, and the landing says so; the differential driver is the road both ports are graded on.

**R4 THE WITNESS IS AT MOST 20 LINES, REDUCED, AND STILL DIVERGES.** A divergence is reduced by the reducer of section 5 to a standalone program of at most 20 lines that reproduces the divergence on the named engine against sbl -bf before it is written; its ref is cut by util_add_ladder_witness.py from sbl -bf, twice; it never carries an answer SCRIP, dotnet or jvm produced.

**R5 ONE DIVERGENCE CLASS, ONE ROW, THREE ENGINES.** Every reduced witness enters the shared master as a rung (section 6), where SCRIP, snobol4dotnet and snobol4jvm each grade it on their SnoRungs boards; the engine that is red owns the row (a SCRIP red is hq_snobol4's, CEO-1999), minted by the ceo with the rung as its DONE-WHEN, as CEO-1915 already does. The design row's eighteen steps are the instruments' growth, never the cures.

**R6 THE EXCLUSION LIST GROWS ONLY BY MEASUREMENT.** A shape goes into inf_exclude.tsv and inf_exclusions() only with the measurement that it hangs or cannot agree in the language itself (SUCCEED, powers to &STLIMIT, the seven program-identity keywords); a shape one engine mishandles is a witness, never an exclusion.

**R7 THE SEVEN PROGRAM-IDENTITY KEYWORDS STAY EXCLUDED** (&LINE &LASTLINE &FILE &LASTFILE &STNO &LASTNO &STCOUNT) while the one source serves both roads: under the SCRIPtix road the judging program and the SPITBOL child are two programs. Under the offline driver they are one program under two engines and could agree; lifting them for that road alone is a later word (road=offline), not a step here.

**R8 AN ABSENT ENGINE IS A REFUSAL, NEVER A GREEN.** The step harness grades the coverage arm in any root and the engine arms only where the port runner's --binaries answers (hq_dotnet's and hq_jvm's roots); a REFUSED arm is printed and not counted; a step is GREEN only on arms that were graded.

**R9 A STEP IS GREEN WHEN ITS FAMILY IS EMITTED AND BOTH ENGINES AGREE ON ITS BATCHES.** The coverage arm: every COVERAGE.tsv row of step N reads at least `need` hits (20, over two seeds of the six-configuration spread at 300 tests) at --scope N. The engine arm per engine: the step's batches (seed0 rcsNN, 1-10, 300 tests, the step's pins) read differ=0, which means every divergence they find has been reduced, harvested as a rung and cured on that engine, or measured into the exclusion list under R6. Red before the landing: the generator refuses the scope or emits the family 0 times.

**R10 THE SEEDS ARE THE STEP'S.** Step N's batches draw from seed0 rcsNN, so every seat and every day sees the same batches for that step and the campaign's seed0 (20261022 and its successors) stays the campaign's.

## 5. THE REDUCTION OF A DIVERGENCE TO A WITNESS (step 2, util_inf_snobol4_reduce.py)

Input: a batch's .diff (line L: TEST, ORACLE, ENGINE), the batch's text (its preset lines), the engine E. Output: W.sno of at most 20 lines, or a refusal.
1. **THE CLOSURE.** Collect the names the test mentions: a..z, A..Z, f g h, u, the literal indices of a[...]. Take the preset lines those names need: a cell's own line; for a[...] with a literal index that row and the ARRAY line; for f(n)/g(n)/h(n) or an index that is a cell, the ARRAY line and the rows a[0]..a[9] (b..k reach at most 9) unless u[...] is mentioned, then the 36; for u the TABLE line and the rows of the strings the test can reach; v..z the pattern's line and the capitals it names; f g h their DEFINE blocks (eight lines for h, three each for f and g, inf_names when h is needed).
2. **THE SHAPE.** The direct form first: `&ERRLIMIT = 1000`, the closure, `SETEXIT('ERR')`, `R = <test> :F(NO)`, `OUTPUT = DATATYPE(R) ' ' R :(CAPS)`, `NO OUTPUT = 'FAIL' :(CAPS)`, `ERR OUTPUT = 'ERROR ' &ERRTYPE`, `CAPS OUTPUT = 'X=' X ' Y=' Y ' Z=' Z` (and every capital the test names), `END`. If the direct form does not reproduce the divergence on E against sbl -bf, the EVAL form (`R = EVAL('<test>')`, the quotes doubled), because some classes live only in EVAL's road.
3. **DDMIN OVER THE CLOSURE.** While the program exceeds 20 lines, or in any case once, drop preset lines in halves then singly, keeping a drop only if the divergence persists (both sbl and E re-run on the candidate; a line whose removal turns the divergence into a different divergence is kept). The test line is never changed by the reducer; a reducer that must shorten the expression itself hands that to the HQ with the ddmin'd program.
4. **THE PROOF.** The final program is run once more on E and on sbl -bf; it is written only if they still differ, with a header comment naming the batch (scope, seed0, N, line L), the engine and the class the HQ names; HARVEST.tsv gets its row at harvest time. --selftest reduces a planted three-preset divergence against a fake engine (a sed over sbl's output) to prove the closure, the two forms and the ddmin without any port engine.

## 10. RULINGS ON STEP 11, THE CODE ROAD, AS hq_dotnet MEASURED IT (the cto, 2026-10-29 11:0x CDT; hq_dotnet's telegrams of 10-28 and the baton QA Q1-Q5 of random-codegen-step-11-the-code-road-ceo-1999; measured on sbl -bf and snobol4dotnet)

- **Q1 THE SPELLINGS: THE ROWS STAND AS MEASURED.** Column one of a CODE string is the label field at the start and after every semicolon (manual p.178), so an unlabeled statement begins with a blank and a labeled one with its label directly after the semicolon. hq_dotnet's second measurement (10-29, the census over its draft road at harness c28656b): every statement row of COVERAGE.tsv reads 20 or more with its regex as it stands (assignment 70, match 122, label 301, CODE 70, DEFINE 111, the gotos 52 to 600), so no row changes; a step-11 row that reads under 20 on the landed road is re-spelled in that landing to the measured column-one form (`(^ |; )[A-Z] = `, `(^|;)inf_L\d`, `(^ |^inf_L\d |; |;inf_L\d )[^;]* \? `), never the generator bent to the row.
- **Q2 END: `;END$`, THE LAST STATEMENT OF A TEST AFTER ONE THAT ALWAYS LEAVES.** An executed END ends the evaluator (sbl prints nothing after it); an END is reachable as a test only where it never executes, after `:S(inf_ok)F(inf_no)`, and there it also ends the string's compilation (p.179; every label after it undefined). The row reads `;END$` and the generator emits it only in that position.
