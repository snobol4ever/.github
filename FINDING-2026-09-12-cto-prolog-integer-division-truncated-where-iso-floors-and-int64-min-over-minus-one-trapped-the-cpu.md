# FINDING 2026-09-12 (cto, CEO-617 lane; ceo re CTO-33) — `div` truncated where ISO floors, and INT64_MIN over -1 trapped the CPU

**Claim.** After SCRIP `b93d066e8`, ISO 9.1.3 integer arithmetic holds at the 64-bit boundary: `div/2` floors (`-10 div 3` is -4; `//` still truncates to -3), `mod` takes the divisor's sign and `rem` the dividend's, `//`, `div`, `mod`, `rem` by zero raise `evaluation_error(zero_divisor)` named for the operator, and `INT64_MIN // -1`, `INT64_MIN div -1`, `abs(INT64_MIN)`, `-(INT64_MIN)` and any `+`, `-`, `*` that leaves 64 bits raise `evaluation_error(int_overflow)` instead of wrapping or killing the process. Row `prolog-integer-division-floors-and-int64-overflow-raises-instead-of-trapping`; DONE-WHEN `scripts/test_gate_pl_integer_division_floors_and_int64_overflow_raises.sh`, red 4 of 4 at mint, green 4 of 4 landed, wired into `make test`.

**How it was found.** The whole-package SWI board (CTO-33) put `core/test_arith.pl` at 0 of 220: the file's own div/mod consistency loop printed `Failed for X=-10,Y=3` and dozens more, then the process died with rc=136. SIGFPE is the x86 `idiv` trap on INT64_MIN / -1, the one integer division that cannot be represented.

**Where it was.** `src/lower/pl_arith_names.h` mapped both `//` and `div` to the `idiv` leaf, so `div` was `//`. `dop_ax` in `by_name_dispatch.c` fell through to `pl_arith2` for idiv and mod, which executed `a / b` and `a % b` unguarded and returned FAILDESCR — a silent failure carrying no ball — for `rem` and `//` by zero. `neg` and `abs` of INT64_MIN wrapped to INT64_MIN; `+`, `-`, `*` wrapped.

**The cure.** `div` gets its own leaf `$ax_divf` (direct table in `bb_call.cpp`, `PL_AX_VENEER(divf, DIVF)` in `rtx_plunify.s`, `MOD_OP_RT_PL_AX_DIVF` 225, `dop_ax_divf`), and `pl_ax_divides` admits it so the compile-time zero guard still precedes it. `dop_ax` handles idiv/divf/mod/rem for integers before the generic tail: zero divisor raises; `b == -1` is short-circuited (mod gives 0, `//`/`div` of INT64_MIN raise, otherwise `-a`) so `idiv` never sees the trapping pair; the floor adjustment applies to `div` and `mod`; `+`, `-`, `*` go through `__builtin_*_overflow`. All Prolog-own: `rt_num_arith`, which SNOBOL4 and Icon share, is untouched.

**Measured.** Witness `[-3,2,-1,-4]` then `int_overflow`, `0`, `int_overflow`, `int_overflow` in both modes. `core/test_arith.pl` 29 → 55 of 220 and exits by rc.
