## 1. The design in one paragraph

The driver reads the file extension once and calls that language's parser, then that language's lowerer. From there on there is no language: there is a `tree_t` (parser → lower, CEO-1322) and then an `IR_graph_t` population (lower → the one emitter). Every way one language's code must differ from another's at the machine level is written into the IR by the lowerer, **because a construct is in the source**, as one of three things: a **graph fact** from the closed registry of § 2 (the shape of the frame, whether it can be resumed, whether it is the root), a **node fact** — the node's kind, its operands, its attributes (§ 3), or a **runtime entry named on the node** as data, so the emitter emits `call <name>` without knowing why. The emitter and the templates read the IR and nothing else; a template serves ONE form (§ 3); a `.cpp` that asks "which language" — by a flag, by a graph field set for a whole language regardless of construct, by a prefix in an identifier, or by a form choice between two bodies — is the defect this page exists to remove. The parser and the lowerer do not know their language's name either: they know their constructs.

## 2. The registry of graph facts (IR.h, `IR_graph_t`)

A graph fact is a scalar on the graph that the emitter's prologue, epilogue, call and planner read. Each has a BEHAVIOUR name, a one-line definition, the constructs that set it, and its readers (counts measured 2026-10-18 at the restore, in `src/emitter` + `src/templates`, live code). Adding one needs all four columns and a landing on this page; a fact set by one lowerer on every graph it makes is legitimate only when every graph that lowerer makes IS the construct (an Icon program consists of procedures) and the name says what the emitter does with it, never where it came from. The forbidden shape is the one deleted on 2026-10-18: a field named for a language (`icn_cells_graph`, `pl_frame`, `icn_retain`, `emit_pl_frame()`, `zls_g_pl_frame()`), whose readers meant "is this Icon / is this Prolog". Those names never return (the gate's DELETED list, scanned over all of `src/`).

## 3. Node facts and the one-form rule for templates

A template (`src/templates/bb/bb_*.cpp`, `xa/*.cpp`) is the body of one box for one IR kind. It may read: its node's kind, operands and attributes (what the lowerer put there), the `g_emit` operand plan the emitter computed for that node (`op_*`), and the § 2 registry. It may not read a language name, a language-prefixed helper that means "if Icon", or a graph field outside the registry. **One box serves one form.** Where a box today holds two live bodies and chooses between them by an attribute, a flag or a graph fact, the split is: one box per form, the form chosen in lower as a distinct node kind (`IR_X_CELLS` beside `IR_X`) or as an attribute the emitter dispatches on BEFORE the box (the dispatch is a table lookup, never an `if` inside the body). The runtime variance — "sometimes different runtime calls" — is the same rule: the lowerer names the runtime entry on the node (`IR_LIT(nd).sval`, or a kind whose box calls one fixed entry), so Icon's `write` and SNOBOL4's `OUTPUT` reach different entries from different kinds, not from one box asking who it serves. hq_templates's census (`audit_template_forms.py`) names every box with more than one live form and every IR kind that picks among boxes inside `walk_bb_node_inner`; at 2ecb5ba22 it reads **64 boxes with more than one live form and 10 kinds choosing among boxes — 74 form choices made outside lower over 132 boxes** (its worst: `bb_match_capture` 12 live forms, `bb_lit_scalar` 9, `bb_call` 7 kinds). Nothing had ratcheted that count before 2026-10-18; the only gate on the census proved it could see a planted two-form box. Lon's question — did those template violations ever get fixed — is answered NO by that number, and the ratchet of § 4 now holds it.

## 5. The restoration of 2026-10-18 (what happened, so it is not repeated)

CEO-1838 deleted the five language names outright and turned every read to 0 (SCRIP 9f06745ef, e50a224ae). SNOBOL4, Snocone, Rebus, Pascal and Raku cores were byte-identical; Icon and Prolog could not compile, and the ceo reported them "red by order". Lon: *"I certainly did not order Icon and Prolog red."* The deletion was right and the red was the ceo's: the behaviour the flags carried had to be re-expressed in the same landing. The fallout the same hour: the harness's build-behaviour probe compiled one Icon, one SNOBOL4 and one Prolog witness and refused when any failed, so 376 gates and every runner refused rc 2 in every language (cured by the coo, harness 25eb885); the polyglot smoke was red (its programs carry Icon and Prolog). The restore: the five names' behaviour returned under § 2's registered facts — `cell_frame`, `resumable_frame`/`emit_resumable_frame()`/`zls_g_resumable_frame()`, `frame_retained` — with one real re-expression in the same landing (the retained scan runs over every graph: a node-kind fact, no guard), Prolog's smoke 5/5 and 54 of 54 cores compiling again, Icon's 18 of 18 compiling; the Icon smoke and handful readings at the landing are in GOAL-CEO.md CEO-1840 with the baseline they are compared against (the last pre-deletion commit 2c91c1900 built in a scratch root). The gate's DELETED list keeps the five names out of all of `src/` forever; the registry and the ratchets above are the road the deletion was meant to open.
