# ARCH-PROLOG-SPINE-SEGMENTS -- the RBP -> RSP spine conversion for Prolog: when a cell is rsp-addressable, and the few sites that must use rbp

hq_prolog, 2026-10-18, under CEO-1825/1826/1828 (Lon to hq_prolog: *"You will be in charge of the RBP -> RSP spine conversion."*, the Prolog side; hq_icon the shared core) and Lon's question the same morning, verbatim: *"how will we determine when we MUST use RBP versus RSP FORTH-style fixed-relative and shifting offsets addressing?"*. The tree is e41bf8a6a (hq_zetas, Lon's *"Check in to main now"*): the RBP-vs-RSP placement decision is deleted, every BB result and local is on the RSP spine, rbp carries the frame header only, locals are carved FORTH-style at their first use (zst_plan).

## 1. The one test

A cell (a box's result, a local, a frame word) may be addressed through rsp at an instruction **iff the spine depth at that instruction is a compile-time constant of this graph** and the cell is still carved. The planner (zd_plan) already assigns one entry depth per box; the test is whether that assignment is TRUE on every path. Call a box **depth-definite** when it is.

Only two things make a depth indefinite:

1. **A call that can return with its own frame still live below the caller** -- a nondeterministic exit: the callee kept a choice point, so its frame stays on the machine stack and the caller continues deeper by the live region, which is dynamic in general (the callee's callees may be live too). Measured: `main :- p(X), write(X), nl, fail.` with three `p/1` facts enters `write` at depth 208 where the plan says 48 (p's frame, 160 bytes, is live); the retry then jumps through a continuation word read at the wrong depth. In Prolog every CALL_PROC_STAGED to a predicate with a frame (`bb_proc_target_retains`: `zls_g_pl_frame`, i.e. `resumable_callable`, which lower sets for every Prolog predicate), CALL_VALUE (call/N, callee unknown), the db-walk TO (clause/retract enumeration), catch/3 and the soft cut.
2. **A push whose size is not a compile-time constant** (a variable-arity argument block for call/N or apply, a VLA-style carve). After it nothing above it is rsp-addressable.

Everything else is rsp, FORTH-style: a merge point with two incoming depths is normalized by the edge pops (one entry depth per box); a resume through a stored continuation (a retry, the else arm after a failed condition, the fail-through of an if-then-else) gets its depth back by **restoring rsp from the record** (the BOUND's saved word, the choice point's word), never from rbp; a local lives at the deepest non-looping common dominator of its uses, which is a carve, not a frame slot.

Measured today and NOT an rbp need, each a planner inconsistency: the GATE's arms landing after the GATE without its 16-byte cell while the run holding the GATE counted it (every if-then-else on bc2d85fda, the spine-tier bomb); the second if-then-else's else run planned at depth 0 while its UNMARK restores rsp to the BOUND's depth 80 (cured by the absolute claim bases, 2dd79e5e9, now run for every Prolog graph); a FORTH local homed on a BOUND that carves nothing because the GATE's arm edges ran backwards in zst_plan's dominator graph (hello on e41bf8a6a); a variable named only through a unify box's first operand, homed after its first use (`X = 1, write(X)`).

## 2. Segments

A **segment** is a maximal region of a clause's boxes within which the depth is definite relative to the segment's entry. Segment 0 starts at the graph entry (depth 0 = the frame base, which is rbp). A retaining call ends its segment at its gamma: the run breaks there (zd_plan's run walk stops after the call, as the deleted `zd_pl_dyn_call` break did), and the box after it heads a run whose base is **unknown** (zd_pl_bases leaves it `known = 0`, never propagating a base through a retaining call's gamma edge). Definiteness is regained at any box that restores rsp from a definite record: an UNMARK with the `&2` (commit) or `&4` (condition failed) act whose BOUND is in a definite run gets the BOUND's depth as its base (the existing ub rule), so the clause after a committed if-then-else or a finished findall is definite again.

Per box the planner exports `zd_xb[i]` (the run's absolute base, or INT_MIN) and the templates read `op_zdef = (zd_xb[i] != INT_MIN)`.

## 3. The addressing rules, per box

- **R-A frame words** (`FRQ(off)`: BOUND and choice words, act words, variable slots): definite -> `[rsp + off + zabs]` as today; indefinite -> `[rbp + off]`. rbp is the frame base (the gamma releases with `lea rsp, [rbp + kt]`), so `off` is the same constant either way.
- **R-B sticky locals** (`off >= ZST_BIAS`, the local at absolute depth L below the base): definite -> `[rsp + zabs - L]`; indefinite -> `[rbp - L]`. L is absolute only when the carve point is in a definite run, so zst_plan homes a local FORTH-style only when its deepest non-looping common dominator is definite (and carves its own cell, `zd_k_own > 0`); otherwise the local keeps its frame slot (R-A). A use in an indefinite box of a definite local is `[rbp - L]`: no frame slot, no copy.
- **R-C spine operands** (a consumer reading a producer's cell by rsp distance): valid only when producer and consumer are in the same segment, or both definite (the absolute bases make the distance exact across runs). A producer with a consumer in another, indefinite segment is **frame-homed**: it also stores its value to its frame slot (`xop_frame_slot`, the Prolog member rule re-cut from e4301adbf's `pl_xop_frame_member`) and the consumer reads the slot (`op_zread_xf`) under R-A. A retaining call's own result already goes to its frame slot (`!zsp` in bb_call_proc_staged).
- **R-D pops.** Within a segment the pops are the plan's. An edge from an indefinite box to a target that restores rsp from a record (an UNMARK with `&2` or `&4`, a call's beta through its act word, the predicate's gamma/omega/step through rbp) needs no pop and gets 0; the chain of betas back to the retaining call pops relative to the segment's entry, which is where the call's beta expects rsp.
- **R-E exits.** The predicate's gamma, omega, step and clause-retry code read the header through rbp (`x86_hb`), never `[rsp + slot]`: they are entered from indefinite boxes. (e41bf8a6a left `main$2F0_step` reading `[rsp + 656]`.)
- **R-F families: depth by construction, never a reload at a beta** (Lon 2026-10-18 16:2x, CEO-1841: *"Never re-load RSP at a BETA label."*). The runs a retaining edge lands on form a FAMILY: the landing run is its head at relative base 0 (its first box's entry depth), and every run reached from a family member by a plain edge (a gamma, a disjunction arm, a GATE landing, a restoring UNMARK of a BOUND that lies inside the family) gets a base KNOWN RELATIVE to the head. Inside one family every cross-run pop is a constant base difference, exactly as across definite runs; across the family boundary the R-D rule stands (a record restores, or the edge pops nothing). A GATE whose arm ends in a retaining call: that arm's dispatch pair carries 0 - the target's relative exit depth when the target is in the arm's family, so the GATE's beta finds rsp where the arm's continuation popped back to, by the continuation's own constants. The absolute propagation is run a second time after the indefinite marking, skipping indefinite runs, so a run first reached through an indefinite one keeps a base only if a definite path gives it one; a run entered mid-run by an indefinite landing is itself indefinite (and joins the family). SCRIP: zd_pl_bases (absolute_pass, the fam/fbase arrays, the [ZD-MAP] FAM line), flat_drive_gate; the GATE concede pops from its funnel UNMARK's coordinate.

- **R-G a merge GATE with a resumable arm is a retaining edge** (CEO-1854/1855 on hq_prolog's ask; cto, SCRIP 6cf0062ab, 2026-10-21). An arm is resumable when it is sealed and its redo target needs that arm's cells: anything but a cut, an unmark or the gate itself. A GATE with such an arm (`zd_gate_holds`) is listed in `zd_edge_retains`. The run walk therefore ends at the GATE, and the GATE's continuation heads an indefinite family exactly as after a retaining call. The continuation's chain pops back to its own entry, and the GATE's beta is treated as entered via an act word. A resumable arm's GATE_ARM pops nothing, so the arm's TO cell or its live callee frame survives the continuation. The GATE's beta pops per arm: from the banked arm's arrival (its exit less its own pop) to the depth its redo target expects. That depth is X for a beta, E for the GATE's own alpha (a deterministic arm's concede), and the continuation's entry for a retaining call or a holding GATE. Every pair is a constant in the arm's own coordinate, so no beta regrows: k3's program read 49 regrows into a box beta and reads 0. In the family pass, a run headed by a restoring UNMARK takes its base from its BOUND, never from a plain edge priced at a commit UNMARK's pre-restore depth. This is the absolute pass's rule, and nested holding GATEs need it. The witness is cnt(3) with findall over ( fail -> true ; cnt(C), between(1, C, I), N = I ), [1,2,3] in both modes. Known gap: the ball path at a holding GATE's beta still pops from the GATE's planned depth; every target on that flight restores rsp itself.

## 4. The count Lon asked for

The planner marks each box definite or not; an **rbp-required site** is a read or write of a cell carved in an earlier segment by an indefinite box: R-A words, R-B locals, R-C homes. `SCRIP_ZD_MAP=1` prints the bases and the sticky homes per graph (the `BASE` and `STICKY` lines); a census over the corpus counts the R-C homes (`xop_frame_slot >= 0`) and the indefinite boxes per graph. That count is the "few places where a RESULT or LOCAL is required to use RBP" of Lon's order to hq_zetas, and it goes down only by making calls deterministic (first-argument indexing, last-call), never by moving cells.

## 6. The rsp loads in the emitted Prolog, classified (the ceo's asm census, CEO-1832)

`util_rsp_restore_census.sh --lang prolog` at the segments landing: 11,385 `mov rsp, [memory]` over the 54 core entries, 3,803 at an alpha label, 7,582 at a numbered label, 0 at a beta, plus 34,878 `lea rsp, [rbp ...]`. Every one of them is a DISCARD or a frame protocol word, none is a retry re-establishing rsp from a record:

- **At an alpha label: the UNMARK that commits an if-then-else or finishes a bounded goal** (`bb_bound`'s `&2` arm: `mov rsp, FRQ(off + 8)`, the BOUND's saved rsp) and **the UNMARK that enters the else arm or the fail-through after a failed condition** (the `&4` arm, the same word). Both are discards: the condition's cells and any callee frame the condition left live are dropped to the BOUND's depth, and the plan regains definiteness there (the `ub` rule). The count falls only by making conditions deterministic, never by moving a cell.
- **At a numbered label: the call protocol.** The callee's gamma with a live frame (`mov rax, rbp; mov rbp, [rbp + link]`, no rsp load), its deterministic exit (`lea rsp, [rbp + kt]`, counted under lea_rbp), the last-call arm (`lea rsp, [rbp + kt]` after copying the new block over the old), the clause step's ball arm, the choice header open (`pl_header_open` stores rsp; a store, not a load), and the retry of a callee through its act word (`mov rbp, rax; jmp rcx`: the callee's beta runs at ITS frame, the caller's rsp is the callee's to leave). The 7,582 are the `mov rsp, FRQ(...)` of the inlined runtime calls' landings and the BOUND words read through rbp.
- **The cut** (`bb_cut`): `lea rsp, [rbp - D]`, D the planned depth (ARCH § 2): a discard to a compile-time constant, counted under lea_rbp.
- **beta = 0**: no Prolog beta loads rsp. A retry enters a choice point through the act word or the clause step, both of which run on the frame header, and the box chain's betas pop constants only (the ω law). Lon, 2026-10-18 16:2x CDT, in-chat to hq_prolog, verbatim: *"Never re-load RSP at a BETA label. That would be invalid unless for diagnostics. But even that is VERY questionable."* -- said over a cure hq_prolog had built and withdrew before it reached origin (bank rsp in the GATE's frame slot at an arm's landing, reload it at bb_gate's beta): a GATE whose arm ends in a retaining call is re-entered right BY CONSTRUCTION instead -- that arm's GATE_ARM pops nothing and its dispatch pair adds nothing, so the continuation's own constant pops bring rsp back to the arm's real exit depth; the runs an indefinite run spawns carry bases known RELATIVE to their family's head and the pops inside one family are constant base differences; only a BOUND's UNMARK restores from the BOUND's record, a discard the page already allows.

So the second DONE-WHEN of the conversion (beta = 0, and an alpha count equal to the indefinite boxes) reads beta = 0 today; the alpha count is the UNMARK restores, which are discards by the page's definition, not indefinite boxes. The number that measures the conversion's remaining debt is ZD-CHECK's indefinite-box count once its retaining-edge arm reads `zd_edge_retains` (CEO-1830 § 5): the boxes after a retaining call that read a cell carved before it through rbp (R-A words, R-B locals, R-C homes).
