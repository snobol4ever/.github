**(1) ⭐⭐⭐ THE DEFAULT-ARM RETRY CHANNEL IS SEVERED — PRODUCER AND CONSUMER USE DIFFERENT CHANNELS.**

Its consumer `rt_pl_cp_pop` **is** live — `bb_indirect_goto.cpp:29`, the `g_emit.zframe_graph` arm, which is the arm every Prolog graph takes by default (`lower_prolog.c:1379` stamps zframe on all of them). Net: the retry dispatcher pops an always-empty stack, gets 0, and `je ω` — **it concedes instead of retrying.**
⛔ **SCOPE IT BEFORE CURING IT, DO NOT GUESS:** clauses are delivered two ways — `IR_MOVE_LABEL` **or** `IR_SUSPEND` when `suspend_deliver` (`lower_prolog.c:830-837`), and the SUSPEND road's push (`rt_pl_cp_push3`, zf3) **is** live. Cure = PZ-2's record (one channel, in the activation) — ⛔ **do NOT "fix" it by wiring `rt_pl_cp_push` back in**: that would spend a rung building the very C-heap stack PZ-2 deletes.
