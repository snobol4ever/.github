## §0 Vocabulary (the four words every section uses)

- **Fixed cell.** A box's own `sub rsp,K` at α and `add rsp,K` at ω, K a multiple of 16 (the FORTH-style ζ-SPINE of ARCH-ENGINE.md). The cell is the box's REAL storage, not a cache of a frame slot.
- **Grant.** A `zls_field` registration in the flat plan (the per-graph region carved once at proc entry), typed (`ZK_DESCR` / `ZK_RAW` / `ZK_PTR_GC` / `ZK_PTR_CODE`) so the collector can walk it.
- **Window / rebase.** `x86_fc_hit` (`x86_asm.h`) turns an `FR(off)` whose `off` lies in `[op_fc_base, op_fc_base+op_fc_bytes)` into `[rsp + off - base]`; every other `FR` stays a flat frame reference. The base value only ever appears as a difference.
- **Degrade, never die.** A candidate the planner refuses keeps the flat path BYTE-VERBATIM. Every registrar in this family is all-or-nothing per statement for that reason: a dropped member would strand a carve or under-pop an ω.

## §1 `fc_cells_on` — THE ONE PORT OPINION (Z4-6, GOAL-ZETA-FOUR)

**The defect it closed (s206, the ZHEAP finding of 2026-07-28): PORT-BLIND GRANT versus PORT-GATED CONSUMPTION.** `fc_geom` granted a cell on every port while `x86_fc_on`/`x86_fc_hit` fired under FORTH only, so under any other port one box got contradictory answers: the geometry said carve, the consumers said flat, `zls_fc_cell` netted out the flat quad the box then needed, and `fc_vwpop` counted ω pops for cells never pushed. Measured consequence (Z4-0/Z4-6): the three pattern probes SEGV under frame-rsp while `arith`/`fib` run.

**The cure was structural, not a per-site patch:** one predicate, consulted at the QUERY SURFACE of every family member (`fc_geom`, `fc_save_active`, `fc_vlit_active`, `fc_cond_fp`, `fc_head_fp`, `fc_vwpop`, `fc_seq_active`, `fc_leaf_disp`, `zls_fc_cell`), so LOWER's consumers, the emitter's grants, the layout's quad net-out and the template predicates all read ONE answer regardless of what the side tables registered. The port was process-global and set before any lowering, so both phases read a stable answer; none of the registration-order hazard of §3 applied to it. The allowlist was `{FORTH, HEAP}` on purpose: FORTH the live protocol, HEAP kept "granted-but-broken" unchanged so Z4-8's pre/post SEGV proof stood. `fc_cells_active()` was minted as the one sanctioned external read (the RBX census).

⛔ **LIVE TODAY: the port axis no longer exists.** `zeta_choices.h` is deleted and `ZC_PORT`/`rt_zeta_port_mode` have zero hits in `src/` (RULES.md § ZETA HAS NO MODES). `fc_cells_on()` is literally `return 1`, still consulted at ten sites in the file, and `fc_cells_active()` is still the one external read (`emit.cpp`). What survives is the discipline, not the switch: the family answers "is this box cell-resident" in exactly one place, and the same one-authority shape is §6's carve law.

## §2 `fc_geom` — the per-box fixed-cell geometry authority (ZB-FC-0/ZB-FC-1, per-kind MEASURED verdicts)

Same authority pattern as §1: queried at the emit dispatch point, promoted into `g_emit` (`op_fc_bytes` / `op_fc_base`), consumed by the α/ω port hook and the `FR`/`FRQ` in-range translation in `x86_asm.h`. Returns 1 and `*k` = the cell size (a 16-multiple BY LAW, ARCH-ZETA S10a item 6) iff the kind participates; 0 means inert, and the hook and translation stay dormant.

**The ladder of kinds, each admitted by its own measurement (Lon 2026-07-12, ZB-FC-0 then ZB-FC-1):**
- **SPAN** first: the keystone looping box, two private 4-byte fields (counter at scratch+0, β-undo delta at scratch+4), one 16-byte cell, all conditional ω routed through the `x86_jcc` synth. Widening is per kind under the **S10c port invariant**: the box's own rsp motion must not straddle an `FR` access (SPAN's ±8 `strchr` dance does not).
- **TAB / RTAB**: one 4-byte entry-delta each, written at α before any read, read at β, zero internal rsp motion. The window is the SHIFTED locals base (`zls_off` = result+16), so the result slot and operand slots stay flat outside it.
- **BREAK / BREAKX**: BREAK one counter; BREAKX counter plus entry-delta save. BREAKX is generator-kind: the cell suspends across γ/β cycling per S10c and LIFO keeps every β entry at the frontier.
- **LEN / ANY / NOTANY — a MEASURED VERDICT, NOT A REFUSAL.** They own NO ζ locals (state is register arithmetic; β-undo re-reads the operand slot or immediate). Under the S13 fixed-size law a box with no locals has a ZERO cell, so `fc_geom` returning 0 IS their grant. **POS / RPOS** are the canonical pure boxes.
- **ARB (ZB-FC-4, Lon s50 ALL-STACK ruling, ARCH-ZETA S14)**: an 8-byte counter+saved-cursor generator, never a linked activation, so it moved OFF the ZLS2 linked-block `BUMP|RELEASE` grant onto a clean 16-byte fixed cell like SPAN and BAL; its ZLS2 grant was deleted.
- **ALTERNATE has NO cell (ALT-FLAT, s202)**: its state (delta, resume and next-alternative continuations) lives in flat zls quads (see §5).
- **Icon (ZB-ICN-FC-1/2/3)**: `IR_SCAN_TAB` and `IR_SCAN_MOVE` are twins (fscan.r: oldpos saved at α, restored at β then fail): saved cursor at op_off+16, result DESCR flat at op_off+0 and read cross-box. `IR_SCAN_MATCH` is `function{0,1}` single-yield (fstranl.r): needle `{ptr,len}` at op_off+16/+24 is TRANSIENT within α→γ (written by `rt_scan_needle`, read by the memcmp, never read at β). **ZERO-CELL BY LAW** for Icon ANY/MANY/POS (position tests with no private scratch; `many`'s loop runs to completion inside α→γ on eax alone). **NOT-YET for the `function{*}` scans UPTO/FIND/BAL:** a generator scan cursor is durable across γ/β cycling, but inside a scan environment (`s ? upto(c)`) the outer `every`'s re-pump threads control back through `IR_GEN_SCAN`'s enter/leave β WITHOUT preserving the box's own α-push/ω-pop LIFO; a fixed rsp cell double-accounts and corrupts rsp (measured: rung06 scan-subject upto → empty under FORTH, was 6). Standalone `upto(c,s)` re-pumps fine; the hazard is scan-subject only. They stay flat until the scan re-pump story is on the spine.

✅ **LIVE kind set at HEAD, in order:** the ALT-CAP guard (§4), `IR_MATCH_ASSIGN_SAVE` when `fc_save_active` (§5), the value-spine literal line (`LIT_INTEGER/STRING/REAL/CHARSET/NAME` and `VAR` under `fc_vlit_active`, gated by `zc_nofc()` with the `fc_subj_member` exemption, §4), **`IR_DISJUNCTION` with `fc_vdj_active` → 32 bytes (added after the strip; no recovered text, see the git log of the frame rungs)**, then ARB/SPAN/TAB/RTAB/BREAK/BREAKX/BAL/REM at 16 and `IR_SCAN_TAB`/`MOVE`/`MATCH` at 16. ## §3 `zls_fc_cell` — the PAT$N REGION NET-OUT (s191) and the synthetic window base

**The double count (Lon directive s191: "99.999% of allocation are now inside the BB's").** The eight UNCONDITIONAL kinds (SPAN/TAB/RTAB/BREAK/BREAKX/BAL/REM/ARB) each self-push a fixed 16-byte rsp cell at α AND were each granted exactly ONE 16-byte locals quad in the bulk proc-entry carve, which the `fc_hit` window rebase then makes UNREACHABLE (`FR(off)` for `off` inside the window emits `[rsp + off-base]`, never the flat slot). Pure double count, netted out here: the entry's `loff` becomes `FL_FC_SYNTH` and the node consumes one quad (its result) instead of two.

**Restricted to the UNCONDITIONAL arms ON PURPOSE.** `fc_geom`'s conditional arms (`fc_save_active`, `fc_vlit_active`, `fc_alt_fpmax`) read side tables populated during LOWER, so asking them at `zls_build` time is registration-order-dependent: they could answer 0 here and 16 at emit, and that disagreement would silently misplace a cell. All eight kinds were verified 1:1 (one locals quad, one 16-byte cell) against `zls_grant_locals`, and all eight are in `zls_locals_shifted`, so the netted quad is the LOCALS quad, never the cross-box-read result quad. **Together with SLOT-ELIDE S4a (remainder row) this is the arm that drives an all-converted graph's region to 0 and lets the DROP-PROLOGUE rung delete its `sub rsp` entirely.**

**`FL_FC_SYNTH` (was `ZLS_FC_SYNTH`) = 0x7F000.** The synthetic window base is arithmetically irrelevant (it only ever appears as a difference) and is deliberately placed far past any real region so that an ESCAPING reference (a cross-box reader of a netted quad, which the `zls_s4_ok` audit says does not exist) emits an obviously wrong `[rsp + 520192+]` that greps out of the `.s` and faults loudly, instead of silently aliasing the neighbouring node's live slot.

✅ LIVE: body identical (still refuses arm members first, still the eight kinds); the constant is `FL_FC_SYNTH 0x7F000` at the top of the file.

## §4 The two symmetry rulings: NOFC-SYM (`zc_nofc`) and ALT-CAP

### `zc_nofc` — NOFC-DEFAULT-ON (s22r) and why the killswitch lives on ONE line of `fc_geom`

**The measured asymmetry (s22l).** `SCRIP_NOFC` used to flip only the CONSUMER's read (`bb_assign_global`'s vfc, `bb_binop_arith`'s vfcb, `bb_binop_concat_slot`'s vfcc, each carrying its OWN copy of the getenv, the "spelled three times" shape the ZD-K law was written about) while the PRODUCER kept its `fc_geom` grant and kept carving. A refused statement therefore had its producer write `[rsp+0]` and its consumer read the flat slot, nobody's write: `func_call` printed `result: 0`, and loop-resident programs leaked 16 bytes per statement per iteration until the stack died. **The fix is symmetry, not a new mechanism:** under NOFC a node that is not ZD-armed takes NO grant and NO carve (fully flat, the pre-ζ world), and a ZD-armed node is untouched here because the ZD staging sets `op_fc_bytes`/`op_zdepth` itself at the emit choke. Two regimes with no third state is what makes `SCRIP_NOFC=1` an honest instrument for "the FC arm and its five pops are deleted" rather than a self-inflicted break.

**The narrowing, MEASURED both ways: the killswitch belongs on the value-spine literal line ONLY, never at the head of `fc_geom`.** A blanket `if (zc_nofc()) return 0` fixed the entire DEFINE/EVAL break set (`func_call`, `func_call_overhead`, `indirect_dispatch`, `eval_fixed` went green) and then broke FIVE passing pattern programs (`mixed_workload`, `pattern_bt`, `pattern_bt_deep`, `roman`, `string_pattern`). **The reason is a category error the name "FC" invites:** the ZB-FC-3c/ZB-FC-4 cells (the match family, the Icon scan twins, the granted SAVE) are those boxes' REAL AND ONLY STORAGE, their templates are written against the cell, so they are not a legacy alternative to anything; only the ZB-VAL-0/2/3 value-spine grant (the producer half of the popping vfc/vfcb/vfcc read that the non-popping ζ spine replaces) is the "FC arm" the ladder retires, so only that line answers to the killswitch.

**The default-ON measurement (s22r, crosscheck 317, one binary, `setarch -R`).** Default m3 199/118, m4 186/130/1, DIV 13 → NOFC m3 204/113, m4 188/128/1, DIV 16: FIVE fixed in m3 (`063/064/065_pat_fence_fn_*`, `116_pat_arbno_of_fence_inline`, `156_pat_cap_alt_abandon_pop`), two in m4, ZERO broken in either mode by set diff. The earlier numbers that deferred the flip were stale for a stated reason: they were measured while `xa_flat_prologue` still carved a whole-graph frame, and that frame is deleted, so neither half of that trade survives its premise. And the third regime was the finding: `SCRIP_NOFC=1` plus `SCRIP_NOFC_CARVE=1` was IDENTICAL BY SET to default in both modes, which proved the value-spine half of the switch a verified no-op (the spine was already fully ZD-armed); 100% of NOFC's delta was the ZW-1 whole-graph carve suppression in `emit.cpp`.

**The s22y subject exemption (`fc_subj_member`).** The subject producer's cell IS the delivery mechanism (the head pops it from TOS at the top of α), not a legacy alternative the spine replaces; suppressing it while `fc_vread_fp` arms the head was the s22s producer/consumer split. Under SUBJECT-CELL rung (b) (crosscheck 318, m3 220→233, m4 217→229, zero broken) the exemption is why subject scalars still carve under NOFC.

### ALT-CAP (s66) — the s65c INVISIBLE-RED root cause, and why THE GRANT IS THE FIX

s202's premise for arm residents ("zero rsp motion") is FALSE AT EMISSION: `bb_match_alternate` carves its own 32-byte record at α (`sub rsp,32`: cursor@0, β-target@8, next-alternative@16). An arm-resident `ASSIGN_SAVE` denied its cell here fell to the flat `rt_cap` array path, whose raw flat coordinate is spelled with `op_zdepth` = the box's OWN carve = 0, leaving the slot exactly the ALT's 32 too low: `rt_cap_push` received unclaimed stack. **MEASURED with four minted, oracle-checked variants:** a capture INSIDE an arm (`'ab' | 'xy' . ## §5 The registrars: `fc_arm_member` (ALT-FLAT), the ALT/SEQ/capture/HEAD side tables, and their caps

**ALT-FLAT (s202, Lon: "fixed offsets all the way down").** Every node inside a granted ALT's arm ranges is registered at `fc_alt_register` time, and `fc_geom` and `zls_fc_cell` BOTH refuse it: arm residents keep their FLAT zls quads and push NO rsp cell, so every arm's rsp footprint is 0, every arm yields at the ALT's own frontier, and the S10d pad-to-max law became structurally unnecessary and was deleted. **ORDER IS LOAD-BEARING:** `fc_walk_range`'s per-arm admission walk (`lower_snobol4.c`) runs BEFORE registration, so admission footprint math is computed on the pre-refuse answers; every LATER reader (statement walk, `fc_leaf_walk`, the fct twins, `zls_build`, emit staging) sees the refuse.

**Why side tables keyed by raw node pointer, and the caps.** The PEERS RULE forbids new `IR_t` fields, and appending operands would corrupt `flat_drive`'s `N = n_operands/2`; the tables therefore key by node pointer with the same staleness envelope as the zls tables (fresh process per compile; EVAL graphs mint fresh nodes, see §7). `fc_alt_register` refuses N>10 silently because 3N+2 must fit `XA_BB_EMIT_PAIR_MAX` (32) stub pairs, and holds 256 ALTs; `fcm` (arm members) holds 1024.

**SEQ owns no cell and no runtime datum (ZB-FC-3b, derived from the live tree, overturning the written plan).** S13 prescribed pad-to-max σ stubs and a per-i un-pad in shared glue (ALT's shape); SEQ needs neither. With F the SEQ's frontier and S_i the sum of element footprints 0..i, at element i's γ rsp = F − S_i, exactly where element i+1's α expects it, and at its ω the subtree has popped so rsp = F − S_{i−1}, exactly element i−1's yield frontier where its β expects it. Every σ/φ transition is already at the correct depth, every dispatch target is statically known from the source element, so `seq_i` is DEAD: the LIFO stack position IS the sequence position (the s32 two-flavor ruling landing on its feet: the stack flavor's representation of "seq_i and the head link" is NO DATUM AT ALL). The ANS-FORTH asymmetry, exact: ALTERNATION needs a runtime flag (ALT keeps `alt_i`); SEQUENCING does not. ⛔ LIVE: `IR_MATCH_SEQUENCE` was deleted outright (SEQ-ERAD SE-5/SE-6) and `fc_seq_active` is `return 0`; the derivation is kept here because it is the reason sequencing has no box at all.

**Captures are TWO boxes (ZB-FC-3c; `fc_save_*`, `fc_cond_*`).** `ASSIGN_SAVE` owns the 16-byte cell (delta at cell+0; the software `rt_cap` array retired on the granted path). `ASSIGN_COND`/`IMM`, different IR nodes with NO cell of their own, read SAVE's delta through a CROSS-BOX rsp displacement: by the S10c law (cells pop at ω, not γ) the entire inner subtree is still suspended at COND's α, so SAVE's cell sits at `[rsp + fp(inner)]`, a static displacement, which is ALTERNATE's own-cell-across-arm mechanism reused, not invented. `fp(inner)` is LOWER's range sum over the inner allocation range, exact only for fc-LINEAR spines, so eligibility is TWO-DIRECTIONAL: an ARBNO/ALT/ARB/DEFER inside the inner breaks the static displacement just as a capture inside an ARBNO body breaks the port invariant; either direction refuses both registrations and the capture keeps the flat `rt_cap` path verbatim. COND/IMM stay ZERO-CELL BY LAW: a cell would silently inflate every enclosing ALT's fpmax (the lowerer's arm sum picks up granted SAVEs automatically and must pick up nothing else). IMM is the identical topology at phase 2. `fc_cond_fp` also refuses (−1) when the SAVE is an arm member (ALT-FLAT coherence: SAVE gets no cell, so COND must not read one). **FLATDISP-LEAF-ORDER (`fc_pair_extent_register`, the `rt_dcap_pump` segv root):** capture pairs ALLOCATE `[COND, SAVE, inner...]` but FLOW SAVE→inner→COND, so `fc_leaf_walk`'s running prefix registered COND before SAVE's 16 accumulated and COND read the SAVE slot 16 short of the true depth (program 039: `[rsp+32]` over the head's rsp-snapshot cell, the `0xffff9b20` gdb saw; SAVE's own write at `[rsp+48]` was right). The lowerer records each pair's inner allocation END, and the walk recurses `[k+1, E)` first and registers COND at the RESULT prefix, the S10c suspended depth.

**HEAD is not hook-shaped (ZB-FC-3d, the D4 splice-survivor fork resolved by PARTITION, s49; `fc_head_*`).** `fc_geom` stays 0 for HEAD BY LAW so no enclosing sum ever counts a self-releasing cell. ## §6 `zw_node_k` / `zw_carve_k` — THE ONE CARVE AUTHORITY (ZW-1, CARVE-ERAD step 1) and the s137 perturbation

**`zw_node_k`, the ZW-1 UNIVERSAL K AUTHORITY (Lon s21x-m: "turn on the per-BB allocation across the board, then crawl the rungs").** The ONE per-node cell-size answer for the universal α-sub/ω-add regime: K = the node's whole zls extent (front result quad plus granted locals, rounded to 16), EXCEPT a dead-result node with no locals, which gets ZERO (the zero-cell law: RESULT iff it has one and it is used, LOCALS iff any; no zls entry means no storage need). v0 was CARVE-ONLY BY DESIGN: the consumer set `op_fc_bytes` WITHOUT `op_fc_base`, so `x86_fc_hit` never fired, every `FR`/`FRQ` spelling stayed byte-identical, and the only new machine effect was the rsp motion at the hook: one variable isolated, so the crosscheck fail-set became the rung ladder. Window migration (front quad and locals riding the cell) was the crawl's second axis.

**`zw_carve_k` answers ONE question, how many bytes this node carves on rsp at its own α, and is the ONLY place that question is answered (Lon: "complete ZETA CELLS on the RSP FORTH-style stack; remove the whole graph carve").** Provenance: the predicate was written INLINE at `emit.cpp`'s walk choke, where it decided `op_fc_bytes`; the lower-side prefix walk (`fc_leaf_walk`, the `op_flat_disp` registrar) accumulated `fc_geom` cells only and could not see a universal carve. **THAT SPLIT IS THE DISPLACEMENT:** a spine producer carves K at α and holds it live for its consumer (RESULT-IS-THE-CELL), while a consumer still addressing through the static authority compensates by a prefix computed as if the carve did not exist, and reads K bytes too low (the `emit.cpp` negative result "arming a spine producer that FEEDS a match deepens rsp under a consumer whose compensation cannot see it": the arming frontier is a property of the READERS, not of the armed box). With one authority the allocator and the address side cannot hold two opinions, the same ONE-K discipline `zw_node_k` established for the size; port, regime and bisect conjuncts all live here so the prefix advances by EXACTLY the bytes the α hook will spend. UNIVERSAL CARVE is default-ON (Lon s12): `SCRIP_BB_ALLOC=0` opts out, `SCRIP_BB_ALLOC_ALL=0` restores the exclusion list (sr-citizen DEFINE, calls, `GOTO_DEFERRED`, and the grammar boxes GLIT/GCC/GALT, which use `[___+N]` frame slots exclusively: RK-GRAM-3d). A node `fc_geom` grants is not carved twice.

**⭐ s137: THE s136 PERTURBATION, localised and cured at its source.** `bb_node_id` in dense mode (mode 4) is a LAZY allocate-on-first-call counter, so calling it ASSIGNS a number in CALLER-VISIT ORDER. `zw_carve_k` called it UNCONDITIONALLY while consuming it only under the `SCRIP_BB_ONLY`/`SCRIP_BB_SKIP` bisect filters, so `zdp_analyze` (which reads the carve authority BEFORE the emitter walks) seeded every nid in LATTICE order instead of EMIT order and every `.Lbynamefnzd<nid>` label moved: the whole of the 81/656 movers, an allocation, suspect #1 of the finding's ranked list. ⛔ **THE CURE IS NOT TO REPLICATE THE EMITTER'S VISIT ORDER** (a second opinion about an order somebody else owns, and it would drift); it is to STOP NUMBERING WHEN THE NUMBER IS NOT WANTED. ## §7 `fc_tables_reset` — stale node-pointer keys across runtime compiles (s67, the 140/test_case wild jump)

The fc side tables are keyed by RAW NODE POINTER and were only ever fed by MAIN lowering. A RUNTIME compile (an EVAL chain, a CODE fragment, an rt pattern tree) lowers fresh IR whose malloc'd nodes can land on a FREED prior graph's addresses (`eval_build_chain` ends in `IR_free_dyn`). Historically a stale hit emitted ONE r12-viewed box inside an otherwise rsp-viewed self-allocated blob, whose `[r12+disp]` writes landed ABOVE the shim anchor, straight over the C caller's frame (measured: `EVAL_fn`'s return address became 0x21; the blob at +0xa5 wrote `0x148(%r12)` while its own allocation was `sub 0x1b0,%rsp`). A stale displacement hit is the same disease with a silent wrong-displacement payload. Every runtime-compile entry resets the fc tables BEFORE lowering: emission consults them only for the graph lowered since the reset, and all pre-reset graphs are already emitted (mode 3 emits main wholesale before run). The zls tables share the pointer keying and are NOT reset here (`bb_compile_pat_tree`'s `zls_reset` is the precedent; the zls lifecycle belongs to the frame-map design).

⛔ **THE GATE THIS SECTION NAMED WAS BUILT, AND THE ARGUMENT IT WAS MEANT TO HOLD WAS FALSE (cto, 2026-09-18, row `rationale-frame-layout-c-remainder`; the full record is `ARCH-FRAME-LAYOUT-GRANTS.md` §16).** This section used to end: *the other side tables are not touched by it; their safety rests on the "all pre-reset graphs are already emitted" argument, which no gate holds today.* Both halves of that were stale. `fc_tables_reset` reset ONE counter of sixteen, and the stated reason for the asymmetry — that the zls tables are not reset here either — did not describe the tree: **all three runtime-compile entries already called `zls_reset()` wholesale on the line beside it.** ⭐ **MEASURED, not argued:** a 300-distinct-runtime-compile SNOBOL4 EVAL witness makes 615 registrations at **609 distinct addresses** — six addresses registered twice by two different graphs through recycled memory, and on `fvr` (a node→frame-position map whose lookup returns the FIRST match) two registrations found a live stale entry for their own address. The stale position happened to agree with the fresh one, so the answer was right: **the hazard was live and the damage was luck.** ⛔ And the caps were a per-PROCESS budget for a per-graph table: the counters climbed one per compile (`fvl` 310, `fvr` 305 at 300 compiles), so past ~1000 compiles `fc_vcap` silently refused the value-spine plan for the rest of the run. ✅ **CURED:** all sixteen pointer-keyed counters are reset; `pz_n` is the one named exclusion (keyed by graph NAME, updated in place, read by a later compile for a graph already emitted).
