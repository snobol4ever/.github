# FINDING 2026-09-04 (ceo, QUARTET) — CRLF came back because `csv.writer` defaults to CRLF and no law or gate said LF

**CLAIM.** Lon ordered LF on 2026-09-03 (*"Do not use CRLF, use LF."*) and had to order it again on 2026-09-04 (*"Fix the CRLF to LF problem. Why am I still hearing about that?"*). The order had been executed on one file and recorded in no law and no instrument, so the writer that produces the files kept producing CRLF and a later seat restored CRLF in good faith.

**MEASURED (corpus `c2c7d8396`, 2026-09-04 11:10 CDT).** Tracked files with CR outside `packages/`: `tests/{snobol4,raku,snocone,pascal,rebus}/ALL.csv` (1737 · 860 · 284 · 151 of 167 · 78 lines with CR) and `benchmarks/icon/geddump.s` (14 CR bytes, all inside a `.string "\t\n\r "` — data, not line endings). `tests/prolog/ALL.csv` (corpus `1feca4aa4`) and `tests/icon/ALL.csv` were LF. RULES.md: zero occurrences of CRLF or LF as a rule.

**ROOT CAUSE.** `scripts/util_build_master_suite.py` opens `tmp_csv` with `newline=""` and calls `csv.writer(f)` at three sites; Python's `csv.writer` default `lineterminator` is `\r\n`. Every master rebuild re-minted CRLF. Two census writers (`util_census_optimizer_bypass.py`, `util_census_function_scope_statics.py`) had the same default. corpus `40441ed53` (09-04 00:04) then RESTORED snobol4's CRLF — the seat applied the binary-read rule (change only the bytes you mean to) correctly, against a file whose CRLF was itself the defect, because no law said which byte was the mistake.

**CURE (landed).** corpus `335cd65d9` `6508f24fe` `dc6385986` `6bf54e218` `169a93209` — the five files LF, one commit each, each proven by `cmp` against HEAD's bytes with CRs stripped, `csv.reader` row count and the `test_gate_no_xfail_survives.sh` population identical before and after. SCRIP `6eab9de95` — all five `csv.writer` sites pass `lineterminator="\n"`; new gate `test_gate_our_files_are_lf.sh` (rc=0/1/2, offenders named, population printed, `corpus/packages/` and generated flex/bison outputs excluded, end-of-line CR only) wired into `make test`'s cheap tier; fail-once proven on a scratch repo (rc=1 with one CRLF file, rc=0 after LF, rc=2 on a non-repo); real run 5050 files, 0 CRLF, 0.3 s. Law: RULES.md § FACT RULES, OUR FILES ARE LF (this landing).

**THE LESSON, already in the instrument laws and re-witnessed:** an order executed on the instance and not on the producer is undone by the next production; a rule with no gate is a memo; a seat obeying a byte-preservation rule against a defective baseline preserves the defect. The gate is the difference between hearing about it once and hearing about it every day.
