## §11 `zls_is_wiring` — THE PHANTOM 16-BYTE RESULT SLOT (ZW-5, O-1 LIGHTING, s23q/s30/RK-GRAM-3d)

**Recovered.** A *wiring* op is pure control: K=0, no result cell, no value produced, no zls extent. It must be named **before** the grant switch runs, because **`zls_entry()` and the result `zls_field()` both fire unconditionally ahead of the switch**. That ordering is the whole lesson: the earlier cure, `case IR_ASSIGN/IR_STATEMENT: return 0` inside `zls_grant`, was *too late* — returning 0 from the switch only stopped `k` incrementing; the node had already been registered with a 16-byte field, so `zls_node_bytes` returned 16 and the span still included it. The phantom slot shifted every subsequent node's offset by +16 and inflated the UCLAIM span.

Three enrolments, each with its own witness:
- **`IR_STATEMENT` (ZW-5, O-1, s23q)** — the statement-terminal trailer.
- **`IR_STATEMENT_BEGIN` / `IR_STATEMENT_END` (s30)** — same law, both K=0 pure-relay/whack boxes. **MEASURED:** without them the two phantom slots inflated the UCLAIM span by 32 (2×16) and corrupted the match frame's `[ζ+88]` outer-base restore slot; `058_pat_fence_keyword` segfaulted with base/rsp = 0x0 at `main_γ`, and `ZW5=0` cured it.
- **`IR_GLIT` / `IR_GCC` / `IR_GALT` (RK-GRAM-3d)** — the Raku grammar scanner nodes are scanner-register-only, the same class as `IR_STATEMENT`.

⚠ **LIVE, AND THE LIST HAS MOVED UNDER THE RECOVERED TEXT.** At HEAD the class is `IR_GOTO, IR_GATE_ARM, IR_GOTO_DEFERRED, IR_SUCCEED, IR_FAIL, IR_RETURN, IR_SUSPEND, IR_CORET, IR_COFAIL, IR_CUT, IR_MATCH_END, IR_STATEMENT, IR_STATEMENT_BEGIN, IR_STATEMENT_END, IR_STMT_MARK, IR_GLIT, IR_GCC, IR_GALT` (`frame_layout.c:228`), consulted at 13 sites. Two changes the recovered text cannot know about:
- ⛔ **`IR_MOVE_LABEL` IS GONE** from the tree and `IR_GATE_ARM` has taken its place in the class. Any recovered sentence naming `MOVE_LABEL` — including the SLOT-ELIDE liveness fence in §13 — reads today as `GATE_ARM`.
- ⭐ **`IR_STMT_MARK` JOINED** the class. That is the line-mark box (`bb_line_mark`, `src/templates/bb/bb_stmt_mark.cpp`, CTO-16/23) that stores `g_line` once per statement in every frontend; it is exactly the same shape of K=0 relay, and it is in the class for exactly the s30 reason. The wiring class is therefore the standing answer to "a new pure-control box appeared": **enrol it here, or it silently buys a 16-byte result slot nobody reads.**

## §12 SN4-FRAME-DIET (s174) — THE REACHABILITY GRANT AND ITS ROOT REPAIR

**Recovered.** The frame diet grants storage only to nodes the emitter can actually reach, by an **entry-rooted walk** over the graph, and deletes the default-arm phantom: *"every armless value producer (LIT/VAR/binop class) burned result+16 with no registered field and no reader"*. Two repairs make the walk honest:
- **THE WIRING-ROOT REPAIR.** The emitter is **CHAIN-BFS**: every statement anchor (`IR_GOTO`) and every deferred or indirect transfer is an emission root of its own. Label-reachable chains with **no static γ/ω in-edge** — an indirect `:($X)` goto, a `CODE` label transfer — would otherwise be walked as unreachable and left ungranted, and then emitted. Witnesses: crosscheck 214/215/216/1020/1021 and the `drive_value_slot` FATAL. Wiring ops take no ticks themselves; rooting them marks their subtrees. ⭐ The **registry-only `NRETURN` landing** has no wiring in-edge and is not itself wiring, so it stays ungranted — the exception is deliberate and is the shape of the whole rule: *a root is an emission entry, not merely an unreferenced node*.
- **SN4-FLAT-PROC (s176)** adds label-span roots and the anchor-carrying mark, with slot0/base 0.
- **Dynamic-transfer graphs take FULL grants** — a graph that can jump anywhere cannot be dieted by reachability at all, and asking for a smaller answer there is asking the planner to guess.

⚠ **LIVE, with one number four times larger than the text says.** The walk is at `frame_layout.c:519` and still names its own repair. But **`FL_MAX_MARKS` is 262144 at HEAD, not the 65536 the recovered text records** (`frame_layout.c:19`), and the mark table's overflow is an `abort()` with a named message, not a degrade — which is correct for a table whose overflow would silently under-root a graph, and is worth knowing before anyone "tidies" it into a refusal.

## §13 SLOT-ELIDE S0 / S1 / S4a and the ZB-VAL-8b USE PREDICATE

Lon set the goal three times in three sittings, and the three rungs are his three sentences:

- **S0, THE CENSUS (s133, "unused result quads are bug #1").** `SCRIP_SLOT_CENSUS=1` prints, per graph, how many result quads exist against how many are ever referenced **as a VALUE operand**. Operand references from the pure-wiring construct kinds (ALT/SEQ/ARBNO/FENCE1, the Icon scan twins, REPALT, and what was then MOVE_LABEL) do **not** count — their operands are entry/resume *nodes*, not value reads. `DISJUNCTION` and `MATCH_BEGIN` are deliberately left in the value-reader class because their operand lists mix wiring with real reads, **so the dead count is a conservative FLOOR on the S1 win, never an estimate of it.** An O(n²) walk, census only.
- **S1, THE SCRATCH ALIAS (s133, "some BB's will have a result and some will not determined by NEED").** A node whose result nobody reads gets **no quad of its own**: its `zx` entry aliases ONE shared per-graph scratch quad (the first dead node's own slot becomes it), so templates that self-write results write harmlessly and the layout math never sees a hole. The frame shrinks by (dead−1)×16 and the prologue `stosb` with it — which matters because **s132 measured the match phase STORE-BOUND at 63% stores**. **Two safety fences, both op-filters so drift cannot manufacture a wrong answer:** (1) only kinds POSITIVELY KNOWN to own zero locals may elide (`zls_elide_ok` — a kind with locals keeps its result, so the locals@+16 layout law is untouched); (2) liveness marks a node on ANY operand reference except from the pure-wiring constructs — **conservative toward LIVE**. `SCRIP_SLOT_ELIDE=0` is the killswitch.
- **S4a (s138, "reduce the RSP adjustments to ZERO for BB's whose result is NOT USED and have just the local BB memory if any").** The locals-shifted SNOBOL4 match family's front 16-byte "result" quad is runtime-DEAD BY CONSTRUCTION — every runtime accessor reads past it — so `zls_s4_ok` marks that class dead-front-quad without needing per-instance liveness.
- **THE USE PREDICATE (ZB-VAL-8b, s182, "allocate its RESULT value, IF it has one and if it is used").** This is the measured answer to *"is THIS INSTANCE's result cell ever read as a value"*, **which the IR itself cannot express**: `IR.h` carries no `n_uses`/`use_count`/`consumed` field, and `ir_node_produces_value()` is OPCODE-keyed — it answers "can this KIND produce a value", never "is this instance's value read". `zls_mark_value_refs` already computed exactly that fact (an `operands[]` reference is a VALUE use; a γ/ω wire is a CONTROL use and grants nothing) but it died as a local array inside `zls_build` after steering layout alone. Persisting it on the entry lets the EMITTER ask, so a box whose result nobody reads can also skip **writing** it. ⭐ **Conservative by construction: an unknown node answers 1.**
- **`IR_CMP_TEST` joining the zero-locals class was AUDITED, not assumed** — it appears in NO arm of `zls_grant_locals`, its template reads only `FRQ(op_sa)/FRQ(op_sb)/FRQ(op_off)` with no cross-box reader of its front quad, and its control result travels in EAX via test/jcc, never through the cell. The cell holds the SPITBOL predicate value (manual p.33: *"when any of these functions succeed, they produce a null string value"*), which is REAL when read (`OUTPUT = LT(A,B) 'yes'` feeds it to `str_concat_d`) and DEAD when the statement consumes only the S/F branches — **exactly the per-instance discrimination the use predicate measures, and the reason an opcode-keyed answer could never have been right here.**
- **ZLEAK-1 (s21x-r)** is the cautionary half: a **process-scope flag driving a graph-scope regime**. The regime is per graph; a flag that outlives the graph makes one graph's decision another graph's law.

✅ **LIVE:** `zls_elide_ok` (`:244`) is unchanged member for member; `zls_s4_ok` (`:245`); `zls_mark_value_refs` (`:247`, called at `:473`); `zls_result_live` (`:263`) is the one-line entry read `return e ? e->live : 1`, still conservative-toward-live, and is consulted from `src/emitter/emit.cpp` and `src/ir/zeta_depth.c`. Both killswitches (`SCRIP_SLOT_ELIDE`, `SCRIP_SLOT_CENSUS`) are still read. ⚠ The recovered fence sentence names `MOVE_LABEL`, which no longer exists — read it as `GATE_ARM` (§11).

## §14 CARRY-THE-TAIL (R12-EXIT-1) and PS-3 patzeta pricing

**Recovered.** `fc_tail_candidate` registers a HEAD/ARBNO pair with the statement's span indices so the pair's tail can be carried rather than re-derived; the finalize is **two-pass (PS-3, s153)** and prices a **defer as a leaf**. The family carried M-2 BUG-5 (`fpl`), N02-FIX (`bmn`/`rmn`) and WRAP-CAPTURE.

That is not decoration: it is the same hazard §16 is about, solved locally for one table long before anyone named the class. **`emit_patzeta_*` and the `pz` table are pricing keyed by GRAPH NAME**, not by node pointer, and update in place — which is why §16 excludes `pz_n` from the reset by name rather than by oversight.

## §15 THE GRANT REPAIRS — ZLS-CALL-BASE and GENP-SPINE s92

Two repairs to the same defect shape: **the planner's field map spoke a different base than the emitter's addressing**, and because the emitter computes argv addresses itself, the blast radius was the FIELD MAP and never the addressing — which is exactly why it survived so long.

- **ZLS-CALL-BASE (MEASURED, not assumed).** All three call arms spoke NODE-BASE while `zls_grant_locals` is handed the LOCALS base (`zls_grant` calls it with `off+16`), so **every registered call field sat one quad high**; two arms also multiplied the base by the arg index (`off*j`). Emitter truth, plain and by-name-gen arms alike: `argbase = resoff+16`, arg *j* at `argbase+16*j`, the extra quad at `resoff+16*(1+nargs)` — with `off = resoff+16` those are `off+16*j` and `off+16*nargs`, **one truth**. BEFORE: `REPLACE(S,'lo','LO')` registered argv at +0/+48/+96 — a phantom DESCR landing on ANOTHER node's result quad at +0, and a `hi_off` one quad past the node — against the emitter's +48/+64/+80. AFTER: exact. ⛔ **What was corrupted was scope `lo_off`/`hi_off`, `zls_node_bytes` (→ `g_emit.op_own_ci`) and THE GC KIND MAP** — which is why this section matters to the collector rung and not only to the planner. Its gate: mode-3 crosscheck 314/1 with the fail SET identical to a rebuilt pre-change baseline (**not merely an equal count**), and mode-4 14/14 call-heavy with DIVERGE=0.
- **GENP-SPINE (s92).** Pre-existing, exposed by `t_poison`'s 0-operand `return`-generator: the `IR_PROC_GEN`/`IR_CALL_VALUE` arm returned `-1 + n_operands`, so a **0-operand generator moved the cursor BACKWARD one unit**; the graph-scope resume/zeta_mark slots landed ON the call's own result DESCR and every value delivery smashed the anchor the ret-epilogue restores rsp from (rc=139 at graph exit, s91-reproducible). The unit count is now `2 + n` (result + argv + act), matching the staged-gen sibling.

✅ **LIVE:** both formulas are at HEAD and the `off*j` typo is gone. The staged-call sibling is the `IR_CALL_PROC_STAGED` grant that `ARCH-FRAME-LAYOUT-FIXED-CELLS.md` §9 documents — a registered-generator callee gets the two `callgen.act` quads and any other staged call is granted nothing.

## §16 `fc_tables_reset` — THE SIDE TABLES ARE KEYED BY RAW NODE POINTER, AND A RUNTIME COMPILE RECYCLES ADDRESSES (cto, 2026-09-18; the instrument this row owed)

`ARCH-FRAME-LAYOUT-FIXED-CELLS.md` §7 records the reset's purpose and ends: *"Their safety rests on the 'all pre-reset graphs are already emitted, and a fresh node is only looked up by its own graph's emission' argument in the recovered text, which no gate holds today; that gate is named in the remainder row."* This is that gate, and building it found the argument was false.

⛔ **BOTH HALVES OF §7's EXPLANATION WERE STALE AT HEAD.**
- `fc_tables_reset` reset **one counter of sixteen** (`fct_n`, the tail candidates of §14). Fifteen pointer-keyed tables — `fca fcab fcc fch fcm fcs fcv fpe fvb fvcl fvdj fvl fvr fvs fvw` — were never reset in the life of the process.
- §7 explained the asymmetry by saying the zls tables are not reset there either. **They are.** All three runtime-compile entries (`runtime_eval.c` `eval_build_chain` and `code_at`, `bb_pat_build.cpp` `bb_compile_pat_tree_sz`) already call `zls_reset()` wholesale on the line beside `fc_tables_reset()`. So the stated reason did not describe the tree, and nothing else did.

⭐ **THE HAZARD IS REAL AND WAS MEASURED, NOT ARGUED.** A 300-distinct-runtime-compile SNOBOL4 EVAL witness, with a temporary probe on the registrars (reverted): **615 registrations at 609 DISTINCT ADDRESSES** — six addresses registered twice, by two different graphs, through recycled memory. On `fvr`, whose entries are a node→**frame position** map and whose lookup returns the **first** match, two registrations found a live stale entry for their own address. In that witness the stale frame position happened to AGREE with the fresh one, so the answer was right: **the hazard was live and the damage was luck.** Nothing made them agree. This is the same disease §7 names, whose earlier instance emitted one r12-viewed box inside an rsp-viewed blob and overwrote the C caller's frame (`EVAL_fn`'s return address became 0x21).

⛔ **AND THE CAP WAS A PER-PROCESS BUDGET FOR A PER-GRAPH TABLE.** With nothing reset, the counters climbed monotonically with the number of distinct runtime compiles: **measured `fvl` 310 and `fvr` 305 after 300 compiles**, one per compile, against caps of 2048 and 1024. Past roughly a thousand distinct EVAL or CODE compiles `fvr` saturated and the value-spine plan was **silently refused for the rest of the process** — correct, because `fc_vcap` makes the planner refuse before the registrar's backstop fires (measured at 3000 compiles: answer still exact, `fc_reg_full` never printed), but permanently and progressively deoptimised with no diagnostic. **A performance cliff nobody would ever have seen.**

✅ **AS LANDED:** `fc_tables_reset` resets all sixteen pointer-keyed counters. After the cure the same witness reads `fvl` 10 / `fvr` 5, and the answers of every witness are byte-identical before and after. **`pz_n` is the ONE named exclusion and must stay one:** the `pz` table is keyed by **graph name**, updates an existing row in place rather than appending, and holds the patzeta pricing a later compile looks up by name for a graph emitted earlier — resetting it would throw away the price of every graph already emitted. ⭐ **Pointer keying is what makes a table unsafe across a compile; name keying is what makes this one necessary across it.** Held by `scripts/test_gate_fc_side_tables_are_reset_at_every_runtime_compile_entry.sh` (4 blocking arms: counter coverage with the two named exclusions printed, the two resets travelling together at every entry, counter boundedness over 300 compiles through the tree's own `SCRIP_FC_REG_HIGHWATER` reporter, and the witnesses graded against the SPITBOL oracle in both media). **FAIL-ONCE measured on a rebuilt tree, not asserted:** with the cure backed out, arm 1 names all fifteen omitted counters and arm 3 reads 310 against a ceiling of 64, while arms 2 and 4 stay green — because the call sites were never the defect and the answer was right by luck.

## §17 ZLS2 — HISTORY, AND WHY IT IS HERE AT ALL

**Recovered.** `zls2_geom`, the `BUMP`/`RELEASE` protocol and the per-role table for ARB and ARBNO phases 0/1/2, under **Lon 2026-07-08 (s7): *"ONLY α AND ω PARTICIPATE IN MEMORY ALLOCATION"***.

⛔ **LIVE: gone at HEAD, zero hits.** The sentence that outlived the code is the ruling, not the protocol: **only α and ω allocate.** ZLS2 was one implementation of it; the fixed cell (`sub rsp,K` at α, `add rsp,K` at ω — `ARCH-FRAME-LAYOUT-FIXED-CELLS.md` §0) is the one that survived. Preserved here so a future reader who finds the phrase in an old ledger knows which half is law and which half is a deleted mechanism.
