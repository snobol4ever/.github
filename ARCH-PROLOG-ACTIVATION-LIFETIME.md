## 1. THE ACTIVATION

A Prolog ACTIVATION is one clause of one predicate entered once: the construct whose lifetime the clause's cells are tied to. It is born at the clause's entry (the frame header is written, rbp links to the caller's frame) and it DIES at the last of three moments: (a) its deterministic exit -- the clause succeeded and left no choice point of its own or of any callee below it; (b) the failure that backtracks past its entry; (c) the cut or the construct exit that drops its last choice point. Between (a) and the later of (b)/(c) an activation that left a choice point is RETAINED: its frame stays live on the machine stack below the caller's continuation while the caller runs on. That retention is the whole difficulty of the regime and the single source of every unbounded situation in it:

- after a RETAINING CALL (the callee's activation retained) the caller's rsp is NOT the rsp it had before the call: the callee's live frame and cells sit between -- the depth is INDEFINITE for the rest of the caller's continuation until the retained frame is dropped;
- a GENERATOR'S RESUMPTION (a range `between/3`, a db walk over clauses, a disjunction's next arm, a retained callee's next solution) re-enters a box at a depth the box did not leave it at -- a retry never restores rsp (CEO-1755/1759);
- a RECURSION is a retaining call by nature when any clause of the predicate leaves a choice point.

Lon's CEO-1942 names these three as the ONLY reasons a cell leaves rsp: *a generator's resumption, a retry after an unknown number of pushes, a recursion*. The Prolog regime has nothing else.

## 2. THE THREE HOMES IN THE PROLOG REGIME

- **ζ-SPINE** `[rsp + c]`: THE DEFAULT. Each box carves exactly the cell it needs at its α and pops it at its ω; a local is carved at first use; every address is a compile-time constant distance from rsp. A cell lives here when every read of it happens at a KNOWN DEPTH relative to its carve -- no unbounded excursion between the write and the read.
- **ζ-ACT** `[rbp - off]`: the ACTIVATION FRAME, one block per clause activation, carrying the frame header and ONLY the cells the spine cannot reach: a cell written before an unbounded excursion and read after it, or a generator's own resumption state. A cell here is RESIDENT: `zls_hf_resident(g, nd)` admits its box, the layout grants its words inside the frame region (`zls_build`, below `zls_hf_key_base`), and `FRQ(off)` addresses it through rbp. A key granted ABOVE the resident region on a home-frame graph has NO FRAME BYTES behind it (`zls_key_unbacked`), and a read of one is the FATAL in `x86_zop` -- the scream that is the work list of D3.
- **ζ-STANDING**: the root frame (mode-3 main's or the program's), reached by walking the rbp links to the null link. Prolog's standing cells are the trail header's and the database's handles; none of the clause's own.

THE SWITCH: every Prolog graph is a HOME-FRAME graph (`g->home_frame = 1` in `pl_graph_stamp`, lower_prolog.c, kept at SCRIP 4edf4b785 and never restored: Lon, CEO-1945, *"keep it whacked and fix the misplaced ones by changing that carve logic to be proper"*). The frame is sized by its RESIDENT words, never by keys (CEO-1895's 338,540-slot frames were keys).

### 6.1 THE RELEASE

THE WAKE PROBE already exists: IR_CLEANUP's wake arm (bb_cleanup.cpp:118-125) compares the header's wake word with 0 and gamma runs `'$wakeup'`; the lowerer plants it only in a program whose text names put_attr or freeze (prolog_parse.c:4004, 4348, the `armed` rule).

**10.3 THE DESIGN. The record is a binding of a header cell, so no unwinder learns a kind.** Two DESCR cells join the header: UNDO_LIST at offset 80 and UNDO_PENDING at offset 96 (the static asserts of rt_pl_trail.c gain their lines; both rooted by `pl_tr_gc_root_ball`). `undo(G)` is a prelude predicate: `undo(G) :- '$undo_push'(G).` whose leaf copies G to the heap (copy_term's copy, as findall copies: the bindings of the undo call are kept), then TRAILS THE HEADER CELL AS A BINDING (`pl_tr_push(cx, UNDO_LIST)`: cell = the header cell's address, old = the list as it is) and prepends: UNDO_LIST = [Copy | UNDO_LIST]. Every unwinder then restores UNDO_LIST to the list as it was at the mark by the two instructions it already has, in C, inline and in asm alike: the entry is a binding entry of a cell that happens to live in the header. THE ONE PRE-STEP, in the two unwinders a body goal's entry can reach (`rt_pl_tr_unwind_to`: one line; `x86_pl_tr_unwind_at`: four instructions before the loop, in the one helper, so the four template sites change by nothing): if UNDO_PENDING is empty, UNDO_PENDING = UNDO_LIST. After the unwind the goals to run are exactly the cells of UNDO_PENDING that are not in UNDO_LIST: UNDO_LIST is a tail of UNDO_PENDING (the list is prepend-only and every unwind restores it to an older tail), so the walk is: from UNDO_PENDING until the cell equals UNDO_LIST. Several unwinds before one probe (a clause chain trying heads) keep the first PENDING, because the pre-step writes it only when it is empty, and every later unwind only moves UNDO_LIST further down the same chain. The asm leaf and the cleanup record learn nothing.

**10.4 The probe is the wake probe's second arm.** IR_CLEANUP's wake node gains the undo arm: `cmp UNDO_PENDING, 0; je gamma; call '$undo_run'` (one compare and a not-taken jump when nothing is pending; the same node shape, the same template, one more arm). `'$undo_run'` (prelude): takes UNDO_PENDING and UNDO_LIST, clears UNDO_PENDING FIRST (so a goal that itself backtracks or calls undo/1 starts a fresh pending set), walks PENDING to LIST and runs each goal as `call/1`: failure of a goal is ignored (swipl's `ignore`), an exception propagates through the ball road as any exception does (test error). WHERE THE LOWERER PLANTS IT, under a program-wide fact ARMED WHEN THE PROGRAM'S TEXT NAMES undo (the wake probe's own `armed` rule, extended to the name `undo`): before the first goal of every clause body, before every body goal that follows a goal (the call port swipl uses), and before the recovery goal of catch/3 (the exception case: the gate's handler road unwinds the trail to the gate's mark, the pre-step filled PENDING, the probe before Recovery runs the goals before the handler, as swipl's error2 asserts). A program that never names undo pays nothing; the probe is a lowering decision (prolog_lower.c's body walk, where the wake probe is placed), never a template branch. The C meta-calls of the dispatcher need no change: their `rt_pl_tr_unwind_to` fills PENDING and the next probe runs the goals.

**10.5 What this is not.** Not a trail entry kind: no unwinder tests a pad value, no asm changes, the collector's trail scan is unchanged (the entry's old is a list cell rooted as every old value is; the two header cells are rooted by the ball visitor). Not a run of a goal mid-unwind: the goals run at the probe, after the whole unwind, as swipl's. Not a third stack and not a global (CEO-1542: the cells live in the r12 island's header, the one place the trail's own state lives).
