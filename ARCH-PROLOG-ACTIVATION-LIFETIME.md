## 0. The activation

## 1. When the frame is released: the one rule

The frame of activation A is released at the FIRST of these, and at no other place:

- **(a) its ω** -- the last alternative fails: `ZFRAME_CLOSE` (`mov rsp, rbp; pop rbp`), then the call box's ω tail pops the argument block and jumps to the caller's predecessor β. B is already B0(A).
- **(b) a FENCED γ** -- A exits with `B == B0(A)`: no choice point younger than its entry is live (its own alternatives committed or exhausted, every callee exited deterministically). `ZFRAME_CLOSE` BEFORE the γ, so the caller's continuation finds rsp at the call box's exit depth and stays definite. Today's `altdet` road (xa_flat.cpp:749-769, `cmp r13, [kt - 40]; je altdet`) is this test at run time; under the planner the test is STATIC where `RETAINS(callee)` is false (pass (i.a)) and the compare is emitted only where it is unknown.
- **(c) a LAST CALL** -- A's last goal is a call and at that point `B == B0(A)` and no cell of A's frame is needed after the callee returns. The callee's argument block is built on the spine, A's frame is released BEFORE the call, the callee takes A's continuations as its own. The cfo's (A): `bcps_block_tail_arm` releases iff the site is armed, `r13` equals the frame's saved choice and every argument cell passes the per-cell check; count/1, mk/2 and app/3 run 1,000,000 deep at 4 MB. THE THREE DECLINES it measured: len/2 is not a last call (correct); cp/1 after member/2 has `B != B0` (correct); `lv(N1, _)` passing a FRESH UNBOUND variable of A's frame declines forever (WRONG: 9,336 deep at 4 MB where swipl runs 1,000,000). THE RULE: a fresh unbound variable of A passed in a last call is GLOBALIZED at the call -- a heap cell is allocated, A's frame cell is bound to it, the heap cell is what the block carries (the WAM's `put_unsafe_value`) -- so the arm never declines on "an unbound cell of this frame". The row's DONE-WHEN: lv/2 at 1,000,000 deep at the shipped 4 MB in both modes.
- **(d) a CUT or commit in A's caller that discards past A's choice point** -- THE DISCARD LAW (the emission page § 1): rsp drops to the caller's `E_cont`, B drops to the caller's B0 of that construct, the trail is TIDIED (§ 2), and A's frame and records below rsp are dead residue the sweep reads as typed cells (ARCH-GC § 13.5). No release code runs for A. This is safe only with § 2 and § 3: nothing older than A points into A's frame (binding direction) and no trail entry does (tidying).
- **(e) program exit.**

## 2. The trail: conditional, and TIDIED at every discard

Trailing stays CONDITIONAL as `PL_U_TRAIL` has it (the cfo's (B)): no entry when no choice point is live; a stack cell is trailed only when it is OLDER than the current choice point's stack bound, a heap cell only when it is below the choice point's heap mark HB. THE RULE THIS PAGE ADDS: a cut, and every commit that discards choice points (the if-then-else condition's commit UNMARK, once/1, the committing negation), TIDIES THE TRAIL -- the segment above the surviving choice point's trail mark is compacted to the entries whose cell is older than that choice point's bounds (the WAM's `tidy_trail`); r12 moves down by the entries removed. MEASURED without it (the cfo): `loop(N) :- member(_, [a, b]), !, N1 is N - 1, loop(N1).` leaves EXACTLY three entries a cut that no older choice point will ever unwind (6,000,003 at N = 2,000,000), every collection scans them as roots (`rt_gc_trail_roots_run`: 1M 1.3 s, 5M 32.3 s, 626 MB), and 6M cuts REFUSE rc 2 at 16,777,215 entries where swipl answers in 0.96 s at 8 MB; the same leak through `( member -> true ; true )`, once/1 over a two-clause predicate, call/1 then `!`; none through `\+ \+`. WHY IT IS A LIFETIME RULE and not a speed row: an entry for a cell of a frame released under § 1 (c) or (d) is a pointer into dead stack; an untrail into an address a later frame reuses corrupts a live cell; conditional trailing alone does not prevent it, because the entry was conditional on a choice point that the cut removed. The trail is Lon's r12 island (RULES.md § NO GLOBAL HOLDS A STACK) and stays one: its entries are LIFO with choice points, and tidying is a compaction of the top segment, not a second stack. DONE-WHEN: the 6,000,000-cut witness runs at the shipped trail in both modes and the trail top at exit reads the entry count of the live choice points alone.

## 3. Binding direction: one rule, the asm leaf's

Two unbound cells unify by binding the YOUNGER to the OLDER: a stack cell to a heap cell; a deeper (younger) stack cell to a shallower (older) one; of two heap cells the higher address to the lower. `rtx_plunify.s` orders by address and is ruled CORRECT (the cfo's (C): nine witness shapes agree with swipl in both modes). `plc_unify_leaf` in unification.c instead ALLOCATES a fresh heap cell and binds both to it: WRONG under the rule -- it allocates where nothing escapes (THE LIFETIME RULE) and makes a heap cell the representative where the older stack cell should be; it follows the asm rule in the Prolog switch and allocates nothing. With § 1 (c)'s globalization and this rule, no cell older than a frame ever points into that frame, which is what makes § 1 (c) and (d) release frames without a scan.

## 4. The allocator at the hard cap

The collector never runs inside the allocator; a poll at the return of an allocating call collects (ARCH-GC § 7); a request that fits only after a collection is served at the cap (§ 13.12). The cfo's (D): a live list in constant stack, `mk(N, A, L) :- N1 is N - 1, mk(N1, [N | A], L).`, runs at 2,500,000 and at 5,000,000 aborts at the shipped 128 MB after 19 collections with 2,794,632 blocks live -- a capacity verdict by the law's own test (collections ran), the list cell costing 48 bytes (two DESCRs and a header) where swipl's costs 24; `-d512m` runs it; garbage churn (50 × a 1,000,000-element numlist) stays at 129 MB with and without an outer choice point, so the collector reclaims under the cap, and the retained frames below the caller (two nested member/2 generators, a generator under findall, an if-then-else over between/3, garbage forced between the call and the retry) read swipl's exact answer in both modes at GC stress 0, 1 and 7 with grew = 0. THE RULE: ONE ROAD AND ONE ERROR AT THE CAP. Today the emitted allocation is FATAL (`[ZHP] heap exhausted AT THE HARD CAP`, then `error 204: memory overflow`, rc 1, uncatchable by catch/3) while the C builtin `numlist/3` raises a CATCHABLE `error(resource_error(stack), _)` that is really the heap (`-d512m` cures it, `-s64m` does not); swipl raises a catchable resource error on both. The emitted road raises the SAME catchable error term the C road raises, and that term is the one swipl prints on the oracle run of the witness, graded against the ref -- never a FATAL with rc 1, never a stack error for a heap cap. DONE-WHEN: catch/3 around mk/3 at 5,000,000 at the shipped cap catches it in both modes with swipl's term. The 48-byte list cell is a speed row for later, not a lifetime rule.

(b) bagof/setof's sort copy into an n-cell C VLA is one runtime call's own work, so it lives on the STACK by THE LIFETIME RULE (Lon 2026-09-28: a construct's own data is on the stack; a program that runs out of stack declares a bigger -s, never moves to the heap to save stack): the copy stays, and a program with a million solutions declares its -s on its attribute row (hard-cap clause 8 (g)), with the two readings that justify the number.

## 7. What this page must not let anyone get wrong

A frame is never moved to the heap to make a release easy (R2 is withdrawn; the lifetime rule); a program that runs out of stack gets a bigger declared `-s`, never a different placement. A retry never loads rsp (CEO-1755/1759); the release at (c) and (d) is a discard and the resume at a choice record is a balanced pop with rbp from the record. The trail and the choice points stay the two r12/r13 structures Lon kept; tidying does not add a third. No per-predicate blanket: `RETAINS` is the planner's per-graph fact (pass (i.a)) with the cfo's five classes folded in, and a seat that reads "the predicate has more than one clause" as a placement proxy is reading CEO-1750's forbidden proxy.
