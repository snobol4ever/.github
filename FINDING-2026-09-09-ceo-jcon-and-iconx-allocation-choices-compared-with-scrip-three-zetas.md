# Jcon and iconx allocation choices compared with SCRIP's three zetas

Date 2026-09-09 (box clock 09:54 CDT at the measurement). Author: ceo, via a read-only research agent.
Trees: SCRIP `f046ef00b`, corpus `b191d461f`, .github `ee41ecfb`. Sources read: `SCRIP/refs/jcon-master` (89 vendored `.java` files under `jcon/`, the translator under `tran/`, the design notes under `html/`), `SCRIP/refs/icon-master/src/runtime/*.r` and `src/h/rstructs.h`, and the SCRIP files named in the task. Every claim below cites file:line; UNVERIFIED marks what the sources did not settle. Nothing was built or run.

## Verdict

All three implementations keep operand results and per-construct working state in a frame-relative slot at a compile-time offset, and all three treat globals and statics as a static area, so SCRIP's ζ-SPINE and ζ-STANDING choices have two independent precedents. Where they differ is the SUSPENDED STATE: Jcon moves a suspending procedure's entire state into a heap closure object at the first call and reaches it by object reference; iconx leaves the procedure frame on its interpreter stack under a generator marker and copies only the intervening region; SCRIP leaves the frame on the machine stack, pinned to RBP, and resumes through a resume record. SCRIP therefore agrees with iconx in kind and is a legitimate LIFO optimisation of what Jcon does on the heap. Both iconx and Jcon DERIVE the need for suspension storage from whether the code can suspend or create (Jcon at translate time, iconx at run time by executing a suspend), never from a shape list, which is exactly what RULES.md:173 now orders for SCRIP. The one divergence that is a present defect, not an optimisation, is co-expressions: iconx copies the creator's arguments AND locals by value into the new co-expression (lmisc.r:44-55, rcoexpr.r:31-52), Jcon hands the body the creator's variable objects by reference (gen_bc.icn:740-741), and SCRIP copies a byte range `[base, base+frame_bytes)` that does not contain the locals at all (rt_coexpr.c:188-196, hq_S's FINDING). Count: 8 rows AGREE (one with a hazard note), 4 rows DIVERGE.

## Divergences: correctness and legitimacy

Row 3a (locals on the frame, Jcon on the heap). Correctness: a frame-resident local is wrong only if a reference to it outlives the frame. Icon lets a variable escape through `return x`, `suspend x`, transmission across `@`, and substring trapped variables of a local string. iconx closes every one of these with `retderef` (rmisc.r:1446-1463), called at `Op_Pret` (interp.r:863), `Op_Psusp` (:745) and in `co_chng` (rcoexpr.r:86-87). Jcon never needs it because the object lives as long as any reference. SCRIP's choice is the iconx choice and is a legitimate optimisation iff the same dereference-on-escape exists; whether it does is UNVERIFIED here. Construct that would go wrong without it: `procedure f() local s; s := "abc"; return s[2] end` (a trapped-variable substring of a dead local).

Row 4a (suspended procedure frame stays on the stack, Jcon on the heap). Correctness: identical results are possible because Icon generators are LIFO by language rule (a suspended generator is resumed or discarded when its bounded expression ends; iconx discards by resetting `rsp` below the `ef_marker` at `Op_Unmark`, interp.r:593-606, and at `Op_Efail` with `gfp == 0`, :907-913). SCRIP's frame retention is the same LIFO discipline without the copy, so it is a legitimate optimisation. The hazard is a leak, not a wrong answer: if a bounded expression's ω does not return RSP to its α value when a callee generator is left suspended, every iteration of the enclosing loop grows the stack. Jcon cannot leak here (the closure is garbage collected); iconx cannot (one `rsp` assignment). SCRIP must prove it (R5).

Row 5 (co-expressions). Three different choices; only SCRIP's gives a wrong answer today. iconx semantics are by value: `Ocreate` copies the values of every argument and local into the refresh block (lmisc.r:44-55) and `co_init` copies them onto the new stack (rcoexpr.r:31-52); a later assignment by the creator is invisible to the body, and `^c` rebuilds from the same refresh block. Jcon passes `this.tmpVarArray` (the array of the creator's `vLocalVar` objects) into the body's closure (gen_bc.icn:740-741), so the body sees the creator's later assignments; whether Jcon documents this as an intentional difference from Icon is UNVERIFIED (no mention in html/diffs.txt). SCRIP intends the iconx snapshot (memcpy, rt_coexpr.c:192) but snapshots `[base, base+frame_bytes)` while params and locals sit below the base (hq_S FINDING lines 8-18 and 63-71); the body then reads heap memory before the block. hq_S's second observation stands on its own: `bb_create.cpp:20` saves `rsp` when `icn_host_pinned()` is false, which is exactly the `flat_gen` case (x86_asm.h:463 excludes `flat_gen` from `icn_host_pinned`), while the body addresses off `rbp`. That is the discriminator hq_S measured: the defect appears only inside a procedure containing `suspend`. This divergence is a hazard, not an optimisation.

Row 7 is not a divergence on the evidence; it is recorded as DIVERGES-pending only because SCRIP's dereference site was not located in this pass.

## The four specific questions

(a) Enumerated shape list or derived? Jcon: DERIVED. `bc_find_save_restore` (gen_bc.icn:911-921) sets the save/restore flag iff the procedure's IR contains an `ir_Succeed` with a resume label or an `ir_Create`; only then is the closure class minted (:1022-1044) and only then are tmps saved at every suspend (:453-460). There is no list of constructs; `every`, alternation, scanning and limitation never change the decision because they compile to labels inside the method (irgen.icn:167-195, :309-333, :49-112). iconx: DERIVED at run time, with no compile-time decision at all. A generator frame comes into existence only when a suspend EXECUTES (`Op_Esusp` interp.r:613, `Op_Psusp` :731, `Op_Lsusp` :655); every expression gets an `ef_marker` dynamically (`Op_Mark` :560). SCRIP: half and half at `f046ef00b`. `flat_gen` is derived from `IR_SUSPEND` presence (emit.cpp:3734 with :3676) and `is_generator` from the body (lower_icon.c:1503), which matches Jcon exactly, but `icn_host_pinned()` (x86_asm.h:463), `emit_match_rbp()`/`emit_defer_rbp()`/`emit_defer_carve_rbp()` (emit.cpp:2260-2273) and the `SCRIP_*` env switches are enumerated shape tests, which RULES.md:173 orders deleted in favour of the two-condition derivation. The two references support the ruling.

(b) How resume reaches a suspended generator's locals. Jcon: a POINTER TO A HEAP OBJECT. The caller holds the `vClosure` in a tmp and calls `Resume()` (bc_gen_ir_ResumeValue gen_bc.icn:551-579); `Resume` reloads every tmp from `tmpArray`, every label from `tmpLabelArray`, every variable reference from `tmpVarArray` (bc_restore_tmps :1261-1275, bc_load_variables :1457-1476), then `tableswitch`es on `PC` (:1372-1395). Jcon reaches the VARIABLES through the same `vLocalVar` objects the creator had; nothing about them was copied. iconx: A SAVED FRAME POINTER INTO A FRAME THAT NEVER MOVED. `gf_marker.gf_pfp` and `gf_argp` (rstructs.h:319-320) are restored into `pfp`/`glbl_argp` at `Op_Efail` (interp.r:925-945); the copied region above the marker is the caller's expression state, not the generator's locals, and it is discarded by `rsp = gfp - 1` (:937). SCRIP: A RETAINED FRAME. The γ exit leaves the frame in place and jumps the γ wire (xa_flat.cpp:438-445); β re-enters with `rbp` restored through the resume record (GOAL-ICON-100.md:27). This is the iconx scheme with the marker replaced by the resume record.

(c) Where co-expression locals live and how the creator's frame is captured. Jcon: the body runs on its own Java thread (vCoexp.java:32, :89-91) inside a fresh closure instance whose `tmpVarArray` IS the creator's array (gen_bc.icn:740-741); tmps are fresh (:1119), `PC` is the body label (:743). No frame is copied; variables are shared by reference; refresh copies values (:1148-1233). iconx: the co-expression owns a malloc'd interpreter stack (ralc.r:126) and a pthread C stack (rswitch.c:91-107); the creator's `pf_marker`, arguments and locals are COPIED BY VALUE into a `b_refresh` block at create (lmisc.r:39-55) and from there onto the new stack (rcoexpr.r:31-52); the body's `Op_Local` then reads `pfp->pf_locals` of the COPIED marker on the new stack (interp.r:269-272), never the creator's. The creator's frame is not referenced at all after create. SCRIP: own pthread (rt_coexpr.c:55-71), snapshot by `memcpy` of `[base, base+frame_bytes)` (:192) anchored at the new thread's `rbp` (:162), with base = `rsp` or `rbp` per `icn_host_pinned()` (bb_create.cpp:20). The iconx answer to hq_S's defect is: copy the whole procedure frame, arguments and locals included, by value, at create, and anchor the body's frame pointer at the copied marker; the extent is `na + sizeof(pf_marker) + nl` descriptors (lmisc.r:22-23), known to the creator at create time.

(d) Jcon argument dereference. Arguments are evaluated into tmps (JVM-locals) by `ir_value` (irgen.icn:374); an `a_Ident` argument is compiled `rval` so the tmp already holds a value, anything else may hold a variable (:376-380). The un-dereferenced descriptor sits in the JVM-local tmp until the callee's prologue: `bc_createvars` (gen_bc.icn:1627-1650) emits `invokevirtual Deref` for a parameter that is never assigned (:1636-1641) and `NewLocal(name, x)` for one that is, which itself calls `x.Deref()` (vLocalVar.java:30-35). For closure procedures the same happens in `bc_createvars_into_array` (:1565-1625) into `tmpVarArray`. So Jcon holds argument temporaries in JVM-locals of the CALLER and dereferences in the CALLEE prologue; iconx holds them on the istack and dereferences in `invoke` before the frame is built (invoke.r:67-74).

## Recommendations for SCRIP, written as assertions a seat can test

R1 (row 5). `scrip_coexpr_create` copies the creator's parameters and locals. Test: hq_S's `w5.icn` prints `local=integer/3` under SCRIP m3 and m4, byte-identical to `iconx`. The copied range is `[base - below, base + above)` and the body is anchored at `cp + below`, where `below` covers `(nparams + nlocals) * 16` (x86_asm.h:834) plus whatever the frame layout puts under the base.

R2 (row 5). The base register `bb_create.cpp:20` saves is the register the body addresses off. Test: with `SCRIP_FLOOR_DIAG` or a `--compile` diff, a `create` inside a procedure containing `suspend` emits `mov qword ptr [...+56], rbp` (not `rsp`) whenever the body's variable reads are `[rbp + ...]`. Under RULES.md:173 this collapses to: the co-expression base is the frame base the derivation chose for the host graph, read from one selector.

R3 (row 5). Co-expression locals are captured BY VALUE at create (iconx lmisc.r:44-55). Test: `procedure p() local x; x := 1; c := create x; x := 2; write(@c) end` prints whatever `iconx` prints (the oracle decides; iconx's code says 1). Do not adopt Jcon's by-reference sharing without an oracle reading that says Icon does so.

R4 (row 3a). A variable reference that leaves its frame through `return`, `suspend` or `@` is dereferenced at the boundary when it points into the dying frame, and only then (iconx `retderef` rmisc.r:1446-1463; called at interp.r:745, :863 and rcoexpr.r:86-87). Test: `procedure f() local s; s := "abc"; return s[2] end; write(f())` prints `b` in both modes, and `procedure g() local x; x := 5; return x end; g() := 7` produces the same error number as `iconx`.

R5 (row 4a). A bounded expression that leaves a callee generator suspended releases its frame at ω. Test: RSP measured at the α of `every i := 1 to 1000000 do { f() }` where `f` suspends once and is never resumed equals RSP measured after the loop, and the program finishes without ERROR 246 (rt_stack_overflow.c); iconx's equivalent is `Op_Unmark` interp.r:593-606.

R6 (rows 1, 2, 4a, 6). The tier of every cell is derived from the two conditions and from callee ownership, never from a construct name (RULES.md:173; Jcon precedent gen_bc.icn:911-921; iconx precedent interp.r:613/:731). Test: the census gate named in RULES.md:173 reads zero `getenv("SCRIP_*")` names matching `RBP|FRAME|PIN|ZFRAME|CARVE` under `src/`, and `icn_host_pinned()`'s shape test at x86_asm.h:463 no longer exists; the Icon master and SnoM read FAIL=0 over the printed denominator in both modes on the same tree.

R7 (row 7). Every call evaluates its arguments to descriptors and dereferences them at the call boundary, except for built-ins that take variables (iconx `nstatic < 0`, invoke.r:71-74). Test: `procedure f(a) a := 2 end; y := 1; f(y); write(y)` prints 1 in both modes; the Icon lane names the SCRIP site (`lower_icon.c` or the `bb_call_*` template) that performs the dereference, closing the UNVERIFIED cell in row 7.

R8 (row 4b). Generator EXPRESSIONS never own a frame of their own; their state is cells of the enclosing activation (Jcon irgen.icn:167-195; iconx interp.r:613-653). Test: `--dump-zeta` for `every write(1 to 3 | 7)` inside a non-suspending procedure shows every cell on the RSP spine and no `rbp` in the emitted `.s` of that procedure.
