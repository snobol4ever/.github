## 1. ⛔ THE LAW: § 6's universal ω is a run-time-selected continuation at every fail port, and the standing law admits exactly one such construct

`ARCH-ENGINE.md` § STATIC WIRING ONLY, UNLESS IT IS AN IF-TYPE CONSTRUCT (Lon 2026-09-13, verbatim *"We use static wiring only, unless it is an if-type construct. So fix that."*, CEO-694): ports are wired at compile time into straight-line jumps; THE ONE EXCEPTION, NAMED, is the Proebsting `ifstmt.gate` shape read by `IR_INDIRECT_GOTO`. `mov rax,[B] ; jmp [rax+RETRY]` at every ω is a continuation selected at run time through a banked slot, and a choice record is not an if-type construct. **Q6 is therefore a change of law, not a design preference Lon blesses in passing, and "work proceeds on the recommendation" does not apply to it.** My reading as the spine's reviewer, for the ceo to carry with the question: the mechanism is sound and cheaper than the β chain it replaces, and the page's strongest argument is one it does not make explicitly — a called predicate box ALREADY receives its γ and ω as run-time values (`rcx` and `rdx` at α, `[H+48]` in the frame), because one predicate is entered from many sites, so the universal ω replaces one banked landing with another rather than introducing run-time selection where there was none. Only Lon moves the law. Until he does, R5 and the retirement of `bb_disjunction`/`bb_bound`/`bb_to`'s pinned arms are parked, and nothing in R1–R4/R7 waits.

## 2. ⛔ DEFECT, § 7.3 nondeterministic γ: `rsp := rbp` discards a younger retained callee

When a box exits with `B ≠ F.B0` because a callee in its own body left a choice, the youngest record lives in that callee's frame BELOW the box's `rbp`. `rsp := rbp` releases it, and the continuation's next carve overwrites the record `B` names; the next failure jumps through garbage. The release at a nondeterministic γ is **`rsp := [B+SP]`** — the youngest record's `SP` — one load: it keeps every frame a live record protects and releases everything carved after the youngest choice. (The deterministic γ's `lea rsp,[rbp+kt+16n]` is right: `B == F.B0` proves no younger record exists.)

## 3. ⛔ CONTRADICTION, § 7.5 / § 8.3 against § 8 write mode: the head allocates

§ 7.5: *the clause's one allocation is the neck carve, so the poll is the carve's slow arm and nowhere else*; § 8.3: *no safe point falls inside a head*. But § 8's `U_STRUCT` write mode *allocates a k-cell block* DURING the head (the subject was unbound; the structure must exist before the head binds the cell to it), before the neck. Two honest resolutions, and the page must pick one:
- (i) **the clause's carve moves to the clause's α, before the head, sized for the worst case** (every head structure's write-mode block included); a read-mode call wastes those blocks (nrev's `app/3` third argument is write mode on every call, but `member/2`-shaped heads are read mode), which is named and measured as the cost of (i), and reclaimed by R10's frontier reset on backtracking. One allocation per clause, one poll, the head genuinely poll-free.
- (ii) the head carries a second allocating site with its own slow arm — and at THAT poll `r13` (a heap block base inside a nested structure), the `(r13, rsi, r14, r15)` quads the general-unify leaf pushes on the spine, and the half-built block are raw words on the spine under a poll: the class of defect the cto's open arm-7 row is about (untagged heap words on the spine below a poll), and what THE COLLECTOR GUESSES NOTHING forbids. Also: a slow arm that sets `g_gc_pending` with no poll of its own, followed by a neck carve whose fast path succeeds, is the MISSING SAFE POINT BETWEEN TWO ALLOCATIONS the exhaustion message names in second place.
**Recommended: (i).** Whichever is taken, *no safe point inside a head* is a claim spanning two sites (the emitter and every head node) and must be held by a gate clause in the contract gate's shape (`test_gate_gc_the_emitted_safe_point_sequence_matches_its_declared_contract.sh` already walks every poll and could refuse one between a clause's α and its neck), never by prose. The same clause holds § 7.4's window between overwriting the caller's header with arguments and the callee's α storing its own `DT_MAP` cell.

## 4. § 7.1 against § 7.5: which locals are zeroed

§ 7.1 zeroes *only the locals a γ→β window can observe*; § 7.5 says at the poll *the frame is fully typed (the locals zeroed)*. Under one static map per activation (the keystone row: maps are keyed by graph, not by safe point), every mapped DESCR slot must hold a DESCR at every poll. The body-only `F.X` slots are first written by the carve's stores, which run AFTER the slow arm's poll, so at that poll they hold stack garbage unless zeroed at α. § 7.5's statement is the correct one: every mapped DESCR slot is zeroed at α (`rep stosq`, sized at compile time); correct § 7.1.

## 5. § 5 the guard-page push: the handler claim is false today, and the fixed arena is a CEO-1210 limit

*"the SIGSEGV handler rt_stack_overflow.c already installs unconditionally reports trail overflow with the named limit at rc=2"* — checked: `src/runtime/rt/rt_stack_overflow.c` (80 lines) names a stack overflow only when the fault lies within 16 MB below the thread stack's low bound with `rsp` beside it (line 25), and otherwise re-raises `SIG_DFL`; it knows nothing of the trail arena. A trail guard-page fault today is a bare SIGSEGV with no name. The overflow test the push drops lives in C today (`rt_pl_trail.c:31` prints the REFUSE naming `PL_TR_ARENA_LG2` and the MB). Dropping it is fine ONLY WITH the handler taught the arena's range in the same landing (R4), keeping the refusal that names the limit and the switch that raises it. Further: `PL_TR_ARENA_LG2 = 25` (`rt_pl_trail.h:5`) is a fixed 32 MB arena, a limit a program reaches (deep nondeterministic recursion with conditional bindings), and NO FIXED LIMIT A PROGRAM CAN REACH (Lon 2026-09-23, CEO-1210) makes its growth a row owed beside R4, not *"a later rung"*. Because the design masks `r12` to reach the arena header (`X86_PL_TR_ARENA_MASK`, landed at R0), growth is either an `mremap` to a size-aligned address or a chunked trail whose header lives elsewhere — the page should say which before R4 builds on the mask. Q3 stands for Lon as written.

## 6. § 3 `DT_PLATOM = 0xB0`: safe, but not for the reason the page gives
