# FINDING 2026-09-12 (cto, CEO-617 lane: THE PROLOG ISO LADDER, rung 5) — a run-time goal ran its left conjunct before the callable check, findall ran its goal before checking its result argument, and a declared empty predicate raised existence_error

**Claim.** After SCRIP `43638b186`, ISO 7.6.2 body conversion holds for a goal reaching `call/1` however it got there: `call((fail, 1))` raises `type_error(callable, (fail,1))` without running `fail`, `call((write(3), 1))` raises without printing, `call((1 ; true))` raises instead of succeeding, and `call((fail, _))` fails (a nested unbound leaf becomes `call(V)`, not an error). `findall/3`, `bagof/3`, `setof/3` check their Instances argument BEFORE running the goal (`type_error(list, 12)`, `type_error(list, [a|b])`, ISO 8.10.1.3). A predicate declared `dynamic`, `multifile` or `discontiguous` with no clauses is DEFINED: calling it fails, never `existence_error`. Logtalk `call_1` 16→20/20 and `findall_3` 12→14/14 in both modes.

**How it was found.** CTO-32's NEXT named the three families from the Logtalk per-case reds: `iso_call_1_13` read `fail` (the `,`/2 wrapper ran `fail` first), `iso_call_1_15` read `succ` (the disjunction never looked at its number leaf), `lgt_call_1_17/18` read `existence_error(procedure, fenix/1)` / `scattered/2`, `sics_findall_3_09` and `eclipse_findall_3_10` read `fail` (the result unification failed after the goal had run). A probe against swipl confirmed the shapes in both modes.

**Where it was.** Three sites. (1) `rt_pl_goal_gen_h_c` / `rt_pl_goal_spine_prep` (`by_name_dispatch.c`) dispatched a run-time goal keyed `,/2`, `;/2`, `->/2` straight to the rung-2 control-construct wrapper, which executes its left operand before anything looks at the right one; and the lowerer's `call/1` of a compile-time control construct with a variable leaf (`call((fail, One))`) inlined the body with no conversion check on the whole term. (2) The findall lowering (`lower_prolog.c`) unified the collected list with Instances at the end, so a non-list Instances was a quiet failure after the goal's side effects. (3) `pl_runtime_define_goal_wrapper` redefined a wrapper whose body fell back to a run-time meta-call as the `existence_error` thrower unconditionally, though the rung-3 key registry already knew the multifile/discontiguous declaration (`$db_decl` kind 2, `e->decl`).

**The cure.** No new box, no interpreter. (1) `pl_goal_conv_scan` treats an unbound leaf at depth > 0 as convertible (7.6.2), and the two resolver entries run `rt_pl_dop_goal_guard_c` on a goal whose key is a control construct before dispatch, returning the ball through the existing slot; the lowerer emits `$pl_goal_guard(WholeTerm)` ahead of the inlined body when a `call/1` argument is a control construct holding a variable leaf (`pl_tree_control_var_leaf`). (2) A new leaf `$pl_list_guard/1` (`rt_pl_dop_list_guard_c`, veneer with `MOD_OP_RT_PL_LIST_GUARD` 226) walks the Instances spine and raises `type_error(list, Instances)` unless it ends in `[]` or a variable; both findall lowerings (with and without free variables) enter through it. (3) A new root leaf `$pl_declared/2` (`PL_ROOT_LEAF(pl_declared)`, `rt_pl_db_key_is_declared` in `unification.c`) answers the registry, and the fallen-back wrapper's body is now `( '$pl_declared'(Name, Arity) -> fail ; throw(error(existence_error(procedure, Name/Arity), Name/Arity)) )`, so the same wrapper serves both `call/1` and a direct body call of the declared name; `unknown=fail` keeps its `fail` body.
