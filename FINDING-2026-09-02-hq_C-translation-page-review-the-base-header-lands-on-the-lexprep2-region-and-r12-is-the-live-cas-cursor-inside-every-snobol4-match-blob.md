## C1 (§ A.1) — the header cannot sit at `[rbp+0..48)`: that region is zeroed by `rt_jmp_frame_lexprep2` on EVERY β re-entry and read as "already suspended" by `bb_suspend`

Today that region is:

- **zeroed whole on every β re-entry**: `rt_jmp_frame_lexprep2(fb, …, region_bytes)` does `memset(fb, 0, region_bytes)` and then writes the pending trail-mark pair at `fb+0` / `fb+8` (`rt.c`). The page knows the pair exists (*"the same reason today's `[fb+0]/[fb+8]` 'already suspended' pair is base-relative"*) and then places `F.B`/`F.B0` on top of it without saying the pair goes, and without noticing the memset.

**Recommended correction (a pure move, no rebase):** put the header at the TOP, beside the wire header that already lives there — `[kt-32]` is the free word today (the dc pad; the PZ-4 baton's NINETEENTH-pass block already banks the yield-time rsp there), `[kt-24]=γ`, `[kt-16]=ω`, `[kt-8]=caller base`. ## C9 (§ B.10, § A "EXCEPTION" row) — a ball that rides the ω→β chain re-enters every RETAINED callee as a REDO; the throw must be tested at every β re-entry of a retained frame, not only at catch boxes (Kulaš & Beierle 2000 § 3.9; SWI's exception port; Tarau § 7.5)

SCRIP chains backward by wiring box N+1's ω to box N's β. With the ball on r15, `throw/1` in box N+1 takes ω and LANDS ON BOX N's β. If box N is a leaf, β trampolines to ω and the ball propagates — the page's "every intermediate box's ω passes r15 untouched" holds. If box N is a call box with a RETAINED callee frame (the normal case after PZ-4), its β arm re-enters the callee at `F.RES` to compute the NEXT SOLUTION — the exception is silently converted into backtracking, and the catch box, which sits to the LEFT, is reached only after every retained sibling has been exhausted, if at all. The page's `H.β → Goal.β` for the catch box itself has the same hole after `H.γ`: a throw from a goal to the right of an exited `catch/3` lands on `H.β` and would redo `Goal` instead of passing through (ISO: catch/3 is transparent once its Goal has exited).

The literature pins the wiring. Kulaš & Beierle 2000 (`2000-kulas-beierle-rewriting-semantics-WRLA.txt`, **§ 3.9 "Catch and throw"**, txt lines 398-422): `throw(B)` rewrites to `ThrowAnc`, which walks the chain of PARENTS by the suffix criterion of § 3.8 — every continuation that is a younger sibling on the way is EMPTIED with undo (`u ThrowAnc(T,X)`), never re-entered; the first parent of the form `catch(G,B',R)` still executing `G` whose `B'` unifies with a fresh copy of `B` runs `R` as a fresh derivation with the unifier, its own continuation emptied (`C∅`); no such parent → `Error`. SWI's port description (`manual-swi-byrd-box-model.txt`, the `exception` port: *"activated on each parent frame … until the exception is caught"*) is the same walk over frames. Tarau (`2011-tarau-binprolog-continuation-passing.txt` **§ 7.5 "Implementing exceptions"**, txt line 922): `throw(E) :- return(exception(E))` — the engine returns a distinguished ANSWER PATTERN, and `catch/3` is the only consumer that inspects it; every other consumer of an engine answer never sees a redo.

**Correction:** the β arm of every box that can hold a retained callee (`bb_call_proc_staged` β, the future clause-step β of § B.3, and the catch box's own β) opens with `test r15,r15; jnz <own ω>` — one instruction pair per retained-frame re-entry, none on leaves — so a ball walks PARENTS only, dropping each retained sibling's frame (undo its `F.TRMARK` range, release) as it passes, exactly Kulaš's `u ThrowAnc`. The catch box's β-handler is then the ONLY β that reads r15 for a value. With that pair in place the page's "propagates frame by frame" is literally true; without it the trace instrument of rung 1 would show `Redo` lines during a throw, which is the cheap way to test it (BX-0 witness: `catch((member(X,[1,2,3]), X > 1, throw(t)), t, true)` must print no `Redo: member` after the `Exception`).

**Cut, cited per port (§ B.6 stands, now sourced):** Kulaš & Beierle **§ 3.8 "Cut"** (txt 345-397): the cut's parent is found by the suffix criterion — the leftmost continuation whose argument does not end in the current goal's tail — and every continuation underway up to and including the parent's is emptied. In frame terms the parent IS the enclosing predicate call box and "emptying up to the parent" IS `B := F.B0` with every younger frame released: the page's `K.α` is Kulaš's rule set with `F.B0` as the parent mark, and the compile-time rewiring of every ω right of the cut to `cut_ω` (`lower_prolog.c`, the `cx->cut_ω` arm) is the `Return` marker resolved statically. Aït-Kaci 1991 § 5.11 (`get_level`/`neck_cut`/`cut`, the B register saved at clause entry) is the WAM form of the same two lines. Jahier 1999 **§ 3** (`1999-jahier-…LPE99.txt` line 193, Equations 6'/7'): the cut's box has `call` and `exit` events and no `redo`/`fail` of its own — matching `bb_cut` (α, γ, β-trampoline) today.
