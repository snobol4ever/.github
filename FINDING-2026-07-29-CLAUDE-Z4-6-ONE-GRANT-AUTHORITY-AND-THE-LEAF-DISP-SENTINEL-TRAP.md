## 3. ⭐ THE SENTINEL TRAP — fc_leaf_disp is DELIBERATELY UNGATED

Root cause is a one-character-class bug in MY gate, and a documentation trap for the next walker: **`fc_leaf_disp`'s true unregistered sentinel is `(int)0x80000000` — the consumer at `emit.cpp:1029` tests exactly that — while the extern declaration's prose says "-1 = unregistered".** My gate's `-1` was consumed as a REGISTERED displacement of minus one byte; every flat reference under the rsp arm went odd. Registration-time coherence beats query-time gating for derived tables. Lesson generalized: **when gating a family, distinguish AUTHORITIES (side-table opinions — gate them) from DERIVATIONS (computed from authorities — leave them; they follow).**
