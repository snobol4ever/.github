## LAW — UNION-TAG (IR.h line 223)

`IR_t` carries `union { const char *sval; int64_t ival; double dval; }`.  **sval and ival ALIAS.**  Slice 1's producer wrote `ival = 0; sval = ptr` — the ival store was DEAD, the role read back as the string pointer, and role-0 discrimination worked only because a heap pointer is never 1, 2, or 3 and fell past the role checks into the role-0 body.
