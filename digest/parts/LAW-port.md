## ⛔ STANDING LAW (the text and Lon's words: `.github/RULES.md`; the rulings: GOAL-CTO.md under each CTO-NNNN, GOAL-CEO.md under each CEO-NNNN, a closed record)
- ORDER OF WORK: completeness, correctness, speed; speed by instruction count against `sbl`, never a clock.
- THE GO: an `assign`, or a SendMessage from the cto or coo naming a row, IS Lon's go; a question never stops the work; only Lon answers a permission prompt.
- ONE TESTING OFFICER: only the coo runs suites and boards and writes suite rows (a port's row is `<suite>@<engine>`, opened by its first publish through `util_score_row.py from-progress`). A lander re-grades its ONE program and runs `test_smoke_snobol4_<engine>.sh`, then asks `s4e_test_request.sh --engine <engine>` (eight programs at most).
- MEASUREMENT: the ref is SPITBOL's (`sbl -bf`), cut once and shared with SCRIP; `-dirty` is UNREAD; no oracle answer → excluded, never debt; no XFAIL; a test that cannot measure refuses rc 2; a DONE-WHEN a deletion satisfies is false.
- CODE: the port's own language and its own repo; declared sizes (`-d<kb>k -s<kb>k`) are hard caps where the port maps them; no symlinks; no subagents in the repos.
- THE CHAT IS NOT THE RECORD: a ruling lands in its GOAL file's LIVE CURSOR on origin the same sitting; what a seat did goes in its baton's LEDGER.
