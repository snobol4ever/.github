2. MODE: `head -2 /home/resources/postoffice/MODE` and its keyed lines; the seats: `ps -eo pid,etime,args | grep 'claude --name'` and ListAgents.
3. THE HEALTH CHECK, every turn, unasked: `python3 .github/scripts/util_suite_banner.py --plain`; `python3 harness/scripts/util_seat_census.py --health`; `python3 harness/scripts/util_queue_visibility_census.py`; `df -h /tmp /home`; `util_round_robin_freshness.py --loop-dir /home/claude_coo/.scratch/verifier`; the tail of `/home/resources/progress/SMOKE.tsv`; `uptime` against 16 cores. ⛔ A red is acted on in the same turn: a seat over its scope is throttled or capped by SendMessage; what needs Lon's permission goes to him with the exact command; an oom in a seat scope or `/tmp` past its bound is an alarm to Lon.
4. Inbox: `bash harness/scripts/s4e_msg.sh check` (long output into a file, read whole); every `s4e_test_request.sh` answered by telegram, one line per test; design and ruling asks to the cto; then `clear`.
5. THE LOOP: exactly ONE top-level loop8 (its parent is not `bash loop8.sh`), tier1 and seatwatch alive; a restart is STOP, wait on the top-level pid, confirm none remains, one `setsid nohup env VARS bash loop8.sh`. Mode 3 is graded first (CTO-475); a program passes only when both modes pass.
6. THE QUEUE: a Lon word becomes a row per seat (its witness the DONE-WHEN), assigned the same sitting; every new red a row assigned to its fixer with a SendMessage naming it; every landed fix graded and its suite row written (`S4E_SCORE_WRITE="why" util_score_row.py from-progress --suite <key>`); about three closed rows audited on origin; `util_queue_zero_base.py --apply`; stale claims released with a reason.
7. THE WAKE RULE: a seat with no live row and an idle session is given work FIRST (its red found, minted, assigned) and THEN woken by one SendMessage naming the row; a seat that finished and holds nothing is left to Lon. Hand off with `bash harness/scripts/handoff_status.sh` pasted verbatim; your own /loop and Monitor armed the whole sitting.
