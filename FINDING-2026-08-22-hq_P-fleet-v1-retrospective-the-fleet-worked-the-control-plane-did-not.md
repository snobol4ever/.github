## WHAT WENT WRONG — SEVEN MECHANISMS, ALL MEASURED

1. **DISPATCH WAS A RACE, NOT AN ASSIGNMENT.** HQ mails "your row is X"; the seat runs `next`, which locks whatever is topmost-free — nothing binds the mail to the lock. Four messages record the race firing (`seat13-q-row-mismatch-json-alternate`, `seat13-q-next-locked-diag-reg-stmtno-not-json`, `seat16-seat16-row-conflict` — seat16's auto-lock on `ptx-shift-m4` was manufactured BY the race and then abandoned in place). seat13 lost its whole session to this plus a dead row (mechanism 5).
2. **THE PICKER IGNORED RANK.** `s4e_msg.sh next` serves FILE order; rank is printed, never sorted on. Measured now: **all 22 free rank-0 rows sit buried behind 30 free rank-4/5/6/8/99 rows**, and the deliberately-fenced rank-99 M1 gate row sits AHEAD of them and unclaimed — the one row HQ fenced off is the 26th row the picker will serve. The +38 rows minted in the final 4 hours were appended to the BOTTOM of a top-served file. HQ was steering with a disconnected wheel: **"no one in control" was literally true of the priority column.**
3. **THE QUEUE WAS A MEMORY, NOT A BUFFER.** 181 rows, **62% dead** (112 DONE rows still present; s191 measured 45/68 dead, ordered pruning, it got WORSE); median row 1,457 bytes of prose, max 5,965; one blank line at exactly the point the rank-0 backlog begins (a parser-truncation landmine); 6.2x growth in 3 days. Nobody — HQ included — could read it, so HQ re-dispatched already-landed work (`diag-reg-stmtno`, `diag-reg-nodeid`: both landed by seat8, both re-minted, one actively locked by seat13 at post-mortem time).
4. **RECEPTION STARVED — ALL ROADS LED TO ONE UNREAD INBOX.** Every seat message addresses `TO hq`; hq stopped consuming at 21:01 UTC and **29 messages accumulated in 1h47m, 15 of them questions, zero answered** — seat13's five waited 45m–1h51m while it landed NOTHING. Splitting HQ added no inbound capacity: hq_C received 1 message (its own brief), hq_P 0, and the fleet-census glob (`/home/claude[0-9][0-9]`) **matches neither `/home/claude_C` nor `/home/claude_P`** — the split HQs are invisible to the fleet's own health screen. A starved seat is a paid session producing zero.
5. **CORRECTIONS HAD NO DURABLE HOME.** The s255 `free-r11` census correction was applied by editing the queue row IN PLACE — the holder and every seat carrying the stale number were never told. HQ diagnosed seat01's broken mailbox and **mailed the diagnosis into the broken mailbox** (`claude01/inbox/…URGENT-your-seat-id-is-broken.msg`, unread; a `STOP CHASING MY PRIME SUSPECT` correction marooned beside it — seat01 duplicated a full pristine-build bisect "unaware of your lead"). One seat→seat message rotted 46h as an undelivered `.msg.*` temp file. And the formal LAW-16 override channel, minted for exactly this, **was never used once** — corrections flowed as contradictions.
6. **VERDICTS WERE PROSE, AND PROSE LIES.** The `free-r10`/`free-r11` inversion: HQ read "free" as *released*, seats read *emptied*; HQ ruled both DONE and contradicted seat03, seat06, seat08 simultaneously — all three right (`0c542020`). Two defective DONE-WHENs in one s191 session (one satisfiable by a WRONG fix, one unsatisfiable by a CORRECT one). seat16's gate audit: **31 of 105 gates cannot say no** (most `exit 0` without an undocumented `--strict` nothing passes). The banner's SUCCESS was passable by doing nothing until s255 computed it. Every subsystem that moved from prose to computation stopped lying (banner, MEDIUM ratchet, handoff_status); every one that stayed prose kept lying.
7. **IDENTITY BY FRAGILE GLOB, RE-BROKEN AT EVERY GROWTH STEP.** 4→8: fleet screen hardcoded 8. →12: `/home/claude10` glob-parsed to identity `claude10`, outside the namespace. s255 zero-pad rename: forked `claude01/` as a phantom mailbox and broke FINDING attribution for seats 1–9 (padded ids grepping unpadded filenames). The 8-seat noise floor kept grading 12- and 16-seat runs — at 16 seats **33% of TIME rows came back unmeasurable**, and the box's own load corrupted wall-clock into a 2x-swinging non-instrument (why hq_P's law is callgrind Ir at fixed work). PROTOCOL.md still says "01..12" while seat13–16 work.

## THE ONE-SENTENCE DIAGNOSIS

**State lived in the wrong places: task state lived in dying seat contexts and in-place-edited queue rows, verdict state lived in hand-typed prose, priority state lived in a column the picker never read, and correction state lived in mail with no bound reader — so the fleet's real work (which git merged flawlessly) was steered by a control plane where no signal was load-bearing.** It was not Opus's feeble mind, or not mainly: Opus-HQ made prose-verdict errors (mechanism 6), but mechanisms 1–5 and 7 would have degraded a fleet of any intelligence — they are races, unsorted scans, unbounded buffers, and unread mailboxes. The 27 protocol patches of the era each hardened one fracture after it broke; v2 (`ARCH-FLEET-CEO.md`) is those patches' logic completed structurally.
