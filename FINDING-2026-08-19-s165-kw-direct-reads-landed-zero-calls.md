### ⛔ RECOMMENDATION TO HQ/Lon: KW-2's "emitted `.data` block + `rt_kw_bind`" is NOT worth building, and here is the measurement
Line 510 asks for two things — *"place all the keywords as statics in the emitted asm"* AND *"use direct references."* **KW-D delivered the second without needing the first**, and the first now looks actively harmful:
1. **It conflicts with KW-2's own measured one-authority decision.** The block deliberately homes at *the consumer's* cell (block comment, lines 93–97: this is what retired the s146 §6 spelled-twice disease). Census of C consumers outside `keywords.c`: `g_anchor`→`bb_match_begin.cpp` (**the hot pattern path, which RC-5 further optimized into register r8**) · `kw_trim`/`kw_fullscan`/`kw_stlimit`→`core.c` (input, scanner, statement limiter) · `kw_stlimit`→`sync_monitor.c` · `g_maxlngth`→`driver/scrip.c` (the `-m` flag) · `g_trace`→`gen_runtime.c`. Relocating storage into the emitted program forces every one of those to indirect through the bound pointer — **including the anchor read RC-5 spent a rung removing an indirection from.**
2. **Its stated payoff does not exist.** The reason to want program-owned storage is CVA-style `mprotect(PROT_READ)` sealing (line 516, "hardware is the auditor of last resort"). But sealing buys nothing for keywords: of the 8 protected entries, `&ALPHABET/&UCASE/&LCASE` are STR constants (already effectively immutable) and `&STCOUNT/&STNO/&LASTNO/&FNCLEVEL/&RTNTYPE` are **runtime-maintained counters the runtime must keep writing** — they cannot live on a read-only page. Page sealing is a *user-constant* (write-once) mechanism, which is CN-4's CVA, not a keyword one.
3. **A `mov` bypass is a keyword non-goal anyway.** Every write must stay validated (208/210/287/209 + KW-5), so the block would buy no write-side speed either.
**Proposal:** mark KW-2's emit+bind half **RETIRED — SUPERSEDED BY KW-D**, and record that keyword protection is *route-enforced* (one door: `kwb_write_ent`) rather than page-enforced, with the `.&KW` indirect door (Addendum 1) as the one seam that must be kept routed when it lands. If Lon still wants program-owned keyword storage for a reason not captured here, the `rt_kw_direct_sym` rebind tripwire already makes it safe to add later without staling the direct reads.
