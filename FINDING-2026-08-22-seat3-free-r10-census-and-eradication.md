## 1. THE MECHANISM, ESTABLISHED FIRST

WREG (`scripts/wreg_claim_registry.txt`, `test_gate_wreg_claim.sh`, minted s12 2026-08-10) proposed claiming rΓ=r10/rΩ=r11 as **permanent, product-wide** dynamic wire registers — every glue kind carries γ/ω in them, so a blob needs zero receiving code. Its own claim gate never left `PENDING`: the claim was designed, never made live. Session s195 shipped a **different, superseding** mechanism instead — the caller PUSHes the {γ,ω} pair onto the stack before entry (`bb_glue_pass_wires_blob`/`bb_glue_wire_exit`, `src/templates/bb_glue_flat.cpp`), landing at `[rbp+8]/[rbp+16]` (framed) or `[rsp+0]/[rsp+8]` (frameless). The register-based path was kept as a killswitch fallback (`bb_wire_stack_on()`, default ON since s195) and never cleaned up. That fallback, plus a handful of other now-dead register-wire remnants scattered by the same migration, is what this row eradicates for r10.

## 4. WHAT GENUINELY SURVIVES, AND WHY (class (c), not touched this session except where noted)

1. **RTCC self-restore idiom** (`rt.c`, `runtime_eval.c`, `rtcc_init.c`, 15 occurrences) — every C-boundary crossing that primes the RTCC block loads `rtccb`'s base into r10 then does `movq 56(%r10),%r10`, restoring r10's own RTCC slot last. Wire-unrelated, currently always-zero (nothing writes that slot), but live and correct.
2. **RTCC binary-call-stub target register** (`x86_asm.h`, 4 of its 10 occurrences) — in BINARY medium the veneered indirect call is `movabs r10,ptr; call r10`, so the call mechanism itself clobbers r10; `x86_rtcc_call`/`x86_rtcc_call_descr` account for that with `m |= RTCC_C_R10` under `MEDIUM_BINARY`. **Fully explained and intentional** (commit `41442121`, s65: *"the two media touch different registers and both preserve the same architectural state, which is the 1:1 contract"*) — not a bug, but the register choice is incidental to the mechanism and this is real, testable follow-up work if r10 must reach zero in `x86_asm.h`, not a mechanical rename.
3. ⛔ **Frameless jmp-entry pattern-blob suspend cache** (`emit.cpp:2725`/`:3033`, 7 occurrences with the discovery in §3) — **the highest-risk survivor.** A frameless blob reads its caller-pushed {γ,ω} pair out of `[rsp]` once at entry into r10/r11 so a later suspend point can push them into a resume record without re-touching the stack. Nothing else in the templates enforces that box-body code emitted between entry and suspend avoids clobbering r10/r11 for its own purposes — an IMPLICIT reservation for that span, the same shape `FINDING-2026-08-20-s194b`/`s194c` convicted for the RTCC bank colliding with the old wire convention. Flagged, not fixed: retargeting the suspend path to re-read `[rsp]` directly would remove the last live claim in `src/emitter/` but needs its own probed rung.
4. **x86_asm.h register-name tables** (6 of its 10 occurrences) and **`rtx_abi.inc`'s idiv-scratch/working-set documentation** (2 occurrences, measured UNUSED anywhere in the tree today — aspirational, not live) and **`driver/scrip.c`'s inline-asm clobber list** (1 occurrence) — infrastructure/documentation, not a claim on anything, not movable in any meaningful sense.
