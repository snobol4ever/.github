## THE CLAIM
A non-generator Icon procedure that calls generators is a HOST: its prologue carved, on the machine stack, one region slice per generator call site, each slice sized `align16(own) + 48 + transitive callee slices`, with a 63-slot multiplier wherever the walk met a cycle. On the 17-module jtran that sum is 66,457,856 bytes for `main` and 66,110,496 for `ir_value` / `ir_a_ProcDecl`; `icon_parser` carves 33,210,064 and `icon_recognizer` 20,317,856; every one of the other 41 compiling corpus programs carves exactly 65,544 (hq_B's census — the distribution is bimodal, nothing lives between). `sub rsp, 66457856` leaves the 8 MB VMA and the first write faults: ERROR 246 on the main thread (hq_B, 5 of 5), and — the face the cto measured — a SEGV in `FN__ir_a_ProcDecl`'s frame zeroing on a CO-EXPRESSION thread (gdb: `rep stos` at rsp, rbp-rsp = 66,131,888) whose pthread stack is sized to the creator's snapshot + 2 MB, where no `ulimit -s` can reach (rc=139 under 4 GB as under 8 MB).

## THE STRUCTURE, MEASURED (21:2x CDT) — WHY NO STATIC PER-SITE SUM CAN EVER FIT jtran, AND THE IN-LAW SHAPE THAT CAN
jcon's 17 modules hold 371 procedures, 63 of them generators, 43 in `irgen.icn`: the AST→IR walker is written as mutually recursive generators. `procedure ir(p, ...)` is a `case type(p)` dispatcher whose every arm is `suspend ir_a_X(p, ...)` (36 generator sites in one body); every `ir_a_X` suspends its chunks and calls `ir(p.left)`, `ir(p.right)`, `ir(child)` back. Recursion depth = AST depth, with NO host activation between levels. With the 63-slot multiplier switched off (`SCRIP_ICN_N2_SELFREC=0`) the compiler refuses exactly two hosts, `main` and `ir_a_ProcDecl` (the walker's entries), and every other host's reservation is at most 8,112 bytes — so the entire 66 MB is that one SCC.
THE ARITHMETIC: a static layout that gives every call site its own nested slice must reserve, for a walker of depth d with fan-out f, f^d slices — the tree of all POSSIBLE nested activations — while at run time the LIVE set is one path (`suspend ir(p.left); suspend ir(p.right)` exhausts the left before the right starts; only one case arm is ever active per `ir` activation): d frames. Exponential versus linear; no offset arithmetic, ceiling, or consistency fix changes that. The current code's 63× `cyc` cut is an attempt to bound it that both explodes (66 MB) and is inconsistent (the container is one cycle sum, the contents are inflated slices — the overrun witnessed in `FN__ir`).
THE IN-LAW SHAPE (the criterion's own direct-selfrec table, generalized): for each generator-only SCC the host reserves ONE TABLE of D slots, each slot = max over the SCC's members of `align16(own) + 48 + area(member's sites to generators OUTSIDE the SCC)`. A site that ENTERS the SCC from outside gets the table (D × slot) at a compile-time offset in the host's carve; the callee lands in slot 0 and receives `table_end` in the pad word, banked in its region header at H+40 (today's depth slot). A site from one member to another gets `next slot` = my own region + slot_size (a compile-time constant), inherits `table_end`, and BOMBS LOUDLY when `next + slot_size > table_end` — the rc-refusal the criterion sanctions, naming the SCC and D. Everything stays on the machine stack in the host's own carve at fixed offsets (nothing moves at any landing), per activation (each host activation carves its own table), sized D × slot_size — LINEAR in the depth cap — and entered slot by slot as the recursion actually deepens, which is Lon's "per activation and per path what it actually enters" read literally. Direct self-recursion is the SCC of size one, so `N2_SELFREC_SLOTS` and its bomb become the same mechanism. Non-SCC (DAG) callee sites keep today's summed slices (polynomial; every non-jtran corpus program already sits at 65,544).

## THE DEFECT THE CARVE WAS HIDING — THE NEXT BUG
With the carve bounded, the SCRIP-built 17-module jtran dies INSIDE the block: gdb puts it in `FN__ir`'s N-2 prologue (`mov [rax+0x39f0], rbp`, writing its header) with rax = the region pointer its caller pushed, 65 MB (0x3DEF180) ABOVE the caller's rbp — the caller is a generator (`n22135_proc_gen`), rbp = its region header H, and the handed slice lies past the end of the 66 MB block. Mechanism, from `icn_gen_host_slice`: when the walk meets a cycle it returns `63 × (sum of the frames on the cycle)` for the WHOLE callee area of that activation, but `icn_gen_host_reserve_offset` lays the activation's call sites out at their FULL inflated slices — so the moment a recursive callee is not the FIRST generator site in a body, its slice begins near the end of the container and overruns. Direct recursion and the 11-line pair survive only because their recursive site is the first site. Per-call-site static slicing cannot be made consistent under mutual recursion: the sizes are geometric in the nesting, the container is linear.
THE CURE, ON THE STACK (Lon's ruling, the ceo's CEO-372 words): a host reserves per ACTIVATION and per PATH what it actually enters — one `own + 48 + slots` carve per generator call site, made by `sub rsp` the moment the host enters the site, banked in the site's slot so re-entry reuses it, released by the host frame's own unwind — never the worst-case transitive sum, never allocated. That deletes the 63-slot multiplier, the cycle sum, `N2_SELFREC_SLOTS` and the `[GENHOST] RESERVES NOTHING` refusal at once. Its price is the frame model: a flat rsp-relative host cannot lower rsp mid-activation and keep its locals, so the host must be rbp-pinned with a floating rsp (the RBP ACTIVATION FRAME of the criterion), and every compile-time "rsp is at the frame bottom here" assumption in its epilogues and landings becomes rbp-relative. The loud rc-refusal cap the criterion already sanctions stays as the floor for anything still over a ceiling. The design goes in the row's NEXT for hq_U and hq_B before code.
