## ⭐ BENCHMARK STANDING — 3-WAY FIXED-TIME RATE, s247 (SCRIP m3/m4 · Arizona iconx · JCON)
⛔⛔ **THE METHOD WAS INVERTED MID-SESSION ON LON'S INSTRUCTION AND THE ANSWER CHANGED** (*"flip the tests to loop on a fixed time and report iterations, versus looping on a fixed iteration and reporting time… I do not trust those numbers"*).
