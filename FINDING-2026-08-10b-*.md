## ⭐⭐⭐ ROOT CAUSE (added same session, s10 — MEASURED in emitted code)

The blob-entry breakage is **an unconverted RBP reader pair left behind by D-1**, not `g_blob_ctx`.

Every PAT$ blob emits its ω continuation as a **store/load pair through `rbp`**:

```
proc_PAT$1_α_body:   mov  qword ptr [rbp + K], rax     # rax = lea proc_PAT$1_ω
proc_PAT$1_β:        jmp  qword ptr [rbp + K]
```

Enumerated at HEAD: `b_len.s` → 4 such references (PAT$0 at +32, PAT$1 at +48); `w_cap_stored.s` → 4, and **both blobs use +32 — they alias the same caller slot.**

Pre-D-1 `rbp` was the blob's OWN frame, so `[rbp+K]` was a private slot and the spelling was legal. D-1 deleted that frame; D-2 made the blob adopt the caller's. `rbp` therefore now names the **invoking statement's MATCH_BEGIN frame** (a licensed keeper), and the store smashes a live slot in it. Symptom depends on what occupied that slot: wild control transfer (**SEGV** — `LEN`/`ANY`/`ARBNO`) or corrupted loop/cursor state (**HANG** — `SPAN`/`BREAK`).

This is the goal file's own named hazard realised verbatim: *"⛔ CARVE-ERAD conviction stands: never cut the region under unconverted readers."* D-1 cut the region; these readers were never converted.

**It explains every observation at once:** universal across constructs · capture-independent · fires on a single non-nested entry (`b_len`) with no recursion · correct pre-D-1.

### On the `g_blob_ctx` hypothesis — verified as a fact, rejected as the cause

`g_blob_ctx[5]` is confirmed a single process-global (`pattern_match.c:624`), and α **does** publish correctly (stores rsp, γ, ω, scanflag, δ0 — seen in the asm). The conversion is **incomplete, not misdesigned**: the wires are published into ctx but the β dispatch never reads them — it still jumps via `[rbp+K]`. Global-vs-per-activation is real design debt that will bite on nesting/recursion, but it cannot explain a single non-nested entry dying. **Not the root cause.**

### Mechanism — ⚠ LABELLED INFERENCE, not runtime-measured

`emit_jmp_pin_rbp()` (emit.h:630) is a four-way OR: `flat_deep_arrival || flat_gen || flat_lcl_proc || zframe_graph`. D-2 removed `flat_pat` from it — **necessary but not sufficient**, because `flat_deep_arrival` is classified independently at `emit.cpp:3059` (`emit_graph_has_deep_arrival`) and still fires for these graphs, so the pinned spelling survives. **CONFIRMING EXPERIMENT OWED** (instrument the predicate per-graph, or step it): I read the emitted asm and the predicate, I did not observe the flag's value at runtime.

### Fix specification (for whoever takes it)

Convert the ω-wire linkage off the deleted frame: `α_body` must stop writing through `rbp`, and `β` must take the ω wire from the published ctx / spine cell instead of `[rbp+K]`. Both media (TEXT + BINARY) together per BOTH-MEDIUM MANDATORY; read `ARCH-ICON.md` + `GOAL-TEMPLATE-REVAMP-RULES-DRAFT.md` before the edit. The slot aliasing (two blobs both at +32) means a per-blob cell is required, not one shared slot — which is precisely PT-4's "a box a few bytes of its own."

**This narrows the decision:** the defect is localized and small, so fix-forward is now viable where s9's blind interior hunt was not. Revert still restores 29 programs immediately and remains the safe default. Lon's call.
