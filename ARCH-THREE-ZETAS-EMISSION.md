## 0. The model, in one paragraph each

**ONE machine stack, ONE coordinate, THREE storages.** Every graph has a FRAME BASE: where its prologue leaves rsp. Every depth in the plan is bytes below that base. The three zetas are three ways a cell is REACHED, and a cell is on exactly one of them, decided by the planner and written in the plan table's `zeta` column:

- **ζ-SPINE (rsp).** A FORTH stack of VARIABLE-LENGTH cells. Each box carves the cell it needs (`K_i` bytes, its own words plus the locals first used at it) at its α and pops exactly that cell at its ω; every cell's depth is a compile-time constant of the graph; a reader addresses the cell as `[rsp + c]` with `c` the constant distance between the reader's depth and the cell's. Failure unwinds the spine one box at a time, balanced; a β re-entry finds rsp at the box's own exit depth with nothing restoring it (CEO-1755/1759).
- **ζ-ACT (rbp).** A BLOCK of cells that exists PER ACTIVATION of a construct -- a procedure or predicate call, a pattern match `S ? P`, a deferred pattern's invocation, a generator's activation -- created at the construct's α by `push rbp; mov rbp, rsp; sub rsp, R` and released at its ω and at its fenced γ by `mov rsp, rbp; pop rbp`. It holds EXACTLY the cells no fixed rsp offset reaches at some read (Lon's TEST 1 and TEST 2), the construct's own state words (§ 3.1 of the coordinate page) and nothing else; the frame's size `R` is the size of that RESIDENT SET, never the number of keys. An ACT cell is addressed `[rbp - off]` by the boxes of its OWNER while the owner's frame is the current rbp, and in no other way.
- **ζ-STANDING (the root frame).** One per program: the root graph's frame, holding the language's GLOBAL-TYPE BB VALUES -- box state that boxes of ANY graph read from anywhere (today the Prolog dynamic-predicate root cells; by design Prolog's B and HB words) -- declared by the lowerer on the root graph because the construct is in the source, and reached from anywhere by WALKING THE RBP LINKS DOWN to the root frame (Lon 2026-10-22 14:2x CDT, CEO-1908, verbatim on § 8 (a): *"None, since it can be reached via RBP and walked down."*): no standing register in any language.
- **THE LIST.** Every ACT frame's link word `[rbp + 0]` is the pushed rbp of the activation that was current at the construct's α; the links run down to the root frame. Emitted code uses the list for TWO things: re-linking rbp at a retaining γ and at a β resume, so that the frame of the construct whose boxes run is always the current rbp (SNOBOL4's mechanism, § 3.3), and WALKING IT DOWN to the root frame to reach a standing cell (§ 4.2, Lon's ruling). The collector walks the same list by return PC (ARCH-GC § 13). Nobody addresses another activation's ACT cell through the list, and the number of links to the root is not a compile-time constant: the standing walk is a loop to the null link, never a counted hop.

## 1. The laws the emission obeys (the coordinate page's § 1, plus two laws this page adds)

For an armed box `i`: `K_i` its carve, `X_i` its exit depth (rsp after its carve), `E_i = X_i - K_i` its entry depth. THE THREE EDGE LAWS (ARCH-ZETA-ONE-COORDINATE § 1.1): γ→α `X_i - gpop_i = E_j`; ω→β `X_i - K_i - wpop_i = X_j`; γ→β `X_i - gpop_i = X_j`. THE GAMMA LAW (§ 3): a γ pops the cells of the construct it leaves only when that construct is FENCED (its β is dead), and may put its result in the popped region. ⛔ RULED BY LON 2026-10-24 15:1x CDT (in-chat to the cto, verbatim: *"Do not free on GAMME ever, except on a FENCE'd success."* · *"My FENCE'd GAMMA I mean like FENCE0 and FENCE1, and other like structures."*): FENCED means a COMMIT STRUCTURE -- FENCE0, FENCE1, a cut, a BOUND, an UNMARK, a GATE's commit, a statement's or a match's end -- the DISCARD-LAW edges below, and nothing else; an alternation's σ, an ARBNO's iteration return, a generator's resume and every ordinary γ→α edge keep every cell live (`gpop_i = 0`), the ω path alone pops a box's own carve, and the fenced landing pops the fenced body as ONE discard (`ZDISCARD`, the FENCE1 landing's `body_kk`). The planner's gback rule ("a box whose γ returns into an earlier box pops to that box's entry depth") violated this for an alternation's arms until SCRIP D1 (2026-10-24): ARB in `FENCE('a') | ARB` popped its cell at γ and re-grew it at β (site 15 of PRECARVE-WHACK-LIST.md), while the UNIFORM EXIT priced the arm as live; now an arm's last box returning into its ALTERNATE, ARBNO or FENCE1 pops nothing (`zeta_plan.cpp`, the late arm-return rule) and the σ pad is what the plan prices. DEFINITENESS (§ 2): a box is definite when `E_i` is a constant on every path; the two things that break it, for every language, are a RETAINING EDGE (the γ of a box whose callee's frame may still be live below the caller at that γ -- the ONE table `planner_retains`, codes 1-11) and a NON-CONSTANT PUSH; definiteness is regained at a discard whose record is definite.

**THE DISCARD LAW (new, ruled 2026-10-22 13:4x on the cfo's cut witness).** A discard edge -- Prolog's cut, the UNMARK that commits an if-then-else or finishes a findall, FENCE's β, MATCH_END/REPLACE, a SNOBOL4 statement's failure landing, ARBNO's unbounded record, a bounded Icon expression's BOUND/UNMARK, catch/3's unwind -- restores rsp to THE ENTRY DEPTH THE PLAN ASSIGNS TO THE BOX IT CONTINUES AT: `restore_i = E_cont`, never the depth of the box that made the record and never the exit depth of the arm the discard leaves. A cut inside a disjunction arm fences that arm, so under THE GAMMA LAW the arm's σ pops to the DISJUNCTION's α depth and the continuation is definite there; the cfo's witness reads `restore = 224` against `E_cont = 80`, and the 144-byte gap is exactly the arm's cells from the disjunction's α to the member call's exit. ZD-CHECK checks `restore_i == E_cont` for every discard edge AT COMPILE TIME (§ 7); today it skips discard arrivals, which is why the witness reads `violations=0`. The emitted form is § 2's `ZDISCARD`.

**THE REACH LAW (new).** An ACT cell is addressed only through its OWNER's rbp and only while the owner's frame is the current rbp. A box inside an INLINE-PINNED construct of the same graph (a SNOBOL4 match frame inside a procedure, ARCH-GC § 13.3) reaches the enclosing frame's cells through ONE static link (`mov rax, [rbp + 0]` then `[rax - off]`), because the nesting is a compile-time fact of the graph; that is a static hop, not the list walk. A dynamic number of hops to reach an ACT cell (a callee reaching its caller's cell) is forbidden in emitted code; what a callee needs from its caller is passed in its argument block or lives on the standing frame, which IS reached by walking the links to their null end (§ 4.2, Lon's ruling of 14:2x): the one dynamic walk emitted code performs.

## 2. The cell protocol: the FIVE forms a template emits for the stack, and no other

Every x86 instruction is produced inside `x86(...)` (TEMPLATE-ONLY EMISSION, both media). For the STACK a template uses exactly these forms, each a function of the plan table's row for the box, and `harness/scripts/util_template_stack_forms.py` (new, § 7) refuses a template that forms an `rsp` or `rbp` address any other way:

1. **`ZALPHA(K_i)`** at α: `sub rsp, K_i` (`x86_alpha_carve`, x86_asm.h:2150, today 0 uses: the emitter wraps the carve around the template instead). `K_i` is the plan's carve: the kind's own words from the cell-shape table (`harness/scripts/box_cell_shapes.tsv`, § 3.5 (iii) of the coordinate page) plus the spine locals first used at this box plus a disjunction's arm extra. A box whose `K_i` is 0 emits nothing. A VARIABLE-LENGTH cell is simply a box's own `K_i`, different per box and per kind: a value box 16 (one DESCR), TO 32 (the counter beside the value), ARBNO 16, a cursor-moving call 32, MATCH_BEGIN 64 plus its extra, the DEFER's glue record 16; the ONE non-constant cell is call/N's argument block (a run-time size carved by the CALL_VALUE box and released by the same box), after which every box of the run is indefinite until a discard.
2. **`ZOMEGA(K_i)`** at ω: `add rsp, K_i` then the jump to the predecessor's β (`x86_omega_free`, new beside `x86_gamma_free`). The ω pops the box's OWN carve and nothing else, so the chain of ωs unwinds the spine balanced. `wpop_i` in the plan is `K_i` plus the cells of the pass-through boxes the chain skips (the emitter's threaded edge is the plan's edge: a ω threaded through pass-through βs straight to MATCH_BEGIN's β must pop what the plan prices on that edge -- the cfo's EDGE triage class, ruled the planner's in § 3.4).
3. **`ZGAMMA(gpop_i, fenced_i)`** at γ: a fenced γ emits `add rsp, gpop_i` (`x86_gamma_free`) and, when the leaving construct's result takes the popped region, `x86_gamma_result(K, n)`; an unfenced γ emits NOTHING, its cells stay for the β. A retaining γ (a callee frame left live, § 3.3) is an unfenced γ that also leaves the RESUME RECORD.
4. **`ZDISCARD(E_cont)`** at a discard port: three emitted forms, chosen by the plan's definiteness column, all of them `rsp := base - E_cont` -- (a) the discarding box is DEFINITE: `add rsp, X_i - E_cont`, a balanced pop by a constant (SNOBOL4 emits 455 of these today; the preferred form, no memory and no rbp); (b) the discarding box is INDEFINITE and its graph's frame is pinned to rbp (every graph that owns an ACT frame): `lea rsp, [rbp - R - E_cont]`, the frame base being `rbp - R` where `R` is the resident size, so the record IS the frame base and no word is written for it; (c) neither -- a keys-only graph with no frame (none today: every graph carves at least its root, blob or match frame) -- `mov rsp, [record]`, the record a word written at the construct's α while definite (a BOUND word, a Prolog choice record's frame bound at `[B + 32]`). A RETRY never uses any of the three (`test_gate_no_rsp_reset_at_beta.sh` stays blocking at 0). **CORRECTION, MEASURED (CTO-353, 2026-10-22 18:xx CDT): (c) ALSO STAYS FOR A DISCARD WHOSE CONTINUATION IS INDEFINITE**, a construct entered after a retaining γ, whose record is the only true value. The plan called it definite because THE DEPTH PLAN'S VIEW OF RETAINS IS NOT THE TABLE: `zd_edge_retains` answers 0 for every graph that is not Prolog-resumable (`emit_resumable_frame`) and omits the generator callee even there. The accessor reads the row's `zeta` column and emits: SPINE → `[rsp + (D - depth_ref) + w]` where `D` is the emitter's DEPTH CURSOR (below); ACT → `[rbp - off_ref + w]` (the resident slot) or `[rbp - R - depth_ref + w]` (the rbp ALIAS of a definite spine cell, § 3.4); STANDING → the walk of § 4.2 into a scratch register, then `[rax + off_ref + w]`. It makes NO decision: a `ref` with no zeta is a FATAL at emit time, which is the compile-time twin of `x86_zop`'s `zls_key_unbacked` refusal.

**THE DEPTH CURSOR.** The emitter keeps ONE number per box while it emits the template, `D`, the current rsp depth below the frame base: set from the plan at every port (`E_i` at α, `X_i` at β, the plan's arrival depth at a γ or ω landing), advanced by every stack-moving instruction the encoder emits (`push`, `pop`, `sub rsp`, `add rsp`, `lea rsp`, a `call`'s pushed return address), read by `ZCELL`. The encoder asserts at each port's exit that `D` equals the plan's number for that port (a template that moves rsp outside the encoder's accounting trips it), and the ZSM checks the same at run time (§ 7). `rsp#` in today's text forms is this cursor; the design makes it the ONLY depth the emitter holds -- `op_zabs`, `op_zdepth`, `op_ztail`, `op_fc_base`, `gen_frame_top()` and the nine regime facts of `x86_zop` are replaced by it and by the plan's columns.

**THE FRAME FORMS** (§ 3): `ZFRAME_OPEN(shape)`, `ZFRAME_CLOSE(shape)`, `ZFRAME_LEAVE(shape)` (the retaining γ: the resume record and the re-link) and `ZFRAME_RESUME(shape)` (the β: rbp from the record, the balanced pop). A construct template that owns a frame emits exactly these at its α, ω/fenced γ, retaining γ and β; a template that does not own a frame never names rbp.

## 3. The activation frame

### 3.1 What an activation is, and when it has a frame

An ACTIVATION is one instance of a construct from THE CONSTRUCT REGISTRY, the graph fact `g->constructs[]` the lowerer sets because the construct is in the source (ARCH-ZETA-ONE-COORDINATE § 3.2 "the IR's missing fact"; Lon's word on it is asked in § 8): a GRAPH ENTRY (a SNOBOL4 pattern procedure or DEFINE'd function, an Icon procedure, a Prolog predicate, a Pascal procedure or function, a Raku sub or method, the program's main), `MATCH_BEGIN..MATCH_END` (`S ? P`), `MATCH_DEFER` (`*P`, `*F()`), an Icon generator's activation (`PROC_GEN`), a scan (`IR_SCAN`'s environment), a co-expression body, a run-time compiled EVAL/CODE chain, a Prolog table header, a findall/bagof region, catch/3. Each construct KIND has a FRAME SHAPE, a STATIC fact shared by every activation of the kind in the graph (ARCH-GC § 13.3's `shape`), with three parts:

- **the HEADER:** the link word at `[rbp + 0]` (the pushed rbp), the return PC at `[rbp + 8]` when the construct is entered by `call` (a procedure, a pattern procedure, a thunk), the registers the construct pins and must give back (SNOBOL4's match saves r12..r15 because r13/r14/r15 carry the subject, cursor and length while it runs), and the construct's STATE WORDS as `state_word_kinds.tsv` derives them from the templates (address-escapes, beta-read, continuation: the Prolog BOUND and DISJUNCTION header words, TO's db state, the resumable callable's continuation and step words, the SCAN's saved environment, the generator header's trace word);
- **the RESIDENT CELLS:** the cells the planner put on ζ-ACT -- parameters (a frame slot by definition: written by the caller's argument block or copied from it at entry), declared cells (a Pascal local referenced uplevel, `zls_home_local_declare(g, name, 9 "uplevel")`; the Raku redispatch record), and the escapes: a cell PRODUCED IN AN INDEFINITE RUN and read outside it (§ 3.4);
- **the MAP:** the collector's layout quads over the header and the resident cells (ARCH-GC § 7, unchanged in format), and the site-table rows of every `call`, poll and γ landing inside the construct, each with base rule `RBP + adj` (the frame is rbp-pinned while its boxes run) or, for a box of a graph that owns no frame, `RSP + depth` with the plan's depth.

A construct HAS AN RBP-PINNED FRAME iff some word of its shape is read at an INDEFINITE port or is an ACT cell: Lon's two tests applied to the header itself. A construct whose every header read is definite keeps its header as a SPINE CELL of its entry box, reached by rsp, and never pins rbp -- SNOBOL4's DEFINE'd function is that today (`xa_flat_chain_prologue_str`, xa_flat.cpp:518: `sub rsp, 64`, the continuations and the saved rbp at fixed depths, rbp never set) and stays so. A construct whose shape is empty emits nothing at all: its cells are spine cells, the collector sweeps them as typed cells (ARCH-GC § 2b, § 13.6), and its boxes name rbp nowhere. MEASURED today every graph of every language carves a frame (the SNOBOL4 root and blob frames, the match frame, the Icon procedure and generator frames, the Prolog pinned frame `kt = (96 | 80) + value_region + 8 × standing_cells`, the Pascal and Raku frames) and sizes it by its keys; under this page a SNOBOL4 pattern procedure whose shape is the two return words and the DTP cell carves 32 bytes, a Pascal function whose locals are all spine cells and whose parameters are read from the caller's block carves its header alone, and a Prolog predicate's `value_region` is its resident set, not its key count.

### 3.2 Creation and teardown

**THE ONE SHAPE, ruled (the 13:5x survey of b282ecafb).** Today two shapes exist. SNOBOL4's blob frame (emit.cpp:4392), the match frame (bb_match_begin.cpp:29) and the DEFER frame are `push rbp; mov rbp, rsp; …`: rbp at the HEADER, the link at `[rbp + 0]`, the frame below rbp. The Prolog, Icon-procedure, Pascal and Raku frames (`xa_flat_zframe_prologue_str`, xa_flat.cpp:348; `flat_lcl_proc`, emit.cpp:4296) are `sub rsp, kt; mov [rsp + kt - 8], rbp; mov rbp, rsp`: rbp at the BASE, the link at the TOP (`[rbp + kt - 8]`), the frame above rbp at positive offsets; the Icon generator frame (emit.cpp:4245) is a third, rbp at a header carved by `lea`, and SNOBOL4's DEFINE'd function (xa_flat.cpp:518) pins no rbp at all. ONE MECHANISM (CEO-1755) means one shape: SNOBOL4's hand-coded one -- rbp at the header, the link at `[rbp + 0]`, the return PC at `[rbp + 8]`, the frame below rbp at negative offsets, the frame base at `rbp - R`. The base-pinned prologues move to it in their language's switch (ARCH-GC § 13.3's two base rules collapse to one `RBP + adj` per shape); until a language switches, its `ZCELL` ACT forms print `[rbp + off]` and `[rbp - d]` as today, which is the same rule with `R` on the other side of rbp.

- **α (`ZFRAME_OPEN`):** `push rbp; mov rbp, rsp; push <pinned registers>; sub rsp, R` with `R` the resident bytes rounded to 16 (the plan's LAYOUT column for the construct). After it the FRAME BASE is rsp, the header is at and above rbp, the resident cells at `[rbp - 8 × n_pinned - off]`. Entered by `call`, the return PC is already at `[rbp + 8]`. The parameters of a predicate or procedure are the CALLER's block on the spine, just above the pushed return address (`[rbp + 16 + 16i]`, the Prolog pinned shape of ARCH-PROLOG-C-OUT-OF-THE-BOX § 1.3), read in place; a parameter is copied into a resident cell only when the construct outlives the block (a retained callee whose caller may pop the block: the planner's TEST 1 on the block).
- **ω (`ZFRAME_CLOSE`):** `mov rsp, rbp; pop <pinned registers in reverse>; pop rbp` then the jump the construct's ω takes (a procedure: `ret`-shaped into the call box's ω tail, which pops the argument block as its own carve and jumps to its predecessor's β). The frame and every spine cell above the base are gone in one move: that is the ONLY place rsp is set from rbp on a failure path, and it is a discard of the whole construct.
- **FENCED γ (`ZFRAME_CLOSE` before the jump):** a construct that CANNOT be resumed at this exit -- a SNOBOL4 pattern procedure whose match succeeded with no alternative left, a Prolog predicate exiting with `B == B0` (no choice point younger than its entry: the deterministic exit), an Icon `return`, a Pascal or Raku return, a match whose MATCH_END commits -- tears its frame down BEFORE its γ, so the caller's continuation finds rsp at the call box's exit depth and stays DEFINITE. The result travels in registers or in the call box's own cell, never in the dead frame.
- **RETAINING γ (`ZFRAME_LEAVE`), § 3.3:** the frame stays.

### 3.3 The retaining γ and the β resume: SNOBOL4's mechanism, the one for all seven (CEO-1755)

Read in the asm of `P = ARB . X 'b' *E` / `S P` at 12:2x (ARCH-ZETA-ONE-COORDINATE § 3): the stored-pattern procedure `.LTp0` succeeds with alternatives left, LEAVES ITS FRAME LIVE on the spine, pushes a RESUME RECORD -- its rbp, the caller's continuation, its own resume address -- and RE-LINKS `mov rbp, [rbp + 0]` one link down before the jump, so the caller's boxes address the match frame again as their own rbp; its resume label reloads ITS OWN rbp from the record (`mov rbp, [rsp + 24]; add rsp, 32`: the balanced pop of exactly its four words -- rbp from a record, rsp never), and its ω pops its whole frame and the link (`mov rsp, rbp; pop rbp`). THE FORMS:

- **`ZFRAME_LEAVE(shape)`** at a retaining γ: `push rbp; push <continuation>; push <resume label>; push <pad>` (the RESUME RECORD, 32 bytes, four typed words the spine sweep reads as RAW -- a code address, a stack address, a code address), then `mov rbp, [rbp + 0]`, then the jump to the caller's continuation. The caller's continuation box heads an INDEFINITE run (the planner's retaining edge, codes 4, 5, 7, 11); the record's depth is not a constant of the caller, and the caller's boxes reach the cells they produced before the call through rbp (§ 3.4), never through rsp.
- **`ZFRAME_RESUME(shape)`** at the β the record names: `mov rbp, [rsp + 24]; add rsp, 32` then the construct's β body. The record is popped by exactly its size; the callee's frame is the current rbp again; its boxes resume at their own exit depths by their own pops (ω→β balanced within the callee).
- **The ω of a retained construct** (its last alternative fails): `ZFRAME_CLOSE` then the call box's ω tail: the call box pops its own carve (the argument block) and jumps to ITS predecessor's β; the caller's spine is balanced to the call's entry depth with nothing restoring it.
- **PROLOG:** today a retaining γ leaves rsp where the callee left it and passes `rax = frame, rdx = β` in registers for the caller to store in its act word (`bcps` block arm, bb_call_proc_staged.cpp:783; the beta-read state word of `state_word_kinds.tsv`), and the caller's β reloads rbp from that word (`bcps_block_beta`, :854). Under this page the act word goes: the choice record IS the resume record. A clause alternative's record on the spine (the B chain, r13 = B) holds the predicate frame's rbp, the alternative's address, the trail mark and the older B; a retry is entered through B (`ZFRAME_RESUME` reads rbp from the choice record at the plan's offset); the predicate's retaining γ re-links rbp to the caller's frame as above; a CUT (`ZDISCARD`) drops B to B0 and rsp to `E_cont` in one box, leaving the retained frames and records below rsp as dead residue the sweep reads harmlessly (ARCH-GC § 13.5). `ARCH-PROLOG-ACTIVATION-LIFETIME.md` carries the rest (last call, the trail at a cut, the binding direction, the allocator at the cap).
- **ICON:** today the generator's γ writes its resume address into its own header (`[rbp + 32]`), the caller banks the handle in its act word, and the caller's β CALLS `rt_gen_spine_resume_enter` before jumping through the handle (bb_call_proc_staged.cpp:999), a C road on the β. Under this page a `suspend` is `ZFRAME_LEAVE` of the generator's activation and the β is `ZFRAME_RESUME`, no C; the `every` loop-back re-enters through the record (`ZFRAME_RESUME`), and the body's γ pops through the chain to the generator's β, never restoring (hq_icon's gdb reading of 10-18, § 1.2 of the coordinate page). `IR_SCAN`'s suspend-leave form is the same record (CTO-338's cure, SCRIP 352ff19db, was this form's pop computed from the wrong label).
- **RAKU:** a gather's `take` is the SNOBOL4 form over the gather body's activation.
- **PASCAL:** no construct retains; the form is never emitted.

### 3.4 Which cells are ACT cells: Lon's two tests, applied by the planner, and the rbp ALIAS that keeps most cells on the spine

TEST 1 (an OPERAND reached at no fixed rsp offset by some consumer) and TEST 2 (a LOCAL not at the same rsp-relative address at α and β) fail only across a retaining edge or a non-constant push, i.e. when the READER is indefinite or the PRODUCER is. The frame base is rbp minus the resident size, a constant of the shape, so a DEFINITE spine cell at depth `d` is ALSO at `[rbp - R - d]` as long as it is carved, from EVERY box of the owner, definite or not. THE ALIAS RULE: an operand produced by a DEFINITE box and read by an INDEFINITE consumer is read through its rbp alias, in place, with no copy and no resident bytes (ARCH-PROLOG-SPINE-SEGMENTS § 3 R-B already does this for locals: *"a use in an indefinite box of a definite local is `[rbp - L]`: no frame slot, no copy"*; this page extends it to operands, replacing R-C's frame home for that class). A cell moves INTO THE RESIDENT REGION -- the producer writes it to a frame slot the shape lays out -- only when its PRODUCER is indefinite and some consumer is outside the producer's run: then neither rsp nor rbp knows the cell's depth. The planner's `zeta` column distinguishes `ACT:alias` (depth `d`, no bytes) from `ACT:resident` (offset `off`, bytes in `R`); the placement census prints the two counts. On the cfo's measured Prolog population (FINDING 2026-10-22, 13,126 call sites of which 11,932 retaining under the blanket) this is what keeps "frame-homed" from meaning "every operand after the first call".

**SNOBOL4, named as Lon remembers it (13:4x):** the two things that put a SNOBOL4 cell on the match frame are (1) a `*DEFERRED` pattern -- `MATCH_DEFER`, whose resolved chain is its callee and may leave its frame live at γ for a later β (code 7, re-ruled a retaining edge in § 3.4 of the coordinate page on the cfo's six-site ZSM reading) -- and (2) a PATTERN-VALUED FUNCTION CALL inside the match -- `*F()`, the EXPR$ thunk's call to a function whose returned pattern is matched in place, a callee that retains (codes 4 and 5). Both are arms of the ONE retaining-edge table and nothing else in SNOBOL4 is one: every other SNOBOL4 box is definite, which is why SNOBOL4 reads 100 percent on the relative plan with zero frame homes and why it is the model.

**What is NOT an ACT cell, each a pre-allocation the switch rows remove:** a value box's result when the box is definite (the layout grants every one a slot today); a literal or global staged into the frame at graph entry (the FCC tree); a det-leaf call's argument block (a spine cell carved at the call's α); a Pascal local not referenced uplevel; a Prolog clause variable whose every use is definite or aliased; anything a graph-level proxy ("the graph is a generator", "the predicate retains") would force -- the tests are per cell (CEO-1750).

## 4. The standing frame

### 4.1 What it holds

The root graph's frame holds the STANDING CELLS: a language's global-type BB values -- box state of PROGRAM lifetime that boxes of any graph read from anywhere -- declared by the lowerer on the root graph (`standing_cells`, each with a name and a reason, as `--dump-homes` prints `home=STANDING why=dynamic-predicate-root` today) because the construct that needs it is in the source. MEASURED: the one population in the fleet is Prolog's dynamic-predicate root cells (`lower_prolog.c:5636`; 6 PTR_GC quads in the root maps over the gc_witnesses). BY DESIGN and awaiting their rows: Prolog's choice words B and HB as standing words (ARCH-PROLOG-C-OUT-OF-THE-BOX § 6), the head cells the global-stacks page sends to ζ-STANDING (`g_core_errjmp_stk`'s head, `g_icn_bi_top`'s head, the SNOBOL4 counter root outside a match; ARCH-GLOBAL-STACKS-TO-THE-ZETAS § 2 rows 4, 10, 12), and whatever a language's lowerer declares by name under the cto's ruling (CEO-1715/1716's rule by analogy: a standing cell is permitted by name, with the count written in the landing). NOT a standing cell: a construct-lifetime operand or local (THE LIFETIME RULE; Lon 2026-09-02: never a surrogate global); engine state the runtime keeps in C (the arena, the atom table, the symbol table); the program's named variables (Lon 2026-10-22 12:2x: the list is unrelated to program local variables) -- unless Lon rules the 09-02 reading (§ 8 (d)).

### 4.2 How it is reached: the walk down the rbp links (Lon 2026-10-22 14:2x CDT, CEO-1908, verbatim: *"None, since it can be reached via RBP and walked down."*)

THERE IS NO STANDING REGISTER in any language. A STANDING cell is read by walking the list from the current rbp to its null end and addressing the root frame it ends at: `mov rax, rbp` · `1: mov rcx, [rax + 0]` · `test rcx, rcx` · `jz 2f` · `mov rax, rcx` · `jmp 1b` · `2:` then `[rax - off + w]` -- six instructions plus one per link, emitted by `ZCELL` for a STANDING row, the one dynamic walk emitted code performs (THE REACH LAW, § 1). It works from everywhere because every ACT frame links to the activation current at its α and the root's link is 0 (§ 4.3), and it crosses C frames (the runtime is built with frame pointers, so a callback's C frames link rbp the same way) and co-expression stacks (§ 4.3). The cost is one link per live activation between the reader and the root; it is measured, not assumed: the placement census prints the standing reads per program and `SCRIP_ZSM=1` the walk's mean and maximum link count on the population, and if deep recursion makes it a problem the number goes to Lon, not a register (the ceo's CEO-1908 relay). The cto's earlier recommendations (r14 everywhere in the coordinate page § 3; a per-language plane entry in the first cut of this page) are WITHDRAWN; Prolog's `r14 = ROOT` leaves its plane in the Prolog switch (the dynamic-predicate roots read through the walk; `rt_pl_quad_seed`'s `mov r14, rdi` and the `[r14 - 24 - 8k]` form go), and r14 is free there as in every other language. TODAY: Prolog's plane has `r14 = ROOT` (`rt_pl_quad_seed`, rtx_plunify.s:19: r14 is the address of the root frame's header H, and standing cell k reads `[r14 - 24 - 8k]`, xa_flat.cpp:278; the root carves `PL_DB_FRAME_CELLS` = 256 cells always and cells past 256 go through the overflow registry `[[r14 - 24] + 24]`, the growth road CEO-1210 requires); SNOBOL4's and Icon's planes have r14 as the match or scan CURSOR δ (r13 the subject base, r15 the length), and Pascal's has r13/r14/r15 as display levels 1-3, so r14 cannot be the standing register of three languages without moving what they keep there; no language but Prolog has a standing cell. THE RULING THIS PAGE MAKES: the standing register is a PER-LANGUAGE ENTRY OF THE REGISTER PLANE, assigned by the language's lowerer declaration when the language declares standing cells (Prolog: r14, as granted), left unassigned while it declares none; a language that comes to declare one names its register in `src/ir/zeta_choices.h`'s plane in the same landing. The alternative, r14 for every language, is put to Lon in § 8 (a) with the SNOBOL4 cost stated; the coordinate page's § 3 recommendation of r14 everywhere is amended by this paragraph.

### 4.3 The root frame's shape

`ZFRAME_OPEN` of the root graph writes its LINK WORD AS 0 -- the list's end, which the standing walk and the collector's ROOT map both recognise -- and keeps the C driver's rbp it must restore at exit in a header word of its own (`[rbp - 8]`), never in the link (today the Prolog root's `[kt - 8]` holds the driver's rbp, so a walk would run on into the driver's C frames); then the GCANCHOR, the standing cells by declaration in the resident region, the pinned registers of the language's plane; `ZFRAME_CLOSE` at program exit restores the driver's rbp from that word. A CO-EXPRESSION body's first frame links to the ACTIVATION THAT CREATED IT (a pointer across stacks, as the collector's segment walk already allows, ARCH-GC § 13.4 item 2), so a walk from inside a co-expression reaches the program's one root frame; a thread's parked `park_rbp` is unchanged. Mode 3 and mode 4 emit the same root; mode 4's startup registration binds the standing cells' addresses for the C roads that reach them by name (the registry road, ARCH-PROLOG-C-OUT-OF-THE-BOX § 5.3).

## 5. The list

- **The link word** `[rbp + 0]` of every ACT frame is the rbp that was current at the construct's α: the activation that was running. The links run from the current frame down to the root frame of the thread.
- **Re-linking** is the emitted code's first use of a link: `ZFRAME_LEAVE` re-links rbp to the caller's frame at a retaining γ; `ZFRAME_RESUME` reloads the callee's rbp from the record; `ZFRAME_CLOSE` pops the link. A box always runs with ITS OWNER's frame in rbp. **The standing walk** (§ 4.2) is the second: the links followed to their null end to reach the root frame's standing cells. The static hop of THE REACH LAW (an inline-pinned construct reaching its graph's frame) is the only other link read.
