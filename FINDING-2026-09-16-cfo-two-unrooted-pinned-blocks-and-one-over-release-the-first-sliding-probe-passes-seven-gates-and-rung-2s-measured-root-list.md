## 1. The insertion-order array of every table was an unrooted pinned block (SCRIP d9b04af4d)

`table_set_descr_d` (aggregates.c) keeps each table's insertion order in `tbl->ord`, a DESCR_t array it `rt_pinned_alloc`s into the collected heap as HB_WS. `gc_visit_tbblk` visited `dflt`, `buckets` and every pair -- never `ord`. Pinned means DOES NOT MOVE, not IS NOT RECLAIMED: the block is unmarked at every collection, reclaimed under a live table, a live movable block slides into its bytes, and the next insert writes a 16-byte key descriptor into it.

How it was found: rung 2's first sliding probe (`hb_no_move` returning 0 on a scratch build, `rt_pinned_alloc` untouched, the `_func_buckets` root from this morning in place) passed six of seven collector gates; the seventh, the Arizona gc2 witness, segfaulted in mode 4 only inside `rt_gc_visit_descr` on a record instance whose `type` field read `0x100000002`. A hardware watchpoint on that word from the first collection on named the writer: `table_set_descr_d:341`, `tbl->ord[...] = k`, and the value is the integer key descriptor itself (v=2, slen=1). The instance had slid into the dead ord's bytes.

Fail-once / pass-once on TODAY's tree, libraries distinct by sha256 (origin 699314447f17cf19, cure 669b670cba9c4e0b): a 60-insert witness (20 integer keys, six live 100-byte strings, COLLECT(), 40 more keys) under `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=20` HANGS rc=124 in m3 AND m4 on origin; under 200 with telemetry it ABORTS "[ZHP] heap verify FAILED ... (corrupt title)". On the cure: byte-identical to sbl in both modes at both stress values, 1 s. Without stress the conservative C-stack scan hides it most of the time (a stale stack word keeps the dead block alive by luck) -- which is why `SCRIP_GC_STRESS` is the instrument for this whole class.

Cure: one line in `gc_visit_tbblk` -- visit `&t->ord` (marks and registers the slot) and every `t->ord[i]`. Gate `test_gate_gc_table_insertion_order_array_is_rooted_not_pinned_alive.sh` (structural arm + the witness in m3/m4 at stress 20 and 200 against the oracle), wired beside the collector gates, adopted into the wiring floor. With it, the sliding probe passes all seven collector gates.

Population: the 94 csnobol4_suite programs run singly read identical on origin and cure (56 pass / 37 my-harness-shape / 1 pre-existing crash). RebM 43/43 both modes. Preflight 50/50.

## Method notes worth keeping

A pinned block is move-proof, not reclaim-proof; every holder the collector cannot see is a reclaim waiting for a slide. `SCRIP_GC_STRESS` makes a stale-stack alibi deterministic. A hardware watchpoint on the corrupted word names the writer in one run. hq_snobol4's `$sp` comparison beat a backtrace census because the unwinder loses the chain two frames above generated code: a backtrace that cannot see a frame reports its absence exactly like a frame that is not there. And compare LIBRARIES, not launcher binaries, when the compiler lives in the .so.
