## New finding: `programs/prolog/swi_tests/` — 231 of 249 files (93%) invisible to a flat glob

Neither prior FINDING's Prolog coverage sections reached inside `swi_tests/` itself. `test_prolog_swi_suite.sh` — the only script referencing this directory anywhere in the 241-script inventory — selects with `for f in "$SWIT"/test_*.pl` (verified by direct grep of the live script, line 44): a flat, non-recursive glob.
