# The Prolog engine

The language's oracles, dialects, suites and source map are skill `prolog`; the shared memory model is skill `scrip-memory-model`. Detail is in `reference.md` beside this file.

## 1. The model (Lon 2026-09-02: "give Prolog its activation ZETA like all the rest"; 2026-09-26: the rewrite as Byrd boxes)

- **A predicate call is one box.** α enters the current clause and unifies its head; γ succeeds; β re-enters to redo the youngest sub-goal or step to the next clause; ω releases after the trail is unwound to the mark. Clause alternatives are the box's β path; the last clause's β is its ω. A bounded box emits no β and no choice point.
- **A box's logic is inside its chunk**: a call from emitted Prolog into C or rtx is legal only for a value service (I/O, atom and string text, sorting, bignum and float arithmetic, `copy_term`, error-term construction). Unification, dereference, binding, the trail push and unwind, term construction, the frame, choice open and cut, and clause selection are emitted. The old C helpers for these (`rt_pl_choice_open`, `rt_pl_disj_open`, `rt_pl_cut_barrier`, `rt_pl_unify_value`, `rt_pl_dop_mkc`, `rt_pl_unify_struct_fresh`, `rt_pl_goal_spine_prep`) are gone.
- **A ground compound literal with a compound inside is ONE box** (CEO-2121, 2026-11-04): lower_prolog.c serializes it into `g_stage2.pl_ground_words` ({tag word, value} pairs; a compound's value is nargs << 32 | the index of its argument run) and emits one `IR_CALL "$mkground"` naming the slice; the leaf-call template lays the table after the box and calls `rt_pl_mkground(table, &result)`, which allocates each compound's argument block (HB_DVEC, as `$mkc` does) and fills it, iterating down the last argument; one poll after the call. A flat ground compound (`foo(1,2)`, `[a]`) and every term with a variable stay cell by cell on `$mkc`. Before it a literal list element cost two LIT_INTEGER boxes and a `$mkc` (181 lines of asm, 90 KB of compiler memory per element: hakank_swi_stable_marriage's two 100x100 matrices never finished compiling). Gate: `test_gate_prolog_ground_literal_is_one_box.sh`.
- **A single-sided rule (`Head => Body`, CEO-2128, 2026-11-04)** lowers to its clause with the head arguments unified one by one by the ordinary unify boxes inside an if-then-else: `'$ssu_begin'(S)` before the bound captures the variable creation serial counter, `'$ssu_check'(S)` after the unifications reads only the trail entries since the bound's mark and refuses the clause when a variable created before S was bound, aliased to another such variable, or aliased to the same fresh variable as another (lower_prolog.c `pl_ssu_lower_clause`, by_name_dispatch.c `rt_pl_dop_ssu_begin_c`/`rt_pl_dop_ssu_check_c`); the guard then `!` then the body follow, and a head that matches nothing throws `existence_error(matching_rule, _)`. subsumes_term/2 is never on the head's road: through it every `=>` library predicate walking a list paid the list per call (lists:max_member_/3 over 500k elements was quadratic).
- **Run-time goals** (`call/N`, a catch goal, the goal of findall, negation, an asserted body) are compiled at run time through the EVAL/CODE path and run as boxes; there is no solver.
- **Unification mirrors the SNOBOL4 matcher** (Lon 2026-09-26, CEO-1279: "The Prolog unification needs to mirror SNOBOL4's pattern matching, using R13, R14, and R15, and also R12 as a stack."): a binding is a conditional assignment pushed on the r12 stack and undone from it on backtracking; the trail is the CAS twin.

## 2. The register plane and the trail (as built, `src/runtime/rtx/rtx_plunify.s`, `src/runtime/rt/pl_trail_layout.h`)

- **r12 = TR**, the trail top. The trail is one arena aligned to its size (`PL_TR_ARENA_LG2` 29), so `r12 & PL_TR_ARENA_MASK` is the arena's header (`PL_TR_HEADER_BYTES` 160): the ball slot, the wake slot, the cleanup record, the floor, the alarm words. Entries are 32 bytes. The trail and the choice records are one of the two r12 islands Lon keeps off the RSP/RBP stack; no movable DESCR lives in an island.
- **r13 = B**, the choice point and cut barrier; saved at a fence (`bb_bound.cpp`).
- **r15 = the ball** (a heap pointer while armed); `PL_BALL_ARM`, `PL_BALL_DROP` and `PL_BALL_GET` write and read it together with the header's ball slot. A ball propagates ω by ω to the innermost `catch/3` gate, which restores its planned depth (`x86_pl_ball_rule_omega`, `bb_gate.cpp`, `rt_pl_catch_handle_c`). This is the model the one error protocol generalises (skill `runtime-calls`).
- **r14** is not a Prolog-wide pin today; the leaf uses it as scratch inside unification.

## 3. Placement in the Prolog regime (the one decider)

Every Prolog graph is a home-frame graph (`home_frame` in `pl_graph_stamp`; Lon, CEO-1945: "keep it whacked and fix the misplaced ones by changing that carve logic to be proper"). A cell leaves the spine only for Lon's three reasons (CEO-1942): a generator's resumption, a retry after an unknown number of pushes, a recursion. The decider is the unbounded-excursion test, one function: `planner_span_retains(g, from, to)` in `src/planner/zeta_plan.cpp`, over `zd_edge_retains` (codes `PR_UNKNOWN_CALLEE`, `PR_GATE_HOLDS`, `PR_DISJUNCTION_HOLDS`, `PR_DB_GENERATOR`, `PR_RETAINING_TARGET`), `IR_SUSPEND` and a retaining disjunction (`zls_dj_retains`). A second copy of that test anywhere is a defect. The order is classify every graph, then plan, then lay out; the frame is sized by its resident words, never by keys.

## 4. Continuations and threads

- **`reset/3` and `shift/1`** are one-shot coroutines on the generator-procedure road: prelude clauses embedded in `src/parsers/prolog/prolog_parse.c` over the `$co_*` leaves (`rt_pl_dop_co_*` in `by_name_dispatch.c`), each coroutine on its own stack, r12 crossing every switch, the state in `scrip_coctx_t`. A continuation failing back across its trail floor raises `permission_error(backtrack, continuation, ...)` where swipl backtracks: the one recorded divergence.
- **Threads, engines and the toplevel are excluded** (Lon, CEO-1766: "Put the multi-threaded Prolog work as excluded for now."): thread cases leave the denominator as `THREADS_EXCLUDED_BY_LON`. The recorded design (one baton, real pthreads, preemption at the poll) is in `reference.md` § 4.

## 5. DESIGN: prolog-lifo (CEO-2000; `corpus/tests/prolog/lifo/STEPS.tsv`, `test_design_prolog_lifo_steps.sh`)

Every Prolog operand on the spine, the frame a header only. Steps (all hq_prolog's): 1 to-beta anchor; 2 one definiteness decider (DONE, relanded after the plain CLP(FD) regression); 3 a join normalized to the deepest arm (DONE 2026-11-04, hq_templates on the cto's shelf: the gate cells road is unconditional and SCRIP_PL_GATE_CELLS and zd_bound_arm_resumes are deleted; the one gate pass zd_pl_gate_arms prices every reaching arm to J, the deepest arm, in one class and gpop 0 per arm in two; a non-retaining CALL_DIRECT landing on the gate's beta is admitted and a soft cut over a retaining condition keeps its frame word under reason code 13; THE BETA DISPATCH POP is read from the ARM's own post-gamma depth (pf_gate, plan_facts.cpp), never from the gate node's row depth, because every arm of a gate shares one exit that records the gate beta as the resume and a redo finds the arm's cell wherever the arm left it; a pass that skipped the non-retaining arms was measured and is wrong, lifo_fd_queens4 traps under SCRIP_ZD_RTCHECK=1 on it); 4 a discard restores by distance or hop; 5 the hop for operands; 6 the clause step balanced; 7 the finish (4-7 FREE on 2026-11-01). On 2026-11-04 (CEO-2126) the setup_call_cleanup construct's failure exit (the CLEANUP RESUME box, whose beta is also the clause's redo for a PENDING construct) restores rsp from its mark BOUND's record before conceding, as the construct's UNMARKs do; the planner's one decider for that exit's depth is `zd_pl_resume_depth` (the exit depth of its omega target), written to the resume's restore column and used to price a clause exit whose redo is the resume, so the exit never pops a live choice's spine cell. Step 4 re-expresses these record restores as hops where the depth is definite. On 2026-11-04 (CTO-505) a TO's resumption state is the TO's own spine cell under R-F: the db TO's words (the slot, G, the choice header) leave the frame as the trailed TO's did, read at beta at the TO's exit depth and past a retaining call through the hop; the pl_to_db row's D3 kind 4 note is superseded, frame_layout.c keeps no mirror slot for it, and the five generator-state cells of retract/1 and clause/2 count under operand-edge, which reaches 0 with them. CEO-1942's generator's resumption is a generator callee's retained frame, not a box's enumeration words. **THE HOP'S EMISSION FORM (CTO-557, 2026-11-08, on the cfo's step-4 ask)**: the hop chain is columns of the plan row (hop_n, hop_rec[i], hop_c; written where `planner_span_retains` finds retaining edges between a cell's carve and its reader; printed by `--dump-plan`), copied by the prepare block to `_.op_hop_*`; one term `x86_hop_base()` beside `x86_zop` loads the chain into rcx (`mov rcx, [rsp + hop_rec[0] + 8]`, the callee rbp of the youngest resume record; `mov rcx, [rcx + 24]`, the caller's entry anchor; one such pair per edge crossed), re-emitted after every call on the road and before each group of reads (a caller-saved scratch, never an owned register or a spill word); `x86_zop` gains the hop regime answering `[rcx + hop_c + w]`; the five forms are unchanged and `FRQ` leaves with the last frame reader; the commit UNMARK restores `lea rsp, [rcx + hop_c]` when the depth is not definite; `x86_zverify` checks each hop_rec names a `pl_spine_record` cell and, under `SCRIP_ZD_RTCHECK=1`, bounds-checks each anchor. Rows A (columns and copy), B (term, regime, bb_bound), C (verifier), then step 5 per reader kind; cut when the one thing admits it (CIO-7 holds the fleet on UniTests).
