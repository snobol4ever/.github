# The Prolog engine

The language's oracles, dialects, suites and source map are skill `prolog`; the shared memory model is skill `scrip-memory-model`. Detail is in `reference.md` beside this file.

## 1. The model (Lon 2026-09-02: "give Prolog its activation ZETA like all the rest"; 2026-09-26: the rewrite as Byrd boxes)

- **A predicate call is one box.** α enters the current clause and unifies its head; γ succeeds; β re-enters to redo the youngest sub-goal or step to the next clause; ω releases after the trail is unwound to the mark. Clause alternatives are the box's β path; the last clause's β is its ω. A bounded box emits no β and no choice point.
- **A box's logic is inside its chunk**: a call from emitted Prolog into C or rtx is legal only for a value service (I/O, atom and string text, sorting, bignum and float arithmetic, `copy_term`, error-term construction). Unification, dereference, binding, the trail push and unwind, term construction, the frame, choice open and cut, and clause selection are emitted. The old C helpers for these (`rt_pl_choice_open`, `rt_pl_disj_open`, `rt_pl_cut_barrier`, `rt_pl_unify_value`, `rt_pl_dop_mkc`, `rt_pl_unify_struct_fresh`, `rt_pl_goal_spine_prep`) are gone.
- **A ground compound literal with a compound inside is ONE box** (CEO-2121, 2026-11-04): lower_prolog.c serializes it into `g_stage2.pl_ground_words` ({tag word, value} pairs; a compound's value is nargs << 32 | the index of its argument run) and emits one `IR_CALL "$mkground"` naming the slice; the leaf-call template lays the table after the box and calls `rt_pl_mkground(table, &result)`, which allocates each compound's argument block (HB_DVEC, as `$mkc` does) and fills it, iterating down the last argument; one poll after the call. A flat ground compound (`foo(1,2)`, `[a]`) and every term with a variable stay cell by cell on `$mkc`. Before it a literal list element cost two LIT_INTEGER boxes and a `$mkc` (181 lines of asm, 90 KB of compiler memory per element: hakank_swi_stable_marriage's two 100x100 matrices never finished compiling). Gate: `test_gate_prolog_ground_literal_is_one_box.sh`.
- **A single-sided rule (`Head => Body`, CEO-2128, 2026-11-04)** lowers to its clause with the head arguments unified one by one by the ordinary unify boxes inside an if-then-else: `'$ssu_begin'(S)` before the bound captures the variable creation serial counter, `'$ssu_check'(S)` after the unifications reads only the trail entries since the bound's mark and refuses the clause when a variable created before S was bound, aliased to another such variable, or aliased to the same fresh variable as another (lower_prolog.c `pl_ssu_lower_clause`, by_name_dispatch.c `rt_pl_dop_ssu_begin_c`/`rt_pl_dop_ssu_check_c`); the guard then `!` then the body follow, and a head that matches nothing throws `existence_error(matching_rule, _)`. subsumes_term/2 is never on the head's road: through it every `=>` library predicate walking a list paid the list per call (lists:max_member_/3 over 500k elements was quadratic).
- **Run-time goals** (`call/N`, a catch goal, the goal of findall, negation, an asserted body) are compiled at run time through the EVAL/CODE path and run as boxes; there is no solver.
- **Unification mirrors the SNOBOL4 matcher** (Lon 2026-09-26, CEO-1279: "The Prolog unification needs to mirror SNOBOL4's pattern matching, using R13, R14, and R15, and also R12 as a stack."): a binding is a conditional assignment pushed on the r12 stack and undone from it on backtracking; the trail is the CAS twin.

## 2. The register plane and the trail (as built, `src/runtime/rtx/rtx_plunify.s`, `src/runtime/rt/pl_trail_layout.h`)

- **r12 = TR**, the trail top. The trail is one arena aligned to its size (`PL_TR_ARENA_LG2` 29), so `r12 & PL_TR_ARENA_MASK` is the arena's header (`PL_TR_HEADER_BYTES` 160): the ball slot, the wake slot, the cleanup record, the floor, the alarm words. Entries are 32 bytes. The trail and the choice records are one of the two r12 islands Lon keeps off the RSP/RBP stack; no movable DESCR lives in an island.
- **THE TRAIL'S SIZE (CTO-638, 2026-11-12, on officer_05's rung-4 ask, row puzzles-red-hakank-swi-euler24)**: the arena is already reserved and committed on demand (`ct_alloc_in` maps it `MAP_NORESERVE`; MEASURED 17:2x CST, a four-line Prolog program reading /proc/self/status: VmSize 4.48 GB, VmRSS 54 MB under the 1 GB reservation), so a larger trail costs address space, not memory, and the mask form stays: r12 alone finds the header in every engine and continuation with no switch code. REJECTED: a header reached by a g_runtime pointer (every engine and continuation switch would swap it, and every header read becomes a load); conditional trailing and trail tidying (the choice points are live, `$ssu_check` reads every entry since its mark, CEO-1279 pushes every binding). The mask form tops out at `PL_TR_ARENA_LG2` 31, the last size whose mask, low mask and overflow compare each fit a sign-extended imm32. The steps: (1) LG2 30 now (33554431 entries; the literals in `pl_trail_layout.h`, `rtx_plunify.s`, `x86_call.h`, `rt_pl_trail.c`'s static_assert and `util_gc_callee_saved_census.py` move together, and the witness `trail_17200k.pl` with `WITNESSES.tsv` line 10 moves past the new cap); (2) the arena reserves exactly its size, aligned (today `rt_pl_tr_init` and `rt_pl_tr_new_arena` reserve twice its size to align; at LG2 31 that is 4 GB, a trivial Prolog program would read about 7.5 GB VmSize, and `util_precarve_whack_probe.sh` runs the smokes under `ulimit -v` 8 GB, the one address-space cap left in the harness, which `util_mem_scope.py` rules a cgroup's job); (3) LG2 31: at the witness's measured 116 bytes per binding (2.0 GB resident at 17.2M) the leanest program meets every scope's 8 GB MemoryMax at about the 67M-entry cap, so memory, not the trail, bounds a program. A shift-form header read above 31 is not cut until a measured program needs it.
- **r13 = B**, the choice point and cut barrier; saved at a fence (`bb_bound.cpp`).
- **r15 = the ball** (a heap pointer while armed); `PL_BALL_ARM`, `PL_BALL_DROP` and `PL_BALL_GET` write and read it together with the header's ball slot. A ball propagates ω by ω to the innermost `catch/3` gate, which restores its planned depth (`x86_pl_ball_rule_omega`, `bb_gate.cpp`, `rt_pl_catch_handle_c`). This is the model the one error protocol generalises (skill `runtime-calls`).
- **r14** is not a Prolog-wide pin today; the leaf uses it as scratch inside unification.
- **A deep C walker raises resource_error(stack), never ERROR 246** (officer_04 2026-11-12, row pl-rungs-red-resource-r05-deep-compare-caught, on junior_01's and senior_03's measurements; gdb on SCRIP origin: compare of two 200000-deep terms faults in `plr_more` 28 frames down, `g_rt_misc.stack_refuse_armed` 0). The walkers built by `PLR_WALK1` and `PLR_WALK2` (`pl_rational.h`) grow their explicit stacks in C-stack segments and already ask `rt_stack_refuses` (`rt_stack_overflow.c`) before each segment, but the refusal answers only while it is armed. So every top entry of a deep walk arms it exactly as `rt_pl_unify_deep_top_c` (`bn_29_pl.c`) does: arm if not armed, walk, disarm, and on a refusal clear the flag and hand the call `rt_pl_cap_error()` through its ball, which `catch/3` sees as `error(resource_error(stack), _)`. A deep walker that recurses in plain C instead is first written over `PLR_WALK1` or `PLR_WALK2`; the SIGSEGV road (`rt_overflow_resume_at_catch`) resumes only from emitted code and never unwinds a C frame.

## 3. Placement in the Prolog regime (the one decider)

Every Prolog graph is a home-frame graph (`home_frame` in `pl_graph_stamp`; Lon, CEO-1945: "keep it whacked and fix the misplaced ones by changing that carve logic to be proper"). A cell leaves the spine only for Lon's three reasons (CEO-1942): a generator's resumption, a retry after an unknown number of pushes, a recursion. The decider is the unbounded-excursion test, one function: `planner_span_retains(g, from, to)` in `src/planner/zeta_plan.cpp`, over `zd_edge_retains` (codes `PR_UNKNOWN_CALLEE`, `PR_GATE_HOLDS`, `PR_DISJUNCTION_HOLDS`, `PR_DB_GENERATOR`, `PR_RETAINING_TARGET`), `IR_SUSPEND` and a retaining disjunction (`zls_dj_retains`). A second copy of that test anywhere is a defect. The order is classify every graph, then plan, then lay out; the frame is sized by its resident words, never by keys.

## 4. Continuations and threads

- **`reset/3` and `shift/1`** are one-shot coroutines on the generator-procedure road: prelude clauses embedded in `src/parsers/prolog/prolog_parse.c` over the `$co_*` leaves (`rt_pl_dop_co_*` in `by_name_dispatch.c`), each coroutine on its own stack, r12 crossing every switch, the state in `scrip_coctx_t`. A continuation failing back across its trail floor raises `permission_error(backtrack, continuation, ...)` where swipl backtracks: the one recorded divergence.
- **Threads, engines and the toplevel are excluded** (Lon, CEO-1766: "Put the multi-threaded Prolog work as excluded for now."): thread cases leave the denominator as `THREADS_EXCLUDED_BY_LON`. The recorded design (one baton, real pthreads, preemption at the poll) is in `reference.md` § 4.

A clause the Prolog db loads at run time is lowered as a fragment whose graph is then freed, so its compile must call `fc_tables_reset` (it bumps `fcn_gen`) beside `zls_reset`: the planner's fcn table is keyed by node address, and without the reset the next fragment's nodes reuse the dead graph's addresses, read its stale facts, and the same clause plans differently from run to run (`runtime_eval.c` does this; `bb_pat_build.cpp` does the same for patterns).

## 7. DESIGN: clause-index (officer_01, 2026-11-12, on the rung-3 escalation of row rosetta-prolog-red-eulers-sum-of-powers-conjecture)

The clause step's switch (`em_07_sh.cpp` near line 493 decides it, `xa_pl_switch` in `src/templates/xa/xa_flat.cpp` emits it) keys the FIRST head argument only, compares key by key, and is ABANDONED for a tag class holding more than 64 distinct keys (`_nk > 64` takes the full chain). MEASURED (officer_01, 2026-11-12 18:xx CST, SCRIP 7dd357ecc, mode 3, `perf stat -e instructions:u` over 100,000 failure-driven calls of static facts `p(I, I^5)`, each less the same fact table with no call): 60 facts, arg1 bound 5.1e7 instructions (513 a call); 249 facts, arg1 bound 1.14e9 (22x for 4.2x the facts: the cut), arg2 bound 2.85e9 (a linear walk that binds and unwinds arg1 at every clause). A STATIC predicate takes this switch. An asserted one takes the db store, whose own index planes (argument 1, and argument 2 built on first demand since SCRIP d43fe4efb) serve it: eulers-sum-of-powers-conjecture, whose pow5/2 is asserted, went from 106.6 s to 43.6 s user at 57e7d9c99 (measured, officer_01) with no change to this switch. The cure, two steps, both inside the template and the emitter, no runtime leaf, no IR kind:
- **A. no key cap: a sorted switch.** A tag class with more than 8 keys emits a balanced compare tree over the keys sorted by value (`cmp`, `je` the arm, `jl` and `jg` the halves; 8 compares for 249 keys) in place of the linear run; the `_nk > 64` cut is deleted (the `n_alt <= 4096` bound stays). The targets (one candidate, a set, fail) are unchanged.
- **B. any argument: a chain of switches.** The emitter computes the key column of EVERY head argument position (the same reading of the alternative's first `UNIFY_CONST` or `UNIFY_STRUCT` on that parameter, `pnames[i]`, `pso` of parameter i) and emits one switch per position that keys at least one clause, in argument order. A switch whose argument is unbound, or a type its keys do not cover, or a key every clause admits (code -2), jumps to the NEXT position's switch instead of the chain; the last position's fallback is the chain. Each switch's labels and set tables are its own (`%s_switch%d`, `%s_ixdet%d_%d`, `%s_ixset%d_%d`); a set's trampolines walk only that position's candidates, which stays sound because a clause with a variable in that position is in every one of its sets. The first bound position with keys decides, as SWI's JIT does on its first indexable argument; a deeper combination (two arguments at once) is not cut until a measured program needs it.
- **The cto's three conditions (review 2026-11-12 18:3x CST; the design stands with them):** (1) every key's candidate list carries the clauses with a variable at that argument, interleaved in source order, so an argument-2 lookup answers the same solutions in the same order as the chain (`_setcode` already builds a set that way; a witness with variable-headed clauses between keyed ones on argument 2 proves it against swipl); (2) the switch serves STATIC predicates only, so no assert or retract can leave a compiled switch stale (an asserted predicate is the db store's, above); (3) step A's compare tree is built inside each tag class (DT_I, DT_PLATOM, DT_PLREF, as the switch dispatches today) and keys by type before value, so an integer 1, a float 1.0 and an atom never share a key, as the WAM switch_on_constant does.
- **Proof:** the row prolog-clause-index-any-argument, whose DONE-WHEN builds the three lookups above in scratch and reads green when arg1 at 249 facts is within 2x of 60 facts and arg2 within 2x of arg1, both as instruction counts less the overhead (red at 7dd357ecc: 22x and 2.5x); eulers-sum-of-powers-conjecture re-graded in mode 3; `test_feature_reps.sh prolog assert`, `test_smoke_prolog.sh` and the other seven smokes (the template is shared by every flat chain). A cure that trades programs never lands.
- **The DYNAMIC database picks its more selective argument** (senior_07, 2026-11-13, row trealla-more-red-samples-indexing-shared-arg; MEASURED: Trealla's indexing_shared_arg sample, 10,000 clauses `record(shared, I, value(I))` and 100,000 calls `record(shared, 777, value(777))`, cost 3 ms a call, about 300 s, because every call walked the 10,000-clause bucket of the shared first argument): the db's index (`pl_ix_t` in `un_head.h`, `un_09_pl.c`) holds two positions, arguments 1 and 2, and each entry counts its clauses (`pl_ix_ent_t.n`, an upper bound while erased clauses await reclaim). `pl_ix_pick` takes the first-argument key as before, but when that bucket holds `PL_IX_MIN` or more clauses and the call also binds a second-argument key, it builds the second index if it can be (every clause has a keyable argument 2) and uses it when its bucket is smaller. A walk that changes position midway stays correct because every candidate passes the bound-argument filter and both chains run in database order. After it the sample's lookup phase costs 1.1 s of CPU. The entry still reads red on its declared 4096 KB stack (CEO-1353): the populate recursion leaves a choicepoint per frame and needs 10,240 KB in SCRIP, 4,336 KB in swipl (which itself fails at 4096k).

THE PRINCIPLE the steps share (skill `byrd-boxes` section 5.2; Lon 12:2x CST: "Is not the dynamic database really just like CODE/EVAL in SNOBOL4?"): code that arrives at run time is compiled once and then linked, never looked up. The audit of every construct, PROPER, PARTIAL or MISSING, is skill `byrd-boxes` section 5.1; Lon's go on all of it (12:1x CST): "let's get all those you suggest fixed, addressed."

1. **The entry into a dynamic predicate is linked.** Today the lowerer puts two guard leaves before every call of a dynamic predicate (`pl_db_leaf1` and `pl_db_leaf2` in `lower_prolog/lpl_01_pl.c`: `$db_nonempty` and `$db_alive`), and `rt_pl_dop_db_alive_c` (`runtime/lib/bn_05_pl.c`) is handed the predicate's name as a string, which `rt_pl_db_born_undef` parses: a lexer runs in the call path (about 10 of 100 instructions of a loop calling one asserted rule). The step: the store's header carries one state word (alive, non-empty, born undefined) that the store's writers keep; the call tests that word inline and takes ω or raises; no leaf is called and no name is read.
2. **A dynamic fact's head is unified by the box** (layout 3.10). Today `pl_db_data` (`lower_prolog/lpl_03_pl.c`) lowers a predicate of facts to an `IR_TO` named `db:trl:det` followed by an `IR_CALL` of `$db_data_unify`: `rt_pl_db_data_unify` (`un_09_pl.c`) matches the stored clause with the C unifier, and the generator's step `rt_pl_db_cand_next` recomputes the index key, the target's head and its bound arguments at every candidate (`pl_ix_pick`, `pl_db_target_head`, `pl_db_target_bound`). One call of an indexed asserted fact costs 5,388 instructions. The step: the generator computes the key once at α into its own cell and its step takes the key (`pl_db_cand_step`); a value leaf hands back the candidate's stored head (shared when ground, renamed when it holds a variable) and the box unifies it with the ordinary `$unify`. Whether a fact is instead compiled into a small blob when asserted, as SNOBOL4 compiles a pattern value built at run time, is ruled after both are measured: storing a fact costs about 61,600 instructions today.
3. **One unifier.** `$clause_unify` (clause/2, retract/1), `rt_pl_catch_handle_c` (the catcher), and every deterministic builtin handed an output variable (functor/3, arg/3, `=..`, copy_term/2, msort/2 and their like) return a value and the box unifies it (layouts 3.6, 3.9, 3.10). The C unifier of `runtime/unification/` goes when its last caller does.
4. **Meta-call by the functor's identity** (layout 3.8). Today `rt_pl_goal_resolve_c` (`runtime/lib/bn_26_sh.c`) formats a "name/arity" key and finds the procedure by `rt_proc_find` and `rt_proc_index_of` at every call: 66 of 100 instructions of a `call/N` loop are C and libc. The step: the goal's functor word indexes the procedure table and the box jumps through the entry.
5. **A ground literal is built once.** Today `rt_pl_mkground` rebuilds the term at every pass of the box (12 of 100 instructions of a negation loop over a three-element list). The step: the term is built when the unit is loaded and the box refers to it; a destructive builtin (`setarg/3`, `nb_setarg/3`) on a shared literal copies first (to be measured before the row is cut).
6. **`findall`, `bagof`, `setof` and the tables collect in boxes** (layouts 3.8, 3.11); copying a term stays a value service.
7. **One test in place of a predicate call.** Every cut and commit calls the prelude's `$scc_cut/0` and every construct that can bind calls `$wakeup/0`. Each becomes one inline test of a word (is a cleanup record above the barrier; is a wake pending) and the call only when the test says so.
8. **SNOBOL4's CODE.** A function whose body CODE compiled is entered through its entry cell, as a compiled function is, not by name through C (5 of 100 instructions in boxes today); its design is cut under skill `snobol4`.

`between/3` is NOT a step: it is already an `IR_TO` generator box (`--dump-ir`); the C share of the `between` failure loop measured above is `nb_getval`, `nb_setval` and the C unifier.
