## 1. The populations

- **The masters**: `corpus/tests/<lang>/` holds one flat container per language: `ALL.<ext>` (every entry, a CONTAINER, never a program), `ALL.ref` (each entry's ref, cut from the oracle; binary to git), `ALL.csv` (the index: entry, feature columns, declared sizes, `compile_args`, `oracle_args`), `PARTITION.tsv`, and `LADDER.tsv` for the construct ladder, beside the container (CIO-55). The one extraction authority is `corpus_suite_harness.py extract MASTER REF NAME OUT`.
- **corpus may hold any licence's test programs, GPL included** (Lon 2026-11-12 19:4x CST, verbatim: "So $/corpus is okay to contain GPL. correct? None of corpus will be contained in a product. Just SCRIP is the PRODUCT."): corpus is test data and never ships, and SCRIP alone is the product. A vendored program is copied into its package with its licence file beside it, so the class-A-only copying of CIO-92 does not bind corpus. A semicolon-ready Icon or Unicon copy lives in its package (skill unicon). A licence question about a corpus copy is answered here, never escalated.
- **The packages**: `corpus/packages/<lang>/<pkg>/` (vendored suites with their own tables, `EXCLUDED.tsv` with a reason per row, runners `harness/scripts/test_<lang>_<pkg>_suite.sh` taking `S4E_CHUNK=entry:<name>`).
- **The gates' test cases** (gates-zero; Lon 2026-11-12 16:0x CST, verbatim: "We want of course to keep test cases."): every program the retired `test_gate_*.sh` scripts carried is a file of `corpus/packages/<lang>/gate_cases/` (`<gate>__<file>`), its ref cut from the oracle by `util_build_package_suite.py --twice`, graded by the cycle as `gate-cases-<lang>`; rebus's 13 are kept as files, ungraded, rebus having no independent oracle.
- **A demo that is a container** (`corpus/demos/<lang>/<name>/ALL.csv`, the files form) records its readings and writes its row like a package, keyed by its folder (`progress_suite_for`; `util_score_row.py`'s `FROM_PROGRESS_PACKAGES`): TeX is `corpus/demos/pascal/tex`, 1/1 (Lon 18:0x: "I would just love to see IcnPacks, TeX, and RakDemo with a Run stamp that it ran from the oracle.").
- **Demos and benchmarks**: `corpus/demos/`, `corpus/benchmarks/` (the only trees with committed `.s`).

## 2. Grading one program (what a lander does)

```bash
python3 ../harness/scripts/corpus_suite_harness.py run ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.<ext> ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.ref --lang <lang> --modes m3,m4 --partition entry:<name>
S4E_CHUNK=entry:<name> bash ../harness/scripts/test_<lang>_<pkg>_suite.sh
bash ../harness/scripts/test_feature_reps.sh <lang> <feature>
bash ../harness/scripts/test_smoke_<lang>.sh
bash ../harness/scripts/s4e_test_request.sh <suite-key> <entry>...        # at most eight; the coo grades
```

`--modes m3,m4` is mandatory; the two modes may diverge and each is graded against the oracle. A program runs at its DECLARED sizes (the entry's heap and stack, CEO-1353/1281); the declared size is a hard cap and out of stack is a bigger declared `-s`, never data moved.

- **The cycle's inventory** (CIO-22, 2026-11-10): `python3 harness/scripts/util_cycle_inventory.py` prints one line per suite row: the container files (`ALL.<ext>`, `ALL.ref`, `ALL.csv`, `PARTITION.tsv`), entries in the table, the container and the cycle's cells, cell names the container lacks (the harness refuses that cell rc 2), entries no cell names, OWED, and the last finished lap's cells by exit code. `--owed KEY` lists a row's owed programs with the builder's reason; `--check` is the structural verdict, `--check-lap` adds the lap; a row whose every cell is held in `CYCLE.skip` is counted by neither. **OWED** is a program `ALL.omit` leaves out by the builder's own measurement that neither `EXCLUDED.tsv` nor `CONTAINERS.tsv` declares out: the package tables are the denominator (CIO-19), so it is cured by the attribute the deleted runner applied (stdin, `out_files`, `stderr`, a mask row, a CONTRACT key) and absorbed, or declared in `EXCLUDED.tsv` with its class and the row's total moved with a stamp; never left between the two. A package in the files form (`ALL.csv` has a `path` column) is a container, `F` in the inventory's S place. `util_declare_owed.py KEY [--write]` declares a row's closed classes from the builder's `ALL.omit` reasons, in `lib_excluded.sh`'s vocabulary, and leaves the curable ones. **AN EMPTY ANSWER IS NO CLOSED FACT UNTIL RE-MEASURED** (the cio 2026-11-12, measured twice: 34 snoflake programs the builder read EMPTY or DIED carried their stdin in an `@input` header it never fed, and all 34 answered with it; the grader found fpc's six the same): a NO-OUTPUT or EMPTY program is declared only after its arguments, stdin and goal are ruled out (`--declare-empty`). `ALL.csv`'s `out_files`, `stderr` and `timeout_s` are read by the builder before any oracle run and written back, as the grader reads them. The mine road (`util_build_package_suite.py --mine`, a package read in place) feeds a program its `ALL.stdin` line or `NAME.in` and passes its `ALL.args` arguments after the program, as the grader does; `--mine-under` names a folder or one program, and with `--mine-add` only what it names is cut again; a row cut again keeps its `heap_kb`, `stack_kb`, `timeout_s`, `compile_args` and every column the census does not write, such as `compare` (the cfo and the cio 2026-11-12: a folder re-cut had set `timeout_s` to the cap on 87 entries and re-entered trealla's `test_sockets` on its pre-socket line). The cycle (`harness/scripts/s4e_cycle.sh`) launches `master` and `pkg` rows of `SUITE_RUNNERS.tsv` only, rewrites a suite's row the moment any of its cells grades (section 9), never for a cell that refused, and re-executes itself at a lap start when the sync changed its text.
- **A chunk is about one minute** (Lon 2026-11-12 18:2x CST, verbatim: "We need the granularity of the test-suite chunk/partition to be small enough that each update is about every 1 MINUTE."; 18:0x: "The test-suite cycle runner better be GREASED LIGHTING. - **Stored partitions** (CEO-1676): `util_suite_partition.py build <ALL.csv>` writes the core (every distinctive feature covered three times), a shell per distinctive feature and chunks of at most 100 entries; `check` and `test_gate_suite_partitions_are_current.sh` refuse a table edited without its partition rebuilt. ## 4. The rules

- **A ref is cut from the oracle, never from our output; no XFAIL.** An oracle that refuses, crashes or hangs gives NO_ORACLE_ANSWER: the program is excluded with the measurement, never counted as debt (CEO-1696/1934). A wrong exclusion costs more than a wrong cure.
- **A landed cure waiting on a reading is graded by the coo at once**, the program run through its suite's own road on a clean root, so a seat at its two-claim cap closes and takes its next row (the coo 2026-11-12).
- **A red no exclusion class fits stays red as debt**, never a new class made to hide it (the coo 2026-11-12, on officer_01's unitests unicon/list-test: SCRIP prints 8 extra FAILED lines from implementation-defined storage that no mask can delete; Lon 2026-11-11: "Keep all the test suite numbers honest").
- **The denominator identity**: PASS + FAIL + every named exclusion class equals the population the tree ships; no board prints a pass count whose denominator drops programs without naming them.
- **A row exists only while a measurement says so** (CEO-1386); a test that cannot measure refuses rc 2 (CEO-1680); no planted breakage.
- **A benchmark is a test**: every kernel has a ref and is graded for correctness; its speed is an instruction count (skill `speed-measurement`).
- **The masters carry no `.s`**; tests carry no artifacts (CEO-16).

**DEFERRED is not OUTSIDE-BASELINE.** DEFERRED means the language is ours and the work is owed: the programs stay out of the red bucket while a CEO ruling schedules them, a debt. OUTSIDE-BASELINE means the oracle cannot run the program, a closed fact about the oracle. The two never merge. `DEFERRED.tsv` lives in `.github`, not beside the package, because a row lifts a score and the lane whose number it raises must not be able to write it; each row names its ruling and the work it waits on, or `test_gate_deferred_rows_name_a_ruling_and_the_work_they_wait_on.sh` refuses.

## 9. THE GRADER IS A MOVING SANDBOX (Lon 2026-11-12 18:0x-19:2x CST; the grader builds it, row `cycle-greased-lightning-grader`)

Lon, verbatim: "The test-suite cycle runner better be GREASED LIGHTING. Each unit of work should be very small relative so that many requests from seats can go through FAST." / "You do not have to report a test suite ALL at ONCE. you report each chunk at it comes, and each chunk is bite-size regardless of which suite it came from. You can then report two date/time stampes, the earliest , and the latest most recent. Do not show the run range anymore. The source base will move forward with all checked-in repo fixes on each turn. one chunk updates. rinse. repeat. THEN, when a request for a quick APPROVAL comes in, you take your base apply the one change for each seat that is asking for special BASE+SPECIAL checks. THEN Rinse and Repeat. This waiting around is GOING to STOP!!!" / "So the GRADER is a SAND BOX that keeps moving forward with the latest that and seat can check against, and the SCORE keeps ALWAYS moving forward. up and down like a YO YO." / "seats ae asking before they push, not after."

- **No lap is a unit and nothing waits for one.** The score moves with every chunk, up or down; a row is never frozen until a suite or a lap finishes.
- **Every chunk reports at once** (LANDED, harness 887b8a727): a cell that graded (rc 0 or 1) rewrites its suite's row from the progress store the moment it ends, in the background, one lock a suite; a cell at rc 2 writes nothing. A row shows two stamps, **Earliest** and **Latest** (the box-clock MM-DD HH:MM of its oldest and newest reading), never a run range (`run_stamps` in `util_suite_banner.py`, the compact banner too).
- **Two lanes, requests first** (LANDED, harness b6e0e0282): a cell whose last reading took over `S4E_CYCLE_SLOW_SECS` (600) runs in the slow lane of `S4E_CYCLE_SLOW` slots (2), every other cell and every seat's request in the fast lane of `S4E_CYCLE_FAST` (8); a free fast slot takes a pending request before any lap cell. CELL lines end `lane=f|s`. The graded-runs slice is 10 cores (the coo, BUDGET.tsv row graded-runs).
- **The base moves forward every turn** (DESIGN, not built): each chunk starts on the newest checked-in base, a snapshot of origin with its build fetched from the build cache; a running chunk keeps its own snapshot, so a sync never throws a chunk away ("THE BINARY MOVED UNDER THIS BOARD" ends). A snapshot is deleted when no chunk holds it.
- **A quick approval is BASE+SPECIAL, before the push** (DESIGN, not built): a seat asks before it pushes. Its change is a local commit in its worktree, fetched from that path (`git fetch /tmp/s4e/<seat>/wt-<name>/<repo> <commit>`; no push, no origin), or an uncommitted diff the request carries (`git apply --3way`). The grader puts it on the current base in a throwaway worktree (`git cherry-pick`, a seat's commits in order), builds, grades the chunks the request names, answers the seat with the verdict and the base, and deletes the worktree. A change that does not apply is answered at once with the files and the base, so the seat rebases; nothing is graded. The seat lands through `s4e_land.sh` only on a green answer.
- **The unit shrinks to one program** (DESIGN, not built): one warm harness worker (`corpus_suite_harness.py serve`) pins once and grades programs fed to it one at a time, so the 4.4 s launch is paid once a worker; then a program whose last reading took over 60 s goes to the slow lane.
- **A probe of the cycle runs sandboxed**: `S4E_CYCLE_PROG`, `S4E_CYCLE_TMP`, `S4E_PROGRESS_DB`, `S4E_RESULTS`, `S4E_SUITES_TSV`, `S4E_POST`, `S4E_POSTOFFICE`, `S4E_TEST_REQUEST_DIR` and `S4E_SCORE_MD` all pointed at scratch, on a worktree whose SUITE_RUNNERS.tsv is trimmed; the live store, table, postoffice and queue are never touched.
