## 7. Rulings a landing must not break (receipts in GOAL-SNOBOL4-100.md, GOAL-SNOCONE-100.md and GOAL-CEO.md)

- **SCRIP follows SPITBOL semantics and is CASE-SENSITIVE**; one oracle, `-bf` always; no `--compat`, no `-F`.
- **The three zetas are the model** (Lon s81: RSP the spine, RBP the activation frames and the standing area; skill `scrip-memory-model`): a pattern box's result and locals default to the RSP spine and move to an RBP frame only where unbounded growth can intervene between its γ and its β; the match frame is established once at `S ? P`; the conditional-assignment stack is the r12 island (CEO-1542/1543, one of the two stacks outside RSP/RBP); a retry never restores RSP (`test_gate_no_rsp_reset_at_beta.sh`); the frame whack of a match end is a pop the plan row states (skill `scrip-memory-model` § 5).
- **Every pattern node is a four-port box wired at compile time**; no per-op filter inside a BB family (a defect reachable through one member is a class defect); no C function enters a box (CEO-1576); the rtx leaves are asm on the rtcc ABI with no veneer.
- **The seven `.sc` parsers are never rewritten** (Lon 2026-09-24); a bug is a few characters; the transpile-then-SPITBOL-then-monitor order is the method.
- **Snocone is C surface syntax over SPITBOL semantics**: comments are `//` and `/* */`, subscripts are `a[i,j]` (square brackets only: `A<1>` is out by ruling), alternation `|`, and concatenation is whitespace as in SPITBOL (the 2026-09-16 `&&` spelling and the C comparison sugar were removed on Lon's word of 2026-10-02 and are syntax errors in both parsers; § 10); the paper governs the score's census, the SPITBOL operator set and skill `snocone-parser-patterns` how SCRIP spells it. Snocone spells things twice (`function`/`procedure`, `DATA(`/`struct`): a census names both spellings or says there is one.
- **A user function called inside a deferred capture target** and the deferred selection/ABORT shapes are the known classes under all seven parsers (GOAL-SNOCONE-100); a cure lands for the class.
- **CODE/EVAL** compile at run time through `runtime_eval.c` under the one granted global (CEO-1271); every EVAL witness grows about 132 KB at any window under 256 KB, so a tiny-arena verdict on one names the window. LOAD/UNLOAD/SAVE/EXEC are a FEATURE, not an error-routing sliver (Lon 2026-08-27); PHI (the regex engine exposed to SNOBOL4) and Λ (code embedded in the pattern) are ruled future features.
- **Beauty is its own oracle** (Lon 2026-08-16): `beauty.sno < beauty.sno` in both modes is byte-identical to the input file; no md5 is ever pinned; if the output differs the FILE is wrong and is re-cut.
- A grammar edit is invisible until `regenerate_parser_and_lexer_from_sources.sh` runs; a cure that trades one program for another never lands (CEO-589); a SNOBOL4 emitter or lowerer change regenerates the benchmark and demo `.s` in the coo's maintenance pass; the cto's ZSM frame census floor moves only on the cto's ruling.

## 10. Snocone, the language (verified 2026-11-01 against `src/parsers/snocone/snocone_lex.c` and `snocone_parse.y`)

- **What it is**: SPITBOL expressions (the SPITBOL manual v3.7, chapters 15, 17, 18 and 19, are authoritative for what an expression means) with C-style structured control flow and newline-as-whitespace. A SPITBOL program that does not use `&&`, `||` or `%` as binary operators and writes `;` terminators and `name:` labels runs unchanged.
- **Concatenation is whitespace**: the lexer emits `T_CONCAT` between two value-yielding tokens separated by white space or a comment, except before a token that may be a binary operator. Inside a condition, boolean AND is juxtaposition and OR is `|`.
- **The zero-space rule**: `f(x)` with no space is a call (`T_CALL`); `f (x)` is `f` concatenated with `(x)`.
- **`&name`** with no space is a keyword reference (`T_KEYWORD`).
- **Statements end at `;`**; newlines are white space (a missing `;` gives a surprising parse, not an error); a physical line whose column 1 is `+` or `.` continues the previous one. A bare `expr;` that fails falls through to the next statement.
- **Comments** `//` to end of line and `/* */` (non-nesting), both white space for concatenation. **Strings** in `'` or `"`, no escapes.
- **Identifiers** start with a letter, `_` or a non-ASCII byte and continue with letters, digits, `_` and `.` (the lexer's `is_idcont`); case-sensitive byte for byte.
- **Conditions** of `if`, `while`, `do ... while`, `for` and `switch` are SPITBOL backtracking expressions: success and failure drive the branch; `if (x)` on a bound name always succeeds.
- **Control flow**: `if`/`else`, `while`, `do { } while (c);`, `for (init; test; step)`, `switch (e) { case v: ...; default: ...; }` (no fall-through), `break`, `continue` (each optionally labelled), `goto LABEL`, `LABEL: stmt`; `procedure` or `function` (both `T_DEFINE`), `return`, `freturn`, `nreturn`; `struct` (DATA); `import` (a library linked into the caller's namespace, skill `snocone-parser-patterns`); compound assignments `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, `/=`, `^=`.
- **Removed by ruling** (Lon 2026-10-02): `==`, `!=`, `<`, `>`, `<=`, `>=`, the `:==:` family, `::`, `:!:` and `&&` are syntax errors in both parsers, as under `sbl -bf`; angle-bracket subscripts are out, and a package program that spells them is EXCLUDED, class ANGLE_SUBSCRIPTS_RULED_OUT_BY_LON (Lon 2026-11-12 11:2x CST: "SInce we are not storing vanilla inside our corpus repo then mark programs with angle-bracketed arrays as EXCLUDED."; vanilla wordsize, its licence forbidding a rewrite). Kept: SCRIP's non-ASCII identifier letters in SNOBOL4 and Snocone (Lon: "keep the extension"), and SCRIP's C parser's expression tree is the canonical form. Gate: `test_gate_snocone_expressions_are_snobol4_expressions.sh`. The Snocone binary OPSYN operators (`'x' & 'y'`) parse like SNOBOL4's and dispatch through OPSYN (the row `snocone-binary-opsyn-operators-...` is DONE).
- **Alternative evaluation** `(e1, e2, e3)` (a SPITBOL form): evaluated left to right, the value of the first that succeeds; all failing, it fails. It is the replacement for `||`.
- **Not in Snocone**: binary `&&` (whitespace concatenates), `||` (use alternative evaluation), `%` (use `REMDR`; `%` is an OPSYN slot), `do ... until`, `then`, two-word `go to`, the goto field `:S(L)F(M)` (the `:` is always a label suffix), a newline token. Both `procedure` and `function` are accepted (`T_DEFINE`).
- **On record, not designed** (Lon 2026-10-02, CEO-1439: "Have a switch statement with specified prefix generate SNOBOL4 labels and a IN-DIRECT GOTO. And have a seperate \"match statement\" which has the cascading match sequence ..."): a PREFIXED switch whose cases become generated SNOBOL4 labels `<prefix>v` reached by one indirect goto (so a case value that is no Snocone identifier is still a real label), and a MATCH statement as an ordered cascade of `pattern = replacement` arms. Open for Lon: the prefix's spelling and the `default:` test (a goto to an undefined label is an error in SPITBOL), and whether the cascade stops at the first match or runs every arm. The older open idea beside it is a compile-time choice between an if-chain and a label table for a large string switch.

## 11. Two pattern laws (from the engine page's pattern-choice and passthru sections)

- **Every β re-derives the cursor (r14) absolutely, from its own knowledge**: a relative box's arithmetic reversal and an absolute mover's restore from its cell were two disciplines whose mix reversed from the wrong value; one absolute rule removes the class. FENCE cannot be re-entered from outside its own activation.
- **The namespace law**: the deferred-pattern road (`*P`) touches a GVA cell only for a real, mutable user variable, by index; a match-time string lookup, or a synthetic name interned beside the user's names, is pollution. As built (2026-11-01): the synthetic names still exist (`EXPR$`, `PAT$`, `SCV$`, `SNO$VL$`) and are kept out of the GVA by `gva_name_hidden` (`src/optimizer/gva_collect.c`); the law's goal, anonymous handles that are never named cells, is not reached.
