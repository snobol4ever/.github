# Seat conduct

The rules every seat is held to that are about the seat's own behaviour, not about a language or a layer. Each was paid for at least three times in the three-month history (DESIGN-PLAN skills-complete, the cio 2026-11-07, the history audit: fourteen recurring problem classes; eight of thirteen friction reports of 2026-11-04 named the kill and background rules). The law is the skills (RULES.md was triaged into them and retired, Lon 2026-11-11); this page is that law for a seat's own conduct with its sources. The bus, the rows and the sitting are skill `fleet-bus`; the box and its pools are skill `shared-box`.

## 1. Processes

- **Kill by pid, and only pids you spawned.** Never `pkill -f <pattern>`, never `pgrep` a pattern another seat shares: the pattern matched the caller's own shell three times (COO-341, COO-343, COO-432) and other seats' runs (Lon 2026-09-13, RULES.md FACT RULE). `/home/harness/scripts/s4e_kill_mine.sh --handle <h> | --pid <pid>` kills what is yours with its children.
- **An orphan belongs to its seat.** A process reparented to the user manager with its parent gone is an orphan; the coo's fleet watch names it to its seat once an hour and never kills it (COO-462 ORPHAN_KILL=0, COO-463); the seat kills it by pid or says in one line that it is wanted. Orphans from killed parents filled a scope and stalled the box 2026-11-01 14:4x.
- **Background work only through a handle.** A job that outlives a tool call runs under `setsid` with its log and handle under `/tmp/s4e/<seat>/bg/`, reaped with the session; a bare `&` or a `sleep` left behind is the orphan of the rule above (the cio's own `sleep 3600`, 2026-11-07 09:12).
- **No seat but the coo runs a loop.** `/loop`, ScheduleWakeup and CronCreate are the coo's alone, and the coo's only when Lon starts it for an unattended fleet (Lon 2026-11-07 10:5x, verbatim: "It seems that seats believe it is okay to perform /loop command. That is forbidden and reserved only occasionally for COO when I run the fleet un-attended."; 2026-11-05 15:1x: "The COO is the engine with the /loop. The only seat with a loop."). Every other root's `.claude/settings.json` denies the two tools, except the cio's while Lon has the cio looping alone on the fleet redesign (Lon 2026-11-07 12:0x: "You better lift that ... now!!!"). A seat waiting on its own build or run parks its row `MEASURING:<seat>:<pid>` and stops; the bus or the coo wakes it (skill `fleet-bus` section 2). **A long poll is not a loop** (the cio's ruling, 2026-11-07 15:0x, on worker_05's ask; fleet-control step 12): when the Stop gate sends a live-set seat with nothing ready to `bash /home/harness/scripts/s4e_msg.sh next --wait 240`, the seat runs it: that is the sitting's `next` holding the reserve open on the tube for 240 s, a wait on the queue (THE LADDER: a seat works what the queue serves it), not a scheduled wake, not `/loop`, not cron; it is the one way a seat stays on the queue without a message, and the gate sends it back as often as the tube is empty.
- **One process per identity.** The bus identity (`S4E_SEAT`) is held by one process; a bus verb holds the lock only for its write, never across a DONE-WHEN or a PREMISE run (CTO-502, fleet-queue step 19); a scheduled routine never runs in a seat root.
- **Heavy work goes through the doors.** A compile takes a cc slot through the Makefile (never a bare gcc), a run takes a run slot (`util_run_slot.sh`, the runners), a census or a sweep takes the batch class (`util_batch.sh`); nothing caps a seat but the one limit, `cores=N` of `CORES.conf` on `s4e.slice` (skill `shared-box` section 3, THE ONE LIMIT; the box-seatcap of 2026-11-07 is gone with every other quota). Speed is an instruction count; a wall clock under load measures nothing (skill `speed-measurement`).

## 2. Files and trees

- **NO SEAT WORKTREES: A SEAT WORKS IN ITS OWN ROOT** (Lon 2026-11-13 17:4x CST, in the coo's window, verbatim: "Hmm, so ever seat has a work tree, dumb ass!!! Hence what you are doing is STUPID!!! I want you to work in /home/claude folder. We do not NEED duplication. Nope, dups not needed. What are you using /tmp folder for?"; row harness-a-worktree-is-scrip-alone-everything-else-from-the-seats-root, officer_02). A seat edits SCRIP, and any other repo it must, in its own root `/home/claude_<seat>`, commits there, and lands from there: `s4e_land.sh --row <topic>` (its home is the root by default) rebases the root's own commits onto origin, gates, pushes, then relinks the root's shared repos to the pristine (`util_sync_root.sh --relink`). `s4e_worktree.sh new` refuses rc 2 for every seat but the grader, whose cycle bases stay. `util_sync_root.sh` leaves a repo with local commits or tracked edits exactly as it is and names it `KEPT`, never stashing, resetting or merging over it; the landing brings it to origin. A dirty root is a seat's working tree, never a refusal: `next` serves the job and does not read its premise there, and a reading of a dirty tree still never enters the progress store (CEO-2060). `s4e_land.sh --probe-root-home` proves the landing and the sync on a scratch root. Outputs stay under `/tmp/s4e/<seat>/`, never under the root: run scratch and logs only, 4 GB, idle ten minutes, swept (COO-474).
- **A ROOT IS ITS SCRIP CLONE, `.claude` AND `CLAUDE.md`; IT HOLDS corpus, harness OR .github ONLY WHILE IT CARRIES A CHANGE TO IT** (the cio 2026-11-14, row harness-one-disk-step-6-the-cutover; Lon, in the cio's window, verbatim: "We are meant not to have folder .github, harness, and corpus in each /home/claude folder, but they have not been cleaned out. Complete the new disk/file layout."). The three live once, at `/home/corpus`, `/home/harness` and `/home/.github`, the grader's to keep at origin; nobody edits, commits or builds in them, and every tool is run by its full path, `bash /home/harness/scripts/<tool>`. To change one of the three: `bash /home/harness/scripts/s4e_take.sh corpus` (or `harness`, `.github`) makes the root's own clone at origin/main, its objects borrowed from the one-disk checkout; edit and commit there; land from the root as ever (`s4e_land.sh --row <topic>`), and the landing drops the clone once it stands clean at origin. While the root holds a clone every tool reads it first (`lib_paths.sh`, `harness_paths.py`: the environment, then the root's own clone, then the one-disk checkout), so a seat's runs grade its own change with nothing set. `s4e_take.sh list` names what a root holds and carries; `s4e_take.sh drop <repo>` removes a clone and refuses one holding an edit, a stash or a commit origin lacks. Proofs: `s4e_take.sh --probe`, `lib_paths.sh --selftest`, `util_root_sitting_check.sh ROOT SEAT` (a whole sitting in a root holding only its clone). **Every session in a root carries `S4E_HOME`, however it was opened** (the cio 2026-11-14, on the coo's measurement, harness f8dd621f6): the env block of the root's `.claude/settings.local.json` names `S4E_HOME` beside `S4E_SEAT`, written by `propagate_seat_digests_cto_484.py --settings` (its dry run reading `to-write=0` is the check), and a live session reads it at its next Bash call. MEASURED before it: a window opened by hand carried none, `util_sync_root.sh` refused, and a tool run from `/home/harness` with it unset read its root as `/home` (`S4E_SCRIP=/home/SCRIP`, a folder that does not exist). A fixture that needs it unset says so with `env -u S4E_HOME`. **The three one-disk checkouts refuse an edit, and the grader reads your clone** (the cio 2026-11-14, harness d66cf0577 and eadca9317; MEASURED the day of the cut: their files were writable and nothing refused, and 216 live batons name a `/home/corpus` or `/home/harness` path, rewritten that morning from the root-relative spelling, the originals in `/home/resources/cutover-saved-2026-11-14/batons-before-path-rewrite.tar.gz`): the skill gate answers an Edit, a Write or a shell write under `/home/corpus`, `/home/harness` or `/home/.github` rc 2 with the cure, whatever skill is loaded: take the repo, make the same edit in the root's own clone, land. While a root holds its own corpus clone, `corpus_suite_harness.py` reads a `/home/corpus/...` argument from that clone and says so in one `ONE-DISK:` line (`--probe-own-clone`), so a baton's grading command grades the seat's own change. A script that still spells a repo as a folder beside the root is a bug of the cutover: the forms the pattern census missed were awk string joins, `dirname` of `dirname`, `parent.parent.parent` and Python `S4E + "/harness/..."` (the cycle's cell list read 8 cells for 1092 on its first lap). What the roots held on the day of the cut is kept in `/home/resources/cutover-saved-2026-11-14` (every branch, stash and worktree head, each named in the list file at its top).
- **A taken clone's tracked files may be shared, read-only links** (the cio 2026-11-12; skill `shared-box` section 4): its clean files are hard links to one pristine. Never make one writable and never write one in place. The Edit tool and `sed -i` write a new inode, so the link breaks and the pristine is untouched (MEASURED, officer_02 2026-11-13); an in-place write (Python `open` with a or w, shell `>>`, `util_build_package_suite.py` and `util_suite_partition.py` writing ALL.csv and PARTITION.tsv) fails EACCES: make that file the root's own first: `cp f f.own && chmod u+w f.own && mv f.own f`. The chmod is of the root's own new copy only, never of a linked file. `cp -p` keeps the read-only mode, so the write still fails (MEASURED 2026-11-13: junior_01's util_add_ladder_witness.py on /home/corpus/tests/raku/ONE-LINERS.raku, and officer_08 needed ONE-LINERS.* and MULTI-LINERS.md too). Commit, land from the root; the lander's sync relinks the files once they equal origin.
- **An upstream checkout is never touched** (Lon 2026-11-12 19:0x CST, in the coo's window, verbatim: "Ensure you NEVER touch and upstream repo. EVER!" / "So we are clear, up-stream repo to me means a third-party repo we have cloned into /home resources." / "I suppose you could build one in place. I see no problem there."): an upstream repo is a third-party repository cloned into `/home/resources`. Its tracked files are never edited, and no git operation of any kind runs in it (no checkout, reset, stash, commit, clean or pull). Building in place is allowed: the build output a compiler or the oracle leaves beside the sources (XSB's `.xwam`, fpc's `.o`) is fine. A conversion, such as the semicolons a SCRIP Icon program needs, is a copy into corpus, never an edit of the original. Even a look writes: a plain `git status` refreshes `.git/index` (the coo's audit sweep of 2026-11-12 19:03 rewrote the index of five upstream Prolog clones, as officer_04 measured), so a read of an upstream clone is `git --no-optional-locks status`, or `find`, never a bare git verb.
- **The worktrees made before 2026-11-13 drain** (`s4e_worktree.sh rm <name>`, `list`, `sweep`; the top `/home/resources/worktrees`, legacy `/tmp/s4e`): remove one once its landing is on origin, after `git rev-list origin/main..HEAD` and `git status --porcelain` read empty in each repo, or keep its work with `rm --save` (the patch goes to the shared escalations folder and the row's LEDGER). The sweep never removes a worktree whose land lock is held (officer_04 2026-11-12, harness 40fc495b1), one holding a commit not on origin, one a live process's cwd or command line names (the coo 2026-11-13, on senior_01's measurement), and removes one with nothing unlanded once idle 30 minutes (`--probe-sweep-land`). `WORKTREES.conf` (`max`, `free_gb`, `top`) still bounds the grader's bases.
- **Generated tables are regenerated, never edited**; a rebase conflict in a registered generated output is resolved by regeneration (`s4e_land.sh` step 19, CTO-491); the skill gate refuses an edit of one outright.
- **The coo root's objects serve every other root** (`util_sync_root.sh`, alternates): no seat ever runs `git gc`, `git prune`, `git repack -d` or `git gc --prune` in a `/home/claude_coo/<repo>`; `gc.auto=0` stands there; a move, rename or re-clone of the coo root is a fleet event the cio rules on first (the cio 2026-11-07 09:1x).
- **Fold commits onto the base you started from, never onto a moved origin** (the grader 2026-11-12, MEASURED: harness 6f4e694ed was folded with `git reset --soft origin/main` after a fetch, so the commit carried the worktree's old copies of SLOTS.tsv and lib_slots.sh and reverted the coo's 8217eefc on origin until d68d7e4a7): squash with `git reset --soft "$(git merge-base HEAD origin/main)"`, read `git show --stat` before landing, and let `s4e_land.sh` rebase.
- **Cross-repo landings are one action through `s4e_land.sh`**: code repos first, `.github` last; never half a landing pulled or pushed; a hand push outside the lease is recorded by `s4e_push_rejected.sh` (skill `fleet-bus` section 7).

## 3. The record

- **The chat is not the record.** A ruling with a lasting idea lands in the skill it governs, on origin, the same sitting (the GOAL files are deleted, Lon 2026-11-11); what you did goes in the baton's LEDGER as you go; a condition told to a seat and never written was lost twice (RULES.md's two-modes note, CEO-935; hq_pascal's friction report 2026-11-04).
- **Every statement is MEASURED (the command beside it) or a HYPOTHESIS, marked**; a number with no command is a hypothesis whoever wrote it (the provenance law, skill `fleet-bus` section 4).
- **Time labels come from `date`, read before the label** (COO-194; the cio's two-hour drift of 2026-11-05); a label is `HH:Mx CST`.
- **A telegram is the record and wakes nobody; a SendMessage is a pointer** (CTO-469). `send` refuses a backtick or a command substitution in the body: write the command's name in words. An empty inbox is the acknowledgement; read, act or reply, clear.
- **No subagents in the repos** (Lon 2026-11-05); a reader spawned for a measurement runs read-only and its load is the seat's (section 1).
