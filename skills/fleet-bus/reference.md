### Bus detail, verbatim from SKILL.md section 2 before the 2026-11-07 trim


`next claim unclaim park done assign reown mint recut queue expire rule resume ask send check clear mailbox sweep board banner fleet whoami premise`. Run bus verbs one at a time with absolute paths; never pipe one into a short read; a send during a bus lock delivers nothing. Unread mail is announced once per arrival: the six-line heading names only new messages, then one line (`N unread`; after `check`, `N read, not cleared`) until the next arrival (step 37).

- **next** is a reserve over your own tubes (`seat-<me>`, `ask-<tier>`, `tier-<tier>`; never the tier below, COO-481), then a legacy ASSIGNED row, then your unfinished claim; a worker is served no FREE row. A seat holds at most two live claims (CEO-2078); an ASSIGNED row not yet running does not count.
- **queue** `<topic> --tier fable|opus|sonnet|haiku | --to <seat>` (fleet-queue, 2026-11-05): moves a FREE or QUEUED row into a tier tube or a seat tube (its one job put, owner `@<tier>` or the seat, state `QUEUED:<tube>`); a sonnet or haiku tier needs a `SKILLS:` line in the baton head. This is how an officer or a company man feeds the tier below from the backlog.
- **mint** `<topic> [rank] [--owner <seat> | --tier <tier> | --to <seat>] --stdin` is the only way to add work: a GOAL with its own `DONE-WHEN:` and `PREMISE-WHEN:` lines. A topic needs a language prefix or `--owner`. Mint refuses a prose criterion.
- **done** `<topic>` runs the DONE-WHEN and refuses a red; the claim is untouched on a refusal. A criterion over `S4E_DONE_TIMEOUT` refuses rc 2 (could not measure). `S4E_DONE_OVERRIDE="why"` is loud and written into the claim.
- **park** `<topic> <STATE>`: `PARKED-AWAITING:<request-id>` (waiting on the coo's reading of the test request whose id `s4e_test_request.sh` printed; refused unless the request is pending or graded; when its verdict lands in `test_requests/done/` the row returns to the request's seat as `ASSIGNED:<seat>` with a ledger line and a telegram, by `next` or the sweep -- step 31) or `PARKED-AWAITING:<topic>` (waiting on another row, as `BLOCKED-ON`), `BLOCKED-ON:<topic>` (waiting on another row; `park` records a `RESTORE-STATE:` line on the baton, and when that row's `done` runs the blocked row returns to it -- ASSIGNED:<seat> for a parked claim or assignment, else FREE -- with a ledger line and a telegram, `DONE unblocked=N`; step 35), `PARKED-LON-HOLD:<why>`, `GRANT-NEEDED:<why>`; a row made moot by a landing is retired by its owner or a boss with `SUPERSEDED:<the landing that made it moot>` or `RETIRED:<why>` (the sweep skips both; the baton gets the STATE line). `MEASURING:<seat>:<pid>` (your own live measurement: accepted while the pid is alive and yours; the Stop gate passes the row while it lives and blocks the moment it ends; the sweep reverts a dead-pid row to ASSIGNED).
- **claim notices** (step 21): a claim tells only the seats whose live rows touch a path the claimed row touches, by prefix, from the batons' `TOUCHES:` lines (else the `Landing files:` words); a row with no `TOUCHES:` tells nobody and the claim says so. - **the Stop gate** (step 10): a seat holding unread mail or a row ASSIGNED, CLAIMED or MEASURING-with-a-dead-pid cannot end its turn; its hook continues it with the row and its NEXT (12 continuations an hour). Park a row before waiting on it.

A ruling with a lasting idea is an edit of the skill it governs, landed like any change; the seats it binds get a telegram citing the landing hash, and the row's baton gets its LEDGER line.

## 4. The rules every row obeys

- **The provenance law**: every statement is MEASURED (the command beside it) or a HYPOTHESIS, marked. A number with no command is a hypothesis, whoever wrote it.
- **A row exists only while a measurement says so** (CEO-1386/553/555): a DONE-WHEN a deletion satisfies is false; a DONE-WHEN is proven red once and green once at mint (the mint receipt names both witnesses); a test that cannot measure refuses rc 2 (CEO-1680).
- **A row name states the symptom, never the hypothesis**; a lane is decided by the cure, never by a noun in the name.
- **The chat is not the record**: a ruling with a lasting idea lands in the skill it governs, on origin, before the sitting ends; what a seat did goes in the baton's LEDGER.
- **No subagents in the repos**; large outputs under `/tmp/s4e/<seat>/`, never under the root; a seat kills only pids it spawned, never `pkill -f`.
- **Tests**: a lander runs its program in both modes, `test_feature_reps.sh`, and the smokes; the suites are the coo's, asked for by `s4e_test_request.sh` (at most eight programs).
