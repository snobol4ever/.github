## 2. The bus: `bash harness/scripts/s4e_msg.sh <verb>` from the root

`next claim unclaim park done assign reown mint recut queue expire rule resume ask send check clear mailbox sweep board banner fleet whoami premise`. Run bus verbs one at a time with absolute paths; never pipe one into a short read; a send during a bus lock delivers nothing. Unread mail is announced once per arrival: the six-line heading names only new messages, then one line (`N unread`; after `check`, `N read, not cleared`) until the next arrival (step 37).

- **next** serves rows ASSIGNED to you, then your unfinished claim, then FREE rows by rank. A seat holds at most two live claims (CEO-2078); an ASSIGNED row not yet running does not count.
- **queue** `<topic> --tier fable|opus|sonnet|haiku | --to <seat>` (fleet-queue, 2026-11-05): moves a FREE or QUEUED row into a tier tube or a seat tube (its one job put, owner `@<tier>` or the seat, state `QUEUED:<tube>`); a sonnet or haiku tier needs a `SKILLS:` line in the baton head. This is how an officer or a company man feeds the tier below from the backlog.
- **mint** `<topic> [rank] [--owner <seat> | --tier <tier> | --to <seat>] --stdin` is the only way to add work: a GOAL with its own `DONE-WHEN:` and `PREMISE-WHEN:` lines. A topic needs a language prefix or `--owner`. Mint refuses a prose criterion.
- **done** `<topic>` runs the DONE-WHEN and refuses a red; the claim is untouched on a refusal. A criterion over `S4E_DONE_TIMEOUT` refuses rc 2 (could not measure). `S4E_DONE_OVERRIDE="why"` is loud and written into the claim.
- **park** `<topic> <STATE>`: `PARKED-AWAITING:<request-id>` (waiting on the coo's reading of the test request whose id `s4e_test_request.sh` printed; refused unless the request is pending or graded; when its verdict lands in `test_requests/done/` the row returns to the request's seat as `ASSIGNED:<seat>` with a ledger line and a telegram, by `next` or the sweep -- step 31) or `PARKED-AWAITING:<topic>` (waiting on another row, as `BLOCKED-ON`), `BLOCKED-ON:<topic>` (waiting on another row; `park` records a `RESTORE-STATE:` line on the baton, and when that row's `done` runs the blocked row returns to it -- ASSIGNED:<seat> for a parked claim or assignment, else FREE -- with a ledger line and a telegram, `DONE unblocked=N`; step 35), `PARKED-LON-HOLD:<why>`, `GRANT-NEEDED:<why>`; a row made moot by a landing is retired by its owner or a boss with `SUPERSEDED:<the landing that made it moot>` or `RETIRED:<why>` (the sweep skips both; the baton gets the STATE line). `MEASURING:<seat>:<pid>` (your own live measurement: accepted while the pid is alive and yours; the Stop gate passes the row while it lives and blocks the moment it ends; the sweep reverts a dead-pid row to ASSIGNED).
- **claim notices** (step 21): a claim tells only the seats whose live rows touch a path the claimed row touches, by prefix, from the batons' `TOUCHES:` lines (else the `Landing files:` words); a row with no `TOUCHES:` tells nobody and the claim says so. Put `TOUCHES: <path prefixes under the root>` on every live baton; a notice names the overlapping paths and needs no reply unless your measurement moves.
- **resume** `<topic> --tree <path> --command '<cmd>' --state <word> --log <path>` (step 16): one timestamped RESUME line under `## RESUME`, newest first, which `next` prints before anything but the MODE line; `resume <topic>` reads the newest. Write it before any stop.
- **the Stop gate** (step 10): a seat holding unread mail or a row ASSIGNED, CLAIMED or MEASURING-with-a-dead-pid cannot end its turn; its hook continues it with the row and its NEXT (12 continuations an hour). Park a row before waiting on it.
- **rule** `<GOAL-file> <ID> --to <seat,seat> --row <topic> --stdin` (headline line, then body; CTO-496 box-scheduler step 12): ONE act writes a ruling once -- the bullet at the head of the GOAL file's LIVE CURSOR, committed alone, rebased and pushed; a telegram to each named seat citing the hash; the row's baton LEDGER line. Every input is checked before the first write; an id already heading a bullet refuses; a failed push undoes the commit. `rule --probe` proves it on scratch.
- **recut** `<topic> --stdin` (box-scheduler 27 and its follow-up, worker_06 2026-11-05): the body carries `DONE-WHEN:`, `PREMISE-WHEN:` (either or both) and `WHY:`; each replaced line is kept directly ABOVE the new one on a `DONE-WHEN-WAS:` or `PREMISE-WHEN-WAS:` receipt with the seat, the time and the why; a retired row reopens FREE. **expire** `<topic>` (fleet-queue 5): the tick's verb for a claim whose reserved job is back in its tube.
- **send** `<seat> <topic> --stdin` with a quoted heredoc; it refuses a backtick or a command substitution. **check** displays the inbox; **clear** archives exactly what check displayed. An empty inbox is the acknowledgement.

## 3. The baton: `/home/resources/postoffice/tasks/<topic>.task.md`

Line 1 is `# TASK <topic>` and nothing else (state lives only in `QUEUE.tsv`: rank, topic, owner, state). Then `GOAL:`, `DONE-WHEN:` (a command, rc 0 green; `${S4E_HOME:?}` is its root), `PREMISE-WHEN:` (rc 0 while the problem exists), `LINKS:`, then `## NEXT` (exactly one, first, the β re-entry point; a replaced block is demoted to `## SUPERSEDED-NEXT`; NEXT stays under 8192 bytes, and the sweep moves every demoted block, a second NEXT and the whole of an over-cap NEXT to `tasks/archive/<topic>.superseded.md`, leaving the head and a pointer -- `util_baton_check.py --all`, step 15), `## QA`, `## LEDGER` (append-only, newest first, receipts). A task is a generator whose frame outlives its activation: α claim, β resume at NEXT, γ the computed DONE-WHEN, ω concede with the reason written.

## 4. The rules every row obeys

- **The provenance law**: every statement is MEASURED (the command beside it) or a HYPOTHESIS, marked. A number with no command is a hypothesis, whoever wrote it.
- **A row exists only while a measurement says so** (CEO-1386/553/555): a DONE-WHEN a deletion satisfies is false; a DONE-WHEN is proven red once and green once at mint (the mint receipt names both witnesses); a test that cannot measure refuses rc 2 (CEO-1680).
- **A row name states the symptom, never the hypothesis**; a lane is decided by the cure, never by a noun in the name.
- **The chat is not the record**: a ruling lands in its GOAL file's LIVE CURSOR on origin before the sitting ends; what a seat did goes in the baton's LEDGER.
- **No subagents in the repos**; large outputs under `/tmp/s4e/<seat>/`, never under the root; a seat kills only pids it spawned, never `pkill -f`.
- **Tests**: a lander runs its program in both modes, `test_feature_reps.sh`, and the smokes; the suites are the coo's, asked for by `s4e_test_request.sh` (at most eight programs).

## 5. Telegrams, SendMessage and the wake rule (CTO-469)

- A telegram (`send`) is the record and is read at the recipient's next sitting; it wakes nobody. A SendMessage is a pointer that starts a turn. `send ceo` is refused: that seat was fired 2026-10-31.
- To the cto: a ruling ask, a design gap, a grant. To the coo: a cross-seat fact, a claim collision, a test request, a red. Unsure: both.
- THE WAKE RULE (Lon 2026-11-04: "You ensure that the seat HAS WORK first, and then you wake it."): the coo, every tick, for each ROSTER seat with no live row and an idle session, finds its work (a red in its language, or an ASSIGNED or FREE row it can serve), mints what is not yet a row, assigns it, and only then sends the SendMessage naming the row. A seat with a RUNNING row and an idle session is sent its row. No wake list, no WAKE.txt. The cto wakes nobody.
- A seat that stops because it waits parks the wait first (`PARKED-AWAITING:` or `BLOCKED-ON:`), shows its banner and stops; a seat that finished its row hands off and stops, and the coo gives it work and wakes it.

## 6. Ending a sitting

The baton's NEXT rewritten, the LEDGER written, the seat's LIVE CURSOR moved, everything pushed (code repos first, `.github` last; a rejected push is logged with `s4e_push_rejected.sh`), and `bash harness/scripts/handoff_status.sh` pasted verbatim.

## 7. The sitting and the landing (moved here from CLAUDE.md on Lon's word, 2026-11-05: "STRIP it down to BARE BONES. No specific knowledge. All that goes in SKILLS.")

**Every sitting, in order.** (1) Sync the four repos (`for r in SCRIP corpus harness .github; do git -C $r fetch -q origin && git -C $r merge --ff-only origin/main; done`), then `cd SCRIP && make -j8` (in the background past two minutes; `FETCHED <key>` is the build cache); never pull half a cross-repo landing; time labels from `date`. (2) MODE: `head -2 /home/resources/postoffice/MODE` and its keyed lines (`ROSTER:` `LANES:` `ORDER-OF-WORK:` `BOSSES:` `FLEET:` `ON-CALL:`). (3) Your rows: `grep -P '\t<seat>\t|:<seat>$' /home/resources/postoffice/QUEUE.tsv`; each held row's baton (NEXT is where you resume); the LIVE CURSOR of your GOAL file. (4) Inbox: `s4e_msg.sh check` (long output into a file, read whole), act or reply, then `clear`; an empty inbox is the acknowledgement. (5) `s4e_msg.sh next` reserves your seat-tube job, then an ask or a job of your tier's tube, then (an officer or a company man) the tier below's; then it serves a legacy ASSIGNED row and your claim; a worker takes no FREE row, and when its tubes are empty it stops. Load the skills the gate names before the first edit; read the oracle's manual before ruling on a builtin or a syntax. (6) One row at a time by the landing recipe; a red in shared code goes by bus to its seat and is cured there, never patched around. (7) Hand off: baton NEXT rewritten, LEDGER written, LIVE CURSOR moved, everything pushed, `handoff_status.sh` pasted verbatim; waiting on the coo's reading: `park <topic> PARKED-AWAITING:<request-id>`; on another row: `BLOCKED-ON:<topic>`; on your own live measurement: `MEASURING:<seat>:<pid>`; then stop.

**The landing recipe.** (1) Reproduce: `monitor_run.sh <prog> --oracle` (the bug lies after the last agreed event); then an asm diff against a passing sibling in the same mode; gdb last. (2) The criterion first, a command that reads RED now: an assigned row carries its `DONE-WHEN:`; a row you open is `mint <topic> --owner <seat> --stdin` with `DONE-WHEN:` and `PREMISE-WHEN:` lines, each starting `cd "${S4E_HOME:?REFUSE(2): S4E_HOME unset}" &&`. (3) Cure in a clean `git worktree add` outside the root (a dirty-tree reading is unread; the worktree needs `harness` and `corpus` siblings); grammar edits through `regenerate_parser_and_lexer_from_sources.sh`; generated tables regenerated, never edited. (4) Prove: DONE-WHEN green; the program in both modes against the oracle; `test_feature_reps.sh <lang> <feature>`; `test_smoke_<lang>.sh`; `util_reflow_200.py --check` and `strip_comments.py --check` per C file; a cure that trades one program for another never lands. (5) Land: `s4e_land.sh --probe`, then `s4e_land.sh` with the row's DONE-WHEN as the gate (it fetches, rebases, regenerates, rebuilds, gates, pushes code repos first and `.github` last, verifies origin); by hand only when it refuses: `git add` named files, `git commit -F -` with a quoted heredoc as `LCherryholmes <lcherryh@yahoo.com>`, no trailers, `git pull --rebase`, re-prove, push code repos first, `.github` last; a rejected push goes to `s4e_push_rejected.sh`; rebuild after the commit. (6) Record: the baton's LEDGER and NEXT; `s4e_test_request.sh <suite-key> <entry>...` to the coo (eight programs at most); close by `claim` + `done` once the coo's reading is green.
