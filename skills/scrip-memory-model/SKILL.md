# SCRIP's memory model: the working knowledge of the stack, the frames, the collector and the boxes

The law is RULES.md (THE MEMORY MODEL, THE LIFETIME RULE, NO GLOBAL HOLDS A STACK, NO FRAME MARKERS, THE COLLECTOR GUESSES NOTHING) and the receipts are in GOAL-CEO.md; the design is the ARCH pages of § 4, the cto's custody. This skill is the map and the rulings a landing in this layer must not break. When a fact here and a page disagree, the page wins and this skill is corrected the same landing. The layer is one machine under seven syntaxes: SNOBOL4 patterns, Icon generators and Prolog backtracking are three syntaxes over one four-port box (α proceed, β recede, γ succeed, ω concede) wired at compile time into straight-line jumps.

## 1. The law of record and its oracles: the verifiers (CEO-1354, CEO-1368/1371, CEO-1542/1543, CEO-1759/1814/1841/1843, CEO-1895)

- **THE THREE ZETAS** (Lon s81: RSP for ζ-SPINE, RBP for ζ-ACTIVATION-FRAMES and the ζ-STANDING area): every box operand and local STARTS on the RSP spine as a FORTH stack of 16-byte DESCR cells with compile-time-constant offsets; it moves to the RBP activation frame only where Lon's two tests force it (an operand no consumer reaches at a fixed RSP offset, or a local whose lifetime outlives the spine between its γ and its β); the standing frame is the root graph's own named storage, never a surrogate global. **THE LIFETIME RULE** (CEO-1354): construct lifetime → stack; outlives its scope → heap; out of stack → a bigger declared `-s`, never data moved to the heap.
- **PLACEMENT IS DECIDED ONCE, IN THE PLANNER** (CEO-1895): `src/planner/` after lower and before the emitter; the emitter reads the plan table and decides nothing (`util_emitter_memory_model_census.py` bar A+B+D, the ratchet). **A RETRY NEVER RESTORES RSP**: no RSP reset or reload at any β (`test_gate_no_rsp_reset_at_beta.sh` blocks at 0); a γ pops only a fenced construct's cells (CEO-1843); the frame whack of a match end is a pop the plan row states (ARCH-ZETA-LAYOUT § 9). The edge laws: γ→α `X_i − gpop = E_j`; ω→β `X_i − K_i − wpop = X_j`; the discard law prices what a definite discard drops.
- **NO GLOBAL HOLDS A STACK** (CEO-1542/1543): the machine stack is the stack; the only stacks outside RSP/RBP are the two r12 islands (SNOBOL4's conditional-assignment stack, Prolog's trail and choice points). **NO FRAME MARKERS, NO SECOND STACK, THE MAPS STAY** (CEO-1368/1371/1492): one static frame map per activation frame, the DESCR type field the only tag, typed visitors, no conservative scan, no pinning; the allocator never collects, only emitted code at the return of an allocating runtime call does (ARCH-GC § 7 FROZEN, § 13 the chain over the maps is the collector's only walk).
- **THE ORACLES OF THIS LAYER ARE ITS VERIFIERS**, each red once before it is trusted (CEO-1680): `SCRIP_ZD_CHECK=1` (the plan's edge laws, 0 violations), `SCRIP_ZD_RTCHECK=1` (the emitted depth checks at every α and β, SIGILL on a miss), `SCRIP_GC_CHAIN_CHECK=1..3` (the chain walk's own checks), `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=1` (collect at every safe point) with the flip plant `SCRIP_GC_PLANT_FLIP=1` (a stale pointer becomes a located fault), `SCRIP_ZSM=1 SCRIP_ZSM_ALL=1 SCRIP_ZSM_DECL=1` (the frame census: outside, excess, graphs; the ratchet floors move only on the cto's ruling), `util_zeta_layout_check.py` (the layout record), `util_bb_placement_census.py --lang <l>` and `util_rbp_placement_census.py` (misplaced operands by kind), `util_gen_gc_allocating_table.py --check` and `util_gen_rtx_clobber_table.py` (the generated tables), the gates `test_gate_gc_*`, `test_gate_zd*`, `test_gate_planner*`, `test_gate_rtx_*`, `test_gate_rtcc_*`, `test_gate_pl_quad_regs.sh`. The language's oracle still grades the program: every witness runs in both modes against its ref, and under stress for a collector landing.

## 5. The suites and how a landing in this layer is graded

A change here is SHARED CODE: it is graded by every language's witnesses, not by a suite of its own. Per landing: the row's DONE-WHEN (a design step: `test_design_prolog_lifo_steps.sh --step N`, `test_design_icon_scanning_asm_steps.sh`, `test_design_builtin_table_steps.sh`, each over `corpus/tests/<lang>/<design>/STEPS.tsv` with refs cut from the oracle; a gate), `test_feature_reps.sh <lang> <feature>...` for the features the diff touches (at most eight core entries), and ALL EIGHT smokes `test_smoke_{snobol4,snocone,rebus,icon,prolog,pascal,raku,polyglot}.sh` before the push (CEO-1928); the collector's witnesses `harness/scripts/gc_bare_poll_witnesses.tsv` and the gc gates the diff's area owns under `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=1` in both modes; the emission identity over the masters is the coo's (`s4e_test_request.sh`, CEO-1992): EXPECTED DIFFERENT names the programs whose plan moved. One program is graded by hand with `corpus_suite_harness.py run ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.<ext> ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.ref --lang <lang> --modes m3,m4 --partition entry:<name>`, both modes mandatory. The heavy passes are the coo's: the blocking set, the boards, `make test-arena` (every gc gate and every smoke at a 1 MB arena), the `.s` regeneration. Measurements are instruction counts (`util_instr_count.sh`, `util_runtime_leaf_census.sh prog --mode m3|m4` by layer; callgrind counts `rep movsq` one Ir per quad); a clock only inside `util_quiet_slice.sh`; the unit is `x`, reference over ours.

## 6. Debugging a red in this layer

1. The MONITOR BRACKET first (`bash ../harness/scripts/monitor_run.sh <prog> --oracle`): the bug lies between the last event both agree on and the first they diverge on; `--modes` runs mode 3 against mode 4 in lock-step. Then ASM-DIFF-FIRST: the smallest repro beside a passing sibling, `--compile` both, diff within one mode; a two-tree asm diff (a worktree per commit, built in about 33 s) beats a bisect; annotate rsp moves by box.
2. The plan before the code: `--dump-plan` two-tree diff of PLAN_ROWs (E X K depth gpop wpop); `SCRIP_ZD_CHECK=1` for a law violation; `SCRIP_ZD_RTCHECK=1` traps at the first α or β entered at the wrong depth (the SIGILL names the box; read its `sub rsp` against its `lea r11, [rbp - N]` check); a conditional breakpoint on every gate β of the graph (`break n<serial>_gate_β if *(long*)$rsp != 152`) finds the first β entered off its cell.
3. A collector suspicion: `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=1` with the flip plant (filter the `[GC-` banner before diffing); a poll that DIFFs in every configuration names a reclaimed root, not a moved one; `ZGC-STALE` in an asm leaf after a C call is a clobbered register, not a root; `SCRIP_GC_CHAIN_CHECK=2`; dump the live-stack site pcs to locate a frameless chain stop; bound memory with a cgroup, not a timeout; a hang under stress is often slowness.
4. A template defect: the port tracer under gdb (`SCRIP_PL_TRACE=1`), a gdb Python stepper over a slab chain then a watchpoint on the bad word; sample a hot frame by interrupting the inferior by pid.
5. Only then gdb with a breakpoint and a hit count. Validate an instrument by running it; a null result bounds the probe; check a clean, consistently pulled tree before routing a red (the control worktree needs the corpus sibling; a `-dirty` reading is unread, CEO-2060).

## 7. Rulings a landing in this layer must not break (receipts in GOAL-CEO.md, GOAL-HQ-ZETAS.md, GOAL-HQ-COLLECTOR.md, GOAL-HQ-TEMPLATES.md, GOAL-HQ-RUNTIME.md and the pages of § 4)

- **The planner decides, the emitter reads** (CEO-1895): no placement decision, no `getenv` switch and no language test in the emitter or a template (`test_gate_emit_no_lang.sh`, `test_gate_planner_six_passes_read_no_emitter_state.sh`); after lower every variance is an IR fact (CEO-1838/1840); a deletion carries its re-expression in the same landing.
- **ZETA HAS NO MODES**: no selector, switch or flag chooses a zeta's behaviour; a mode is deleted machinery, not a default (the `--zeta-storage` selectors are retired). Modes 3 and 4 may diverge as optimization choices, each graded against the oracle on its own.
- **A retry never restores RSP; a γ pops only a fenced construct's cells; a ball never re-enters a retained frame** (CEO-1759/1814/1841/1843); a new IR op needs the flat walk; a graph-level carve is declared to the plan depth or the chain lands on it; a box on a failure edge alone can wall a run (ω heads need test-kind ops).
- **The maps stay and the collector guesses nothing** (CEO-1368/1371, ARCH-GC § 7 FROZEN): never a marker scan, a side-car ledger, a tagged-cell widening of raw words, a conservative visit, a pin, or a deleted frame map; the allocator never collects; a leaf that can allocate returns to a safe point and tags its callee-saved pushes; the poll is a register-preserving asm leaf; the allocating table follows the runtime binary and is regenerated after every runtime landing.
- **The lifetime rule and the declared sizes** (CEO-1354/1353/1281): out of stack is a bigger declared `-s`, never data moved to the heap; a tiny arena refusing a live set is a row, never a reason to raise a default; `SCRIP_HEAP_KB` sets the window, never a program's size.
- **No global holds a stack; no new global without Lon's in-chat grant that session** (CEO-1542/1543, the banner ask); a word of `rtccb` used as a mailbox is a global by another name; `g_runtime` is the one runtime state struct.
- **Generated tables are regenerated, never merged or edited** (CEO-1528): `gc_allocating_table.inc`, the rtx clobber table, `box_cell_shapes.inc` (fresh negative cell-shape codes collide across seats at a merge: regenerate); the bare-poll witness table keys by file and callee and is re-keyed last.
- **Every instruction goes through `x86(...)`, BOTH-MEDIUM MANDATORY**: no raw bytes in a template, no `MEDIUM_*` test in a `bb_*.cpp`; one form per box (a template that branches on sub-kind or neighbour is split in LOWER); a text marker never adds a line (gates read fixed line windows); a truncated separator line is an open comment that swallows the next function.
- **No C function enters a box; logic lives in emitted boxes; the leaf is asm on the rtcc ABI with no veneer** (CEO-1576, CEO-985, CEO-2045, Lon 2026-09-21/23): the C is deleted in the same landing, never wrapped; deciders are deleted, not wrapped in accessor layers; a recording poll form clobbers caller-saved registers and a tail never re-stores registers after an arm polled.
- **Large chunks** (CEO-1478..1484): a scheme change rolls out once small with a smoke, then everywhere in the lane; the core step of a redesign is never staged behind feature landings; fetch origin before a planner cure (the cfo cures the same classes); never widen a Lon ruling (he said no markers: the stack stays mapped).
- Hand-written `.c/.h/.inc` under `src/` on the 200-column rule with zero comments and zero blank lines (`util_reflow_200.py --check`, `strip_comments.py --check`); no `-O2` builds (`RT_OPT` is `-O0`); no fixed limits (`audit_fixed_caps_census.py` is the ratchet); a new gate is adopted into the wiring floor; the ZSM frame census floors move only on the cto's ruling.
