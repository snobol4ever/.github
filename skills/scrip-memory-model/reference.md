## 4. How a template addresses a cell today

The forms (in `src/templates/x86/x86_zeta.cpp` and the family headers) read the node's plan row through the `_` operand plan the emitter fills: `ZRES(w)` and `ZRESD(w)` (the box's own result: its rbp home `op_xf_off` when it has one, else the spine), `ZOPQ(k, w)`, `ZOPD`, `ZOPQS` (operand k's cell through `op_zread`), `ZLOC`, `ZTOS`, `x86_zref(distance)` (a raw spine distance), `x86_zop` (a layout key; refuses an unbacked key with `zls_key_unbacked`), `FRQ`, `FR`, `FRQB`, `RDQ("rbp", off)`, `RDD`, `XSAQ` (frame forms), `x86_rsp_load64` and `x86_rsp_store64` (the call block's staging). `ZCELL(w)` survives only as the one-line spine helper inside `x86_zeta.cpp`; the general accessor of that name was withdrawn (Lon 2026-10-24). The carve helpers are `x86_alpha_carve`, `x86_gamma_free` and `x86_gamma_result`. `util_zeta_accessor_census.py` counts the forms. A template forms an rsp or rbp address only through these.

## 5. DESIGN: zeta-layout, the plan row as the one source of truth (CEO-2019)

The record is the planner's row, written once by the passes and read as struct fields by four readers: the emitter, the collector's record (sites, frame-map quads, spine word-kind masks), the verifiers (ZSM, ZD-CHECK), the instruments (`--dump-plan`, `util_zeta_layout_check.py`). The laws:
- **R1 the flat cell rule**: rsp is constant inside a box; a value kept across a runtime call is a declared word of the box's cell; argument blocks are cell words. The ZSM's own balanced push/pop around `rt_zdp_ev` is the one exemption.
- **R2 the site record is the row's**: `emit_gc_site_play` stamps the site's depth from the row; the text-site rescan (`x86_rsp_st_t`, `x86_text_sites_scan`, the `#@rsp` notes) is deleted; rule 5 ceases.
- **R3 the spine mask**: the word-kind column of `box_cell_shapes.tsv`, the planner's fact column, the site's third quad; the walk visits exactly the declared words; the mask is anchored at the run base, bit w the word at R + 8A - 8(w + 1) with A the site's fourth quad (CTO-509), NW = E/8, every mask of one growing frame a prefix of one block (CTO-504).
- **R4 the runtime entry table**: every entry emitted code calls is a row of `/home/harness/scripts/rt_entries.tsv` (generated into `src/runtime/rt_entry_table.inc` by `util_gen_rt_entry_table.py`, gated by `test_gate_rt_entry_table_matches_the_binary.sh`) with `sym conv rc allocates clobbers transfers`, declared at the definition (`RT_ENTRY_DECL` for C, `RTX_FUNC`/`RTX_ENTRY` for asm); `allocates` is derived from the call graph and checked against the binary (step 5c deletes the separate allocating table).
- **R5 one call, one poll**: `x86_call_rt(sym)` reads the row and emits the call, the poll when it allocates, and the clobber re-establishment; `x86_gc_poll()` the one poll shape (neither exists yet: step 6).
- **R6 the verifier reads the row**: `x86_rsp_slide_known()` is deleted (done); `util_zsm_declared_census.sh --lang L` reads 0 mismatches in both modes for every language.
- **R7 the rbp zeta and the standing cells**: every frame-map quad (`GC_LAY_Q`) comes from the planner; every frame reference lands in a granted cell (`test_gate_every_frame_reference_lands_in_a_granted_cell.sh` at 0); the standing root cells are rows of the root graph's plan.
- **R8 no C road writes the depth**: a C raise returns `FAILDESCR` with the error armed in the context record and the box's ω unwinds through the declared discards; `rt_return_to_emitted` and the catchers' longjmps are deleted (step 9; skill `runtime-calls` carries the protocol).
- **R9 the transfer law**: a transfer pops `X_i - E_t` in its own column (`tpop`), `E_t` the statement base for a SNOBOL4 statement entry or a run-time chunk.
- **The frame whack is a stated pop** (2026-10-31): MATCH_END's `mov rsp, rbp; pop rbp` is the fenced γ pop, stated in its row's `gpop` as the net carve from the MATCH_BEGIN row (whose K carries its frame cells); the checker prices the pair from the row and never reads a box's asm for a number the plan owns.

The step table (executor in brackets): 0 the baseline [cto], 1 the law and the checker [hq_templates], 2a-2e the flat cell rule per language [cfo for SNOBOL4 and shared, hq_icon, hq_prolog, hq_pascal, hq_raku], 3 the site record from the row [cfo], 4a, 4a-2 the spine mask [hq_zetas], 4b the walk reads the mask [hq_collector], 5 the runtime entry table [hq_templates], 6 one call one poll [cfo], 7a, 7b the ZSM expectation from the row [hq_icon, hq_zetas], 8 every frame-map quad from the planner [hq_zetas], 9 no C road writes the depth [hq_runtime], 10 the finish [hq_templates].

## 6. The planner's passes (the order the plan is computed in)

(i) definiteness: per-graph RETAINS as a fixpoint over the call graph (a graph retains when it holds SUSPEND, a co-expression kind, CALL_VALUE, CALL_GEN, PROC_GEN, more than one SUCCEED, a resumable DISJUNCTION, a TO, a holding GATE, a MATCH_DEFER, is redefinable, or calls a graph that retains), then per box, indefiniteness flowing along γ edges and regained at an UNMARK whose BOUND is definite, never along an ω edge; (ii) homes: STANDING, ACT (resident, with bytes, or an rbp alias of a definite spine cell, no bytes), SPINE; (iii) carves: the kind's words from the cell-shape table plus 16 per spine local homed at the box plus the construct extras; (iv) depths by the edge laws, `gpop`/`wpop` by the gamma law, `restore` by the discard law; (v) layout per construct (header words, resident cells 16-aligned, `R`, the map quads); (vi) site rules, then `zd_check`. State words (CEO-1885) are derived per kind from the templates' own reads by `util_template_state_words.py` into `state_word_kinds.tsv`: an address that escapes to a runtime leaf while a retaining child runs, a word read at β, γ or ω where the box is not definite, a graph's own continuation words; nothing else is a state word.

## 7. How a landing in this layer is graded

A change here is SHARED CODE: graded by every language's witnesses. Per landing: the row's DONE-WHEN (a design step `test_design_<name>_steps.sh --step N`, or a gate); `test_feature_reps.sh <lang> <feature>` for the features the diff touches; ALL EIGHT smokes `test_smoke_{snobol4,snocone,rebus,icon,prolog,pascal,raku,polyglot}.sh` before the push (CEO-1928); the collector's witnesses under `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=1` in both modes for a change the collector can see (skill `gc-collector`); the emission identity over the masters is the coo's (CEO-1992), asked for by test request. One program by hand: `corpus_suite_harness.py run ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.<ext> ../corpus/tests/<lang>/ALL.ref --lang <lang> --modes m3,m4 --partition entry:<name>`. The heavy passes (`make test`, `make test-arena`, the boards, `util_spine_population.sh`) are the coo's. Measure by instruction count (`util_instr_count.sh`); a clock only inside `util_quiet_slice.sh`.

## 8. Debugging a red in this layer

1. The MONITOR BRACKET first (`bash ../harness/scripts/monitor_run.sh <prog> --oracle`): the bug lies between the last event both agree on and the first they diverge on; `--modes` runs mode 3 against mode 4 in lock-step. Then ASM-DIFF-FIRST: the smallest repro beside a passing sibling, `--compile` both, diff within one mode; a two-tree asm diff (a worktree per commit, built in about 33 s) beats a bisect; annotate rsp moves by box.
2. The plan before the code: `--dump-plan` two-tree diff of PLAN_ROWs (E X K depth gpop wpop); `SCRIP_ZD_CHECK=1` for a law violation; `SCRIP_ZD_RTCHECK=1` traps at the first α or β entered at the wrong depth (the SIGILL names the box; read its `sub rsp` against its `lea r11, [rbp - N]` check); a conditional breakpoint on every gate β of the graph (`break n<serial>_gate_β if *(long*)$rsp != 152`) finds the first β entered off its cell.
3. A collector suspicion: `SCRIP_GC_STRESS=1` with the flip plant (filter the `[GC-` banner before diffing); a poll that DIFFs in every configuration names a reclaimed root, not a moved one; `ZGC-STALE` in an asm leaf after a C call is a clobbered register, not a root; `SCRIP_GC_CHAIN_CHECK=2`; dump the live-stack site pcs to locate a frameless chain stop; bound memory with a cgroup, not a timeout; a hang under stress is often slowness.
4. A template defect: the port tracer under gdb (`SCRIP_PL_TRACE=1`), a gdb Python stepper over a slab chain then a watchpoint on the bad word; sample a hot frame by interrupting the inferior by pid.
5. Only then gdb with a breakpoint and a hit count. Validate an instrument by running it; a null result bounds the probe; check a clean, consistently pulled tree before routing a red (the control worktree needs the corpus sibling; a `-dirty` reading is unread, CEO-2060).

## 9. Rulings a landing in this layer must not break (receipts in GOAL-CEO.md, GOAL-HQ-ZETAS.md, GOAL-HQ-COLLECTOR.md, GOAL-HQ-TEMPLATES.md, GOAL-HQ-RUNTIME.md)

- **The planner decides, the emitter reads** (CEO-1895): no placement decision, no `getenv` switch and no language test in the emitter or a template (`test_gate_emit_no_lang.sh`, `test_gate_planner_six_passes_read_no_emitter_state.sh`); after lower every variance is an IR fact (CEO-1838/1840); a deletion carries its re-expression in the same landing.
- **ZETA HAS NO MODES**: no selector, switch or flag chooses a zeta's behaviour; a mode is deleted machinery, not a default (the `--zeta-storage` selectors are retired). Modes 3 and 4 may diverge as optimization choices, each graded against the oracle on its own.
- **A retry never restores RSP; a γ pops only a fenced construct's cells; a ball never re-enters a retained frame** (CEO-1759/1814/1841/1843); a new IR op needs the flat walk; a graph-level carve is declared to the plan depth or the chain lands on it; a box on a failure edge alone can wall a run (ω heads need test-kind ops).
- **The maps stay and the collector guesses nothing** (CEO-1368/1371, ARCH-GC § 7 FROZEN): never a marker scan, a side-car ledger, a tagged-cell widening of raw words, a conservative visit, a pin, or a deleted frame map; the allocator never collects; a leaf that can allocate returns to a safe point and tags its callee-saved pushes; the poll is a register-preserving asm leaf; the allocating table follows the runtime binary and is regenerated after every runtime landing.
- **The lifetime rule and the declared sizes** (CEO-1354/1353/1281): out of stack is a bigger declared `-s`, never data moved to the heap; a tiny arena refusing a live set is a row, never a reason to raise a default; `SCRIP_HEAP_KB` sets the window, never a program's size.
- **No global holds a stack; no new global without Lon's in-chat grant that session** (CEO-1542/1543, the banner ask); a word of `rtccb` used as a mailbox is a global by another name; `g_runtime` is the one runtime state struct.
- **Generated tables are regenerated, never merged or edited** (CEO-1528): `gc_allocating_table.inc`, `rt_entry_table.inc` (from `/home/harness/scripts/rt_entries.tsv`), `box_cell_shapes.inc` (fresh negative cell-shape codes collide across seats at a merge: regenerate); the bare-poll witness table keys by file and callee and is re-keyed last.
- **Every instruction goes through `x86(...)`, BOTH-MEDIUM MANDATORY**: no raw bytes in a template, no `MEDIUM_*` test in a `bb_*.cpp`; one form per box (a template that branches on sub-kind or neighbour is split in LOWER); a text marker never adds a line (gates read fixed line windows); a truncated separator line is an open comment that swallows the next function.
- **No C function enters a box; logic lives in emitted boxes; the leaf is asm on the rtcc ABI with no veneer** (CEO-1576, CEO-985, CEO-2045, Lon 2026-09-21/23): the C is deleted in the same landing, never wrapped; deciders are deleted, not wrapped in accessor layers; a recording poll form clobbers caller-saved registers and a tail never re-stores registers after an arm polled.
- **Large chunks** (CEO-1478..1484): a scheme change rolls out once small with a smoke, then everywhere in the lane; the core step of a redesign is never staged behind feature landings; fetch origin before a planner cure (the cfo cures the same classes); never widen a Lon ruling (he said no markers: the stack stays mapped).
- Hand-written `.c/.h/.inc` under `src/` on the 200-column rule with zero comments and zero blank lines (`util_reflow_200.py --check`, `strip_comments.py --check`); no `-O2` builds (`RT_OPT` is `-O0`); no fixed limits (`audit_fixed_caps_census.py` is the ratchet); a new gate is adopted into the wiring floor; the ZSM frame census floors move only on the cto's ruling.
