Forbidden: a move that also changes text; a new umbrella; a large step outside a quiet window or without a last-minute rebase.

## 3. Facts for a header landing

- A data `.inc` (an initializer, a table, an embedded source) is spliced text, not declarations a unit names; the unused-include arm leaves it alone.
- A header-split fixer adds includes to units only; a C-linkage conflict names an unwrapped C header or a tail outside its guard; `bb_templates.h` prototypes are C++ linkage, so a definition must not add `extern "C"`.
- A passenger split exposes carried declarations: run the implicit-declaration pass over the rewritten units (`-Werror=implicit-function-declaration`), since the build's `-w` hides an unprototyped call (the five-units row, `scrip-five-units-call-a-function-no-included-header-declares-...`, FREE).
- A header move has readers in the harness: grep it for the old file name before landing.

**What is measured.** Every root and worktree builds through one ccache (the base is each tree's root since 2026-11-11, `test_gate_ccache_shares_every_tree.sh`), so a unit whose preprocessed text a seat has not changed is a hit for every other seat, and the box pays only for the units a commit changes. The price of a commit is the compile instructions of the units it forces: its touched units, plus every unit that reaches a touched header. Instruments: `harness/scripts/util_unit_cost_census.sh` (per unit, `perf stat -e instructions:u` over the Makefile's own compile line, ccache bypassed, in the batch class), `harness/scripts/util_cache_blast.py --cost TSV` (each commit since a date priced on today's dependency graph, `gcc -MM` per unit), and `harness/scripts/util_unit_spans.py SRC` (every top-level function of a unit, its exact first and last line and the same-file names it references, from clang's AST with the unit's build flags).

**The laws of the cut tree (C1-C8).**
- **C1 data is not code**: a generated or embedded table is a unit of its own (`<table>.c` defining `const` data, its header declaring it `extern`), never spliced into a code unit; its generator writes both. A regeneration then recompiles the table alone.
- **C2 a hot unit is cut to the floor** ("into smithereens if necessary"): a unit edited by more than 20 commits in 30 days, or forcing more than 300 G in them, is cut until each piece's own code costs no more than about twice its header floor (300 to 800 lines of C, by measurement, not by a line count); a function longer than a piece stays whole as a piece of its own, and its own cure is the plan that owns it (the dispatch chains of `by_name_dispatch.c` are DESIGN-PLAN builtin-table step 2's).
- **C3 a piece is a cluster**: the functions of a piece share a language, a family name and, first, the call graph and the history (they are edited by the same commits, `git log -U0` hunks attributed by function); a cut that exports more statics than it keeps private is a bad cut and is re-clustered.
- **C4 each piece exports through its own header** (R1, R2): the statics another piece calls become extern in `<piece>.h` beside it; a consumer includes the headers of the pieces it calls and no other; there is never a shared private `<file>_int.h` that every piece includes (an umbrella under R4, and an edit of it recompiles every piece).
- **C5 a hot header is cut the same way**: a header edited by more than 10 commits in 30 days and reaching more than 20 units is cut so a declaration reaches only its users (R4); the stage-state headers (`stage2.h`, `emit_state.h`, the `g_runtime_*.h` family embedded by value in `g_runtime`) are the dearest, and moving a sub-record out of `g_runtime` by value is a runtime-state change that waits on Lon's grant (skill `scrip-c-code`, no new global without it).
- **C6 the tree reads by phase, then language, then cluster**: a cut unit becomes a directory (`runtime/by_name_dispatch.c` becomes `runtime/byname/<lang>/<cluster>.c` plus `runtime/byname/dispatch.c`); headers sit beside the unit they declare; a move is proved by R5 (stripped objects identical) and changes only paths.
- **C7 cold units are joined** where joining lowers the full-build price without raising the blast: units under the floor that the history edits together, read by `util_cache_blast.py` and the full-build line of the census.
- **C8 a split is mechanical, replayable and repoints its readers**: a cut is a manifest (function name to piece, `harness/designs/cache_tree/manifests/<unit>.tsv`) applied by `util_split_unit.py` (step 1) on origin's HEAD at the moment of landing, so a rebase conflict is cured by re-running the manifest, never by hand; `--replay DIFF` maps a seat's in-flight diff of the old unit onto the pieces by function name; the proof is function identity (every function's bytes and the symbols its relocations name, over the union of objects, against the parent: `util_functions_identical.py`, step 1), then the smokes. Every harness file that names the old path is repointed in the same landing (162 harness files name `by_name_dispatch.c`, 117 `emit.cpp`, 113 `gc_heap.c`, 91 `core.c`, MEASURED by `grep -rlF`); the reader ratchet (step 2) reds on a new harness reference to a source path that does not exist (134 such references stand today, MEASURED).
